# The Borderland — where the Keep sits, and why

*The placement that makes Khaeros stop being a coincidence and start being evidence.*

> **The map pin, one line:** the Keep stands on the **western frontier — the Cruth Lowlands / Radlebb borderland between lawful Karameikos and the Black Eagle Barony** — guarding a road that is *also* the Baron's overland smuggling route. The party thinks they took a frontier-policing job. They walked onto the far end of von Hendriks's pipeline.

B2 specifies no world and no location. Mystara's Grand Duchy of Karameikos is the chosen setting; **this page fixes the *where* within it,** and why that single choice makes the whole campaign lock together.

---

## Why here (what the placement buys)

- **"Borderland" is doubly true.** It's literal wilderness frontier — the wild country B2 wants — *and* the political seam where the Duke's law fades and the Baron's shadow begins. The castellan is holding a line in **two directions at once**, against humanoid raids and (without anyone saying so) against the Baron's encroachment.
- **The Iron Ring cell has a reason to exist here.** The cult/slaving operation isn't random evil parked in the wild — it's a **node on the Baron's overland corridor.** The bugbear slave-station feeds captives down the pipeline to Fort Doom and out by sea; the cult's famine-raids destabilize exactly the stretch of road the Baron wants weak and lawless. **The Caves aren't near the supply line by accident — they're on it.**
- **Khaeros is explained to the inch.** He escaped a Baron's shipment *in this region* and went to ground in *these caves.* When the party finds the manifest, the iron-ring sigil, the evidence, it doesn't point off to an abstraction — it points **down the road they came in on,** toward the hidden cove and Fort Doom. The minotaur in the back cave is a living piece of the Baron's own operation, sitting ~40 miles from the man who ordered him.
- **The Duke's denial is baked into the commission.** Stefan sends the party to a frontier *he knows borders the Baron* and chooses not to think about what that proximity means. He frames raids on a road along his most dangerous vassal's border as a simple monster problem. The campaign's keystone — *the Duke won't look at von Hendriks* — is built into the geography of the job itself.

---

## The region (gazetteer)

- **The Black Eagle Barony** (Barony of Halag), seat at **Fort Doom**, on the **Gulf of Halag** — southwest Karameikos, the realm's worst-governed corner, effectively Baron **Ludwig von Hendriks's** private fief. He controls the witnesses on his own coast.
- **The Cruth Lowlands / Radlebb Woods** — the wild hill-and-forest frontier abutting the Barony; the most lawless stretch of the Duchy. The Keep and the Caves sit in this broken country.
- **Luln** — a battered frontier town that canonically holds out *against* the Baron and the monsters both. The natural named anchor (see below).
- **Thyatis** — the empire to the **east**, reached overland across the Altan Tepes or **by sea** around the southern coast. The sea route is the whole reason Khaeros's capture stayed quiet.

---

## The smuggling corridor (the conspiracy's physical trail)

Why the route is maritime, built from the smuggler's problem backward: von Hendriks wants a minotaur out of Thyatis and into his hands *without Karameikos knowing.* Overland from Thyatis means dragging a minotaur the length of the settled Duchy past Kelvin and Specularum — impossible to keep quiet. **By sea** skips every layer of ducal oversight and lands the cargo on the one shore where the Duke's writ doesn't run.

The corridor, point by point — each a place evidence can surface, pointing *back down the road toward the Baron:*

1. **Thyatis** — the minotaur acquired (captured war-thrall / arena stock). *Seeds his chain mail and training: made a fighter before he was made cargo.*
2. **By ship**, west along the southern coast, **avoiding Specularum** and all lawful traffic.
3. **A hidden cove** short of Fort Doom — Iron Ring business wants no eyes, not even the Baron's own townsfolk.
4. **Overland, inland and north** through the Cruth Lowlands — *and here it breaks:* Khaeros escapes his handlers and goes to ground in the **Caves of Chaos.**

The corridor is the **physical spine of the [Iron Ring evidence trail](the-iron-ring.md)**: a slave manifest, eagle-marked coin, iron-ring fittings, a courier's route — assembled, they trace this road from the caves down to the cove and Fort Doom. Reveal it piece by piece and the party literally maps the Baron's operation by walking it backward.

---

## On naming the anchor

- **Lean on Luln** as the named anchor rather than inventing a town: canonically the right flavor — a frontier settlement resisting the Baron and the wilderness both, a place that knows the border is dangerous in *two* directions. Position the Keep as guarding the road between Luln and the interior, or as a newer ducal outpost pushing the writ further into contested ground — giving the castellan reason to welcome competent help and reason to be nervous about who's watching.
- **Or keep the Keep unnamed/fresh** so you aren't bound to published Luln details. Either works — *the corridor is what matters, not the name.* Keep it in the Cruth Lowlands / Radlebb frontier, on the Barony's edge.

---

## Confidence flags (verify before you pin coordinates)

- **High confidence:** Black Eagle Barony = southwest Karameikos, coastal, seat at Fort Doom, ruled by von Hendriks; the western frontier near it is the wildest, most lawless part of the Duchy; Luln is a frontier town there resisting the Baron; sea access to Thyatis exists around the southern coast.
- **Verify against GAZ1:** exact distances, precise hex placement, coastline/cove specifics, the road network. Check the Karameikos map before committing coordinates.
- **Pure invention (yours to set):** the hidden cove, the corridor's exact path, and the Keep's specific spot.
- **Divergence note:** there is *no* canonical location for the Keep. The common **fan** default places it in the **east** (near Dymrak / the Altan Tepes). This SW placement is a deliberate, campaign-serving choice — own it as a choice.

---

## What it buys session one

When the Duke describes "raids on travelers near a keep on the borderlands," he is describing — without knowing it — **disruption on the Black Eagle's smuggling road.** Everything the party needs to eventually bring down von Hendriks is sitting in that ravine on day one. They just can't read it yet.

The whole campaign folded into one map pin: **the Keep, on the Cruth Lowlands frontier, guarding a road that is also a smuggling route, on the border of the man the Duke won't look at.**
