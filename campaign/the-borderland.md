# The Borderland — where the Keep sits, and why

*The placement that makes Khaeros stop being a coincidence and start being evidence.*

> **The map pin, one line:** the Keep is **Riverfork Keep**, an isolated western fortress in the river country north of Blight Swamp. The B2 wilderness map is its actual hinterland: the Keep in the southwest, the broad river and lizardfolk fens to the south, forest encounters across the interior, and the Caves of Chaos to the northeast. Its garrison thinks it is policing an ordinary humanoid-plagued frontier. It has not recognized the Black Eagle traffic moving through the swamp and waterways around it.

B2 specifies no world and no location. Mystara's Grand Duchy of Karameikos is the chosen setting, and the B2 fortress is **Riverfork Keep** for this campaign. The published B2 maps supply both its playable interior and its immediate wilderness; Karameikos supplies the larger geography and political context.

---

## Why here (what the placement buys)

- **The isolation is real.** Riverfork is farther from the Duchy's settled center than Radlebb, with river, forest, fen, and broken upland separating it from easy reinforcement. Its large garrison, internal market, bank, chapel, lodging, and warehouses exist because travelers and patrols cannot casually rely on the next town.
- **The B2 wilderness map becomes literal geography.** The river is not an invented tributary. The lizardfolk mound lies in the printed fens south of it; the spider lair, raider camp, Mad Hermit, Cave of the Unknown, and Caves of Chaos occupy their published positions around Riverfork.
- **The Iron Ring cell has a reason to exist here.** The bugbear slave-station is a northern intake point on a covert Black Eagle route through Blight Swamp and the western waterways. Barony representatives can bring bulky trade goods to the bugbears and carry captives away without using the maintained roads watched by ducal patrols. The cult's famine keeps the surrounding country violent and discourages close investigation.
- **Khaeros belongs to the same route.** He was landed through the Barony's coastal network and moved north as valuable cargo for an Iron Ring buyer or holding site. He escaped near Riverfork and went to ground in the Caves. His presence is not a geographic coincidence; he is living evidence of the route.
- **The Duchy's misdiagnosis is baked into the commission.** Stefan and Riverfork's officers have reports of road attacks, missing travelers, humanoid activity, Eagles in circulation, and occasional suspicious river traffic. Those facts look like the ordinary noise of a dangerous frontier because no one has assembled them. The party is creating the first case strong enough to make the government look deliberately.

## The region (gazetteer)

- **The Black Eagle Barony** (Barony of Halag), seat at **Fort Doom**, on the **Gulf of Halag** — southwest Karameikos, the realm's worst-governed corner, effectively Baron **Ludwig von Hendriks's** private fief. He controls the witnesses on his own coast.
- **Riverfork Keep** — an isolated ducal fortress in the far west, standing in river-fork country north of Blight Swamp. Its normal mandate is holding the western approaches, keeping its roads and waterways passable, and responding to humanoid raids.
- **The Cruth Lowlands / Achelos Woods** — wild hill, river, and forest country around the Keep. The Caves sit northeast of Riverfork in this difficult terrain.
- **Blight Swamp** — the broad wetland south of Riverfork. Its northern fens are the home of [Sessrik's lizardfolk](the-lizardfolk-of-the-marsh.md) and provide cover for traffic that wants to avoid roads.
- **Radlebb Keep** — the more southerly ducal fortress on the Westron Road. It is not the B2 Keep; the party's evidence will help cause its later, explicit focus on Black Eagle activity.
- **Luln** — a battered frontier town that canonically holds out *against* the Baron and the monsters both. The natural named anchor (see below).
- **Thyatis** — the empire to the **east**, reached overland across the Altan Tepes or **by sea** around the southern coast. The sea route is the whole reason Khaeros's capture stayed quiet.

## The smuggling corridor (the conspiracy's physical trail)

Why the route begins by sea, built from the smuggler's problem backward: the Iron Ring wants a minotaur out of Thyatis and into its western network *without Karameikos knowing.* Overland from Thyatis means dragging him the length of the settled Duchy past Kelvin and Specularum—impossible to keep quiet. **By sea** skips every layer of ducal oversight and lands the cargo on the one shore where the Duke's writ does not run. From there, swamp paths, minor waterways, and guarded overland stages move selected captives north toward buyers and holding sites.

The corridor, point by point — each a place evidence can surface, pointing *back down the road toward the Baron:*

1. **Thyatis** — the minotaur acquired (captured war-thrall / arena stock). *Seeds his chain mail and training: made a fighter before he was made cargo.*
2. **By ship**, west along the southern coast, **avoiding Specularum** and all lawful traffic.
3. **A hidden cove** on the Barony's coast—Iron Ring business wants no eyes, not even the Baron's own townsfolk.
4. **Through the Barony's northern route**, using guarded tracks, swamp margins, and waterways toward the Cruth Lowlands and Riverfork country.
5. **Near the Caves, the route breaks:** Khaeros escapes his handlers, flees into the nearest defensible wilderness, and goes to ground in the **Caves of Chaos.**

The corridor is the **physical spine of the [Iron Ring evidence trail](the-iron-ring.md)**: a slave manifest, Eagle scrip, iron-ring fittings, boat or cart tallies, and a courier's route. Assembled, they trace the network from the Caves through the fens and western lowlands toward Fort Doom. Reveal it piece by piece and the party maps the Baron's operation backward.

## The local pin

- **The B2 Keep is Riverfork Keep.** Use the marked fortress near the western river forks on the Karameikos map.
- **Use the B2 area map without relocating its features.** Riverfork Keep is the southwest fortress; the river and fens occupy the south; the lizardfolk are at area 1; the spiders at 2; Aldric's raiders at 3; the Mad Hermit at 4; the Cave of the Unknown lies near the center; and the Caves of Chaos rise in the northeast.
- **The fen is the northern reach of Blight Swamp.** Niko Voss fishes the accessible pools near the Keep. Maren crosses into the deeper reeds while searching for him and encounters the lizardfolk at their territorial boundary.
- **The covert route does not pass through Riverfork's gate.** It threads along the swamp edge and waterways south of the road, then turns northeast through forest tracks to the bugbear station. Riverfork patrols see fragments without seeing the route.

---

## Setting boundaries

- **High confidence:** Black Eagle Barony = southwest Karameikos, coastal, seat at Fort Doom, ruled by von Hendriks; the western frontier near it is the wildest, most lawless part of the Duchy; Luln is a frontier town there resisting the Baron; sea access to Thyatis exists around the southern coast.
- **Campaign decision:** B2's unnamed fortress is identified with Riverfork Keep. Its floor plan, Valerian, garrison, chapel, civilian services, and wilderness map are the campaign's treatment of that site.
- **Pure invention:** the Caves' precise hex, the hidden cove, and the corridor's exact path.
- **Timeline decision:** Riverfork has not identified the Baron as the organizing intelligence behind its frontier incidents. The party's discoveries begin sustained ducal scrutiny and help produce Radlebb Keep's later anti-Baron mandate.

---

## What it buys session one

When the Duke describes "raids on travelers near a keep on the borderlands," he is describing—without knowing it—**disruption around the Black Eagle's smuggling road.** Everything needed to justify watching von Hendriks is sitting in that ravine on day one. Nobody has connected it yet.

The whole campaign folded into one map pin: **isolated Riverfork Keep watches roads while the Baron's traffic moves through the river and fen below it; the party discovers the hidden route and gives the Duchy its first reason to watch him as an organized threat.**
