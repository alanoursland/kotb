## Dwarven abilities and field skills

- **Infravision:** 60 feet in darkness.
- **Stonework:** A 1–2 on 1d6 notices new construction, shifting walls, unsafe stone, slopes, or stonework traps when Brynja examines the area.
- **Metal appraisal:** Given one turn and simple tools, she identifies common metals, plating, poor alloys, and fraudulent weight without a roll. Merely handling a gold-washed Eagle gives her a 1–2 on 1d6 chance to notice that something is wrong immediately.
- **Engineering:** She can estimate the purpose and labor behind tunnels, fortifications, bridges, drains, and siege work.
- **Resilience:** Uses dwarven saving throws as listed above.

## Backstory

Brynja left Highforge to see how human rulers built and maintained the frontier they claimed to govern. Her exact craft, family expectations, and reason for staying with the company rather than returning home remain for the player to decide.

## Shared company history

Brynja has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Who once believed her warning about a structure that appeared perfectly safe?
- Which companion taught her a non-dwarven craft she now uses regularly?
- What did the company recover that Brynja insisted on returning rather than selling?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** Honest labor creates obligations that ownership alone does not.
- **Strength:** She can explain what a structure, object, or workforce is actually worth.
- **Fault:** Slavery, fraud, and shoddy workmanship provoke the same immediate fury in her.
- **Customize:** Choose her Highforge trade and whether she is expected to inherit a family workshop.
