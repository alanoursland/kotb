## Arcane abilities

- **Spellcasting:** Two 1st-level spells and one 2nd-level spell each day.
- **Arcane examination:** After studying an active magical effect or object for a turn, Alexios can describe observable structure and likely magical school. This does not identify command words or every property.
- **Surveying:** With cord, chalk, and time, he creates reliable scale maps, estimates distances, and notices impossible dimensions or routes that fail to reconcile.
- **Chokepoint instinct:** Alexios can identify where a barrier, fire, missile line, or retreat would control a normal corridor before combat begins.

## Backstory

Alexios joined the Duke's adventurers after magical study left him with useful knowledge, limited prospects, and something to prove outside a classroom. The player should decide who trained him, why that relationship ended, and whether he seeks recognition, independence, or vindication.

## Shared company history

Alexios has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Who has final authority to tell him not to cast *sleep* into a confused melee?
- Which companion helped recover or repair his spellbook?
- What mapping error does the company still mention whenever he becomes overconfident?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** Most dangers become manageable once their boundaries are understood.
- **Strength:** He controls living enemies and physical space extremely well.
- **Fault:** He trusts a mechanical explanation more readily than a social one.
- **Customize:** Choose his former teacher and the theory they could never agree upon.
