## Clerical abilities

- **Turn undead:** Skeletons and zombies turn automatically; ghouls on 7+, wights on 9+, and wraiths on 11+. Roll 2d6 for the number of Hit Dice affected.
- **Field chaplain:** Marcus can organize ordinary first aid, burial details, watches, and frightened noncombatants without needing an ability check under normal conditions.
- **Institutional standing:** He receives +1 on reaction checks when dealing openly with ducal officials or the Church of Karameikos, provided he is not asking them to violate their duties.

## Backstory

Marcus joined the Duke's open call because he wanted the Church of Karameikos to be known for service beyond comfortable city parishes. The player should decide what personal failure made him prefer practical protection to advancement within the hierarchy.

## Shared company history

Marcus has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Which companion taught him that rules sometimes obstruct the right outcome?
- Who has heard the private doubt he never voices during a crisis?
- When did the company save Marcus after his armor made retreat impossible?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** A shared Karameikos can be built without erasing either people.
- **Strength:** He remains useful when everyone else is frightened or wounded.
- **Fault:** He can mistake institutional compromise for genuine reconciliation.
- **Customize:** Decide who sponsored his ordination and what Church practice he privately questions.
