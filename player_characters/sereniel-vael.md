## Elven abilities

- **Infravision:** 60 feet in darkness.
- **Secret doors:** A 1–2 on 1d6 notices a secret door when Sereniel actively searches the appropriate surface.
- **Arcane magic:** Two 1st-level spells and one 2nd-level spell each day.
- **Martial training:** May use all weapons and wear armor while casting spells.
- **Callarii fieldcraft:** Sereniel handles ordinary horses, boats, forest camps, and river crossings without a check under normal conditions.

## Backstory

Sereniel first answered the Duke's call as a courier, guide, or observer and discovered they preferred the company's chosen loyalties to solitary work. The player should define their Callarii obligations and whether ducal service is a vocation, an experiment, or a temporary bargain.

## Shared company history

Sereniel has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Who learned their language first, and which phrase did they teach badly?
- Which companion has ridden Sereniel's horse or shared their place in a boat during an emergency?
- When did the company choose negotiation because Sereniel asked them to wait?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** Cooperation does not require sameness.
- **Strength:** They can speak to several cave peoples without the Concordant Staff.
- **Fault:** They are quick to interpret clumsy inclusion as an attempt at assimilation.
- **Customize:** Name their horse or boat and identify the Callarii elder whose opinion matters to them.
