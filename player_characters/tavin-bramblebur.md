## Halfling abilities and field skills

- **Missile accuracy:** +1 ancestry bonus is included in the listed missile attacks.
- **Small defense:** Armor Class improves by 2 against creatures larger than humans.
- **Hide:** 90% outdoors when cover is available; 1–2 on 1d6 in dungeon or urban cover when motionless and quiet.
- **Supply sense:** Tavin can estimate how many people a quantity of ordinary food will sustain and how much wagon or storeroom space it requires.
- **Hospitality:** When Tavin offers meaningful food, comfort, or fair trade rather than a token gesture, grant +1 on the first reaction check.

## Backstory

Tavin left the Five Shires for reasons he describes differently depending on the meal and found steady danger through Duke Stefan's open call. The player should decide whether he seeks fortune, novelty, respect, distance from home, or a story he is not ready to tell.

## Shared company history

Tavin has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Who first trusted his supply calculations over an official quartermaster's?
- Which companion knows why he actually left the Shires?
- What disastrous meal does the whole company still blame on him?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** If peace cannot feed anyone, it will not remain peace.
- **Strength:** He turns promises into quantities and workable exchanges.
- **Fault:** He sometimes treats cultural wounds as problems a good market can solve quickly.
- **Customize:** Choose his family trade and the comfort from home he always carries.
