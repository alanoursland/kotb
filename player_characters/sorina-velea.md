## Arcane abilities

- **Spellcasting:** Two 1st-level spells and one 2nd-level spell each day.
- **Occult examination:** After studying a place, corpse, or ritual object for a turn, Sorina can distinguish common fraud, ordinary Traladaran folk practice, arcane magic, and likely necromancy. She does not automatically identify an Immortal, cult, or political organization.
- **Evidence preservation:** Sorina knows how to wrap, label, sketch, and transport dangerous ritual evidence without casually cleaning or combining it.
- **Folklore memory:** A successful Intelligence check recalls a relevant rural warning or story. The story may be symbolic, incomplete, or attached to the wrong historical explanation.

## Backstory

Sorina began studying magic after a local incident convinced her that dismissing every old warning as superstition was as foolish as believing all of them. The player should choose that incident, her teacher, and whether curiosity or responsibility keeps her with the company.

## Shared company history

Sorina has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Who once stopped her from examining something that was still dangerous?
- Which companion believes one of her favorite stories despite claiming otherwise?
- What unexplained object from an earlier mission does the company still possess?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** Stories preserve evidence, but they do not interpret themselves.
- **Strength:** She separates magical facts from religious and political claims.
- **Fault:** Curiosity can keep her studying after sensible people begin backing away.
- **Customize:** Define her first magical incident and the warning she always obeys because of it.
