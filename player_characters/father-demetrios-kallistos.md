## Class abilities and special rules

- **Turn undead:** Demetrios presents his holy symbol and rolls 2d6. Skeletons are destroyed automatically; zombies and ghouls are turned automatically; wights turn on 7+, wraiths on 9+, and mummies on 11+. Roll 2d6 for the number of Hit Dice affected unless the result is automatic destruction.
- **Clerical prayers:** Two 1st-level and one 2nd-level prayer per day.
- **Diplomatic authority:** He carries letters identifying him as the Duke's accompanying priest and ecclesiastical witness. They command courtesy, not obedience.
- **Languages:** His ordinary language ability is useful without the staff, but he does not know humanoid cave languages.
- **Restriction:** He will not fight beside the party, enter an unsecured chamber first, pursue a retreating enemy, or remain in a battle merely to provide healing.

## The Concordant Staff

This old processional staff is an artifact of the **Church of Thyatis**, temporarily entrusted to Demetrios for the Duke's mission. Its chased-silver head bears two open hands separated by a small flame. It is obviously valuable to a priest or antiquarian, but it does not look like a weapon.

The staff continuously supplies a modified form of the 6th-level clerical prayer **Speak with Monsters** while Demetrios actively focuses upon it.

### Activation and limits

- Demetrios must hold the staff upright, speak a brief invocation, and devote his full concentration to it.
- While maintaining the effect he may listen and ask **one question per round**, but can do nothing else. He cannot move, attack, cast another prayer, turn undead, manipulate equipment, or actively defend with his shield.
- Taking damage, being forcibly moved, falling unconscious, or choosing another action ends the effect immediately.
- There is no daily or total duration limit. It continues only while Demetrios concentrates.
- The staff cannot invoke the reversed prayer, *babble*.
- No one else can activate it while it remains under its present charge.

### Speak with Monsters

Within 30 feet, Demetrios may address any **living** creature. Even unintelligent living creatures understand his questions and can make whatever response they are capable of giving. The staff has no effect on undead.

Activating the staff **breaks an attack** by living creatures. Every living creature within 30 feet stops attacking and listens for **one full turn**—10 minutes, or 60 combat rounds. This affects living creatures already charging, shooting, or fighting when the invocation is made. It does not interrupt an attacker outside the area, and undead inside the area continue acting normally.

During that turn:

- Every living language-using creature in the area can understand and be understood by every other living language-using creature there. Everyone except Demetrios may converse freely. The staff translates words, not lies, metaphors, etiquette, motives, or cultural assumptions.
- Demetrios remains limited to one question each round because activating and sustaining the staff consumes his attention.
- A question asked by Demetrios receives **+2 on any reaction or negotiation check made to determine whether the recipient answers**. If no roll is appropriate, treat the recipient as noticeably more willing to answer than normal.
- No one is compelled to answer, and an answer may be incomplete, evasive, or deliberately false.

At the end of the initial turn, the pause continues indefinitely if Demetrios maintains concentration and keeps the other side engaged in conversation. He can stave off renewed attack for as long as the exchange continues. Hostile, evasive, or dishonest answers still count as conversation; complete silence or a categorical refusal to engage allows the attack to resume when the initial turn expires.

During the initial turn, affected creatures cannot initiate or continue an attack. They may defend themselves if attacked by someone outside the effect. After that turn, a participant who breaks the continuing parley by attacking gains no protection from the staff.

The staff makes conversation possible, stops immediate violence, and can preserve a parley. It does not charm anyone, compel truth, erase old grievances, or force agreement.

### Custody protocol

- Demetrios keeps the staff in his own hand or secured beside him while sleeping.
- He does not lend it, demonstrate it casually, or permit experiments upon it.
- If danger becomes likely, he withdraws before the party commits to battle.
- If the staff is taken, its recovery and safe return supersede the expedition's other objectives for him.
- Publicly, he says only: **“The Church has charged me with its safe return. That charge must be my first priority.”**

## Escape procedure

When violence appears imminent:

1. If the attackers are within 30 feet and using the relic will not expose it to immediate seizure, Demetrios activates the staff. The attack stops for one turn.
2. He uses the pause to begin genuine dialogue. If conversation takes hold, he can maintain the truce indefinitely, but he remains stationary and defenseless while focusing.
3. If dialogue fails, the party attacks, or attackers outside the aura remain active, he ends the effect and uses *hold person* against pursuers who can be affected by it.
4. He uses *darkness* to block missiles, observation, or pursuit through a choke point, and *protection from evil* if a supernatural attacker closes with him.
5. He flees with the staff. He does not return to retrieve a fallen party member while the route remains unsecured.

If the party chooses battle while parley remains possible, Demetrios withdraws. He does not use the staff's peaceful influence to give the party a tactical surprise round.

## Backstory

### Where I come from

Demetrios grew up in Kerendas in a household of minor imperial officials. His family expected him to become a lawyer or administrator. A talent for languages, ritual, and patient argument instead carried him into the Church of Thyatis, where he was trained to see ordered institutions as the surest defense against cruelty and chaos.

### What changed me

Service in Traladara exposed him to communities that experienced imperial order as conquest rather than protection. He did not reject law or Thyatian learning, but he came to believe Duke Stefan's attempt to build a shared Karameikan identity offered a more faithful purpose for both. He transferred his allegiance to the Church of Karameikos openly and without scandal.

### Why I accepted the Duke's commission

Demetrios volunteered because the commission tests the belief that brought him to Karameikos: strangers need not be made identical before they can live under a common peace. The Church of Thyatis agreed to lend the Concordant Staff because he had once served it honorably and because Duke Stefan personally guaranteed its return.

### What I want

He wants one durable agreement between the Keep and a community in the Caves—something specific enough to survive fear, distance, and the departure of heroic negotiators.

### What I fear

He fears that his hope will expose others to betrayal, and that losing the staff will prove to both churches that trust across institutions was foolish.

### What I will not do

He will not use sacred hospitality to arrange an ambush. He will not surrender the staff to purchase the party's safety. He will not pretend that translation is consent.

## Dungeon Master note: the hidden charge

Demetrios is under a **Quest** laid as a condition of the staff's loan. He knows the magical charge is real: he must preserve the Concordant Staff and return it safely to its appointed Church of Thyatis custodian in Specularum when the mission ends or when continued field use becomes unreasonably dangerous.

He does not volunteer that he is magically bound. If asked about his caution, he answers truthfully that the Church charged him with the relic's safe return and that this duty must take priority. He regards the *Quest* as a solemn guarantee freely accepted, not as coercion he needs the party to defeat.

The charge does not require cowardice or abandonment at the first sign of danger. It does prevent him from knowingly gambling the staff, lending it to the party, joining an assault, or treating its loss as secondary to another objective.
