# Player Characters

This directory holds player character records and table-facing sheets for accompanying characters in the Riverfork Keep campaign.

- [Blank character sheet](character-sheet-template.md) — copy this file and rename the copy for a new character.
- [Father Demetrios Kallistos](father-demetrios-kallistos.md) — allied cleric and diplomatic support. This sheet contains a clearly marked Dungeon Master note.

## The established company

The players select four to six of the ten prepared adventurers below. In that version of the campaign, the selected characters have worked together since 1st level, completed several successful contracts from Duke Stefan's open call, and earned 3rd level as a company. They know one another's habits, signals, strengths, fears, and weaknesses. Unselected characters are not former companions waiting offstage; they simply are not members of that version of the company.

Their previous missions are deliberately unnamed. The players should invent one shared victory, one shared mistake, and one unresolved disagreement before play.

### Prepared adventurers

- [Irina Petrescu](irina-petrescu.md) — Traladaran cleric; undead, healing, and the old faith.
- [Marcus Calpurnius](marcus-calpurnius.md) — Karameikan cleric; protection, healing, and ducal institutions.
- [Leontes Varro](leontes-varro.md) — Thyatian fighter; armored defense and field command.
- [Stelian Ardelean](stelian-ardelean.md) — Traladaran fighter; tracking, archery, and wilderness survival.
- [Brynja Stronghollow](brynja-stronghollow.md) — Highforge dwarf; stonework, traps, metals, and resilience.
- [Sereniel Vael](sereniel-vael.md) — Callarii elf; woodland reconnaissance, archery, and control magic.
- [Tavin Bramblebur](tavin-bramblebur.md) — Hin scout; stealth, missiles, food, and trade.
- [Danica Murescu](danica-murescu.md) — mixed-heritage thief; infiltration, traps, ledgers, and criminal networks.
- [Alexios Karios](alexios-karios.md) — Thyatian magic-user; direct and battlefield-control magic.
- [Sorina Velea](sorina-velea.md) — Traladaran magic-user; defensive magic and occult investigation.

Ordinary player character histories should remain player-safe. Any accompanying-character sheet containing private information must mark its Dungeon Master section clearly.
