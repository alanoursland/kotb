- **Ledgers and routes:** Danica can recognize ordinary merchant records, cargo tallies, coded ownership marks, smuggling compartments, and attempts to make related transactions look unrelated.
- **Evidence discipline:** If allowed to collect a scene before others loot it, she can preserve who carried what, where an object was hidden, and which pieces probably belong together.

## Languages

- **Thyatian and Traladaran:** native bilingual; spoken and written.
- **Darokinian:** commercial and conversational fluency.
- **Thieves' cant:** criminal introductions, warnings, marks, and limited coded speech.
- **Commercial cipher:** invoices, warehouse abbreviations, caravan marks, and simple substitution codes.
- **Company signs:** the party's practiced silent field signals.

## Backstory

Danica entered ducal service with useful skills and a history she describes as “warehouse work,” allowing the player to decide how legal that work really was. The company knows the truth—or at least the version she trusted them enough to tell.

## Shared company history

Danica has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Who knows the most dangerous name from her former life?
- Which companion objects most strongly when she opens something that is not theirs?
- When did Danica's copied ledger save the whole company from being blamed?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** Follow the money before believing the explanation.
- **Strength:** She can turn scattered objects into a chain of evidence.
- **Fault:** Secrets feel like resources to her, even when friends deserve the truth.
- **Customize:** Choose her former employer and one crime she refuses to commit again.
