## Fighter abilities and field skills

- **Tracking:** In suitable terrain, Stelian finds ordinary tracks automatically when time is available. Under pressure, concealment, or bad weather, make a Wisdom check; particularly alien tracks may impose a penalty.
- **Monster sign:** After examining tracks, remains, or a lair for a turn, he can usually distinguish predator, scavenger, territorial animal, and organized raider.
- **Campcraft:** A camp chosen and prepared by Stelian reduces the chance of ordinary wilderness surprise by 1 on the die, to a minimum of 1-in-6.
- **Forager:** In productive wilderness he can supplement, but not replace, the company's rations.

## Languages

- **Traladaran:** native; spoken fluently and read adequately.
- **Thyatian:** fluent field speech; reads ordinary notices and orders.
- **Goblin:** conversational, learned from border encounters; his accent is rural and obvious.
- **Company signs:** the party's practiced silent field signals, supplemented by bird calls.

## Backstory

Stelian answered the Duke's call after trouble on the forest edge convinced him that roads and settlements create obligations in both directions. The player should decide whom he left behind, what sort of wilderness he knows best, and why the company became home.

## Shared company history

Stelian has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Which companion learned to recognize his warning calls first?
- Who once convinced him not to kill a creature he believed was stalking the company?
- Which shared journey produced the field guide he still carries?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** A predator's nature and a person's choices are different kinds of danger.
- **Strength:** He notices physical causes before accepting supernatural explanations.
- **Fault:** He has little patience for people who deliberately prey on the helpless.
- **Customize:** Choose his home forest and the animal he refuses to hunt.
