## Fighter abilities and field skills

- **Hold the line:** When Leontes spends his action organizing hirelings or frightened allies rather than attacking, ordinary followers within hearing gain +1 morale until the next round.
- **Military eye:** He can estimate the discipline, readiness, and command structure of an armed group after observing it for a turn.
- **Protection discipline:** Before entering danger, choose one companion. When marching is possible, Leontes places himself between that companion and the most obvious threat; this is positioning, not a supernatural guard ability.
- **Weapons:** Trained with all weapons and armor.

## Languages

- **Thyatian:** native; spoken and written.
- **Traladaran:** fluent from years in Karameikos and the company's travels.
- **Military Goblin:** commands, ranks, numbers, directions, food, weapons, prisoners, and surrender terms. Complex conversation requires a check or translator.
- **Company signs:** the party's practiced silent field signals; Leontes usually gives them in combat.

## Backstory

Leontes took work from the Duke after discovering that guarding ordinary travelers mattered more to him than serving a prestigious master. The player should decide whether he left military service honorably, refused it altogether, or is trying to prove himself worthy of it.

## Shared company history

Leontes has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Who did he carry out of the company's worst defeat?
- Which companion can persuade him to abandon a position before it becomes a last stand?
- Who helped win the sword +1, and what did the company agree to call it?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** Strength is justified by what it protects.
- **Strength:** Soldiers and martial leaders recognize that he understands their responsibilities.
- **Fault:** His first instinct is to turn uncertainty into a perimeter he controls.
- **Customize:** Choose his former occupation and the order he once obeyed but still regrets.
