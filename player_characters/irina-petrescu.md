## Clerical abilities

- **Turn undead:** Skeletons and zombies turn automatically; ghouls on 7+, wights on 9+, and wraiths on 11+. Roll 2d6 for the number of Hit Dice affected.
- **Religious knowledge:** Irina can identify ordinary Traladaran prayers, funerary customs, feast symbols, and common distortions of the old faith. She can recognize that something is alien or corrupted without automatically knowing its true source.
- **Pastoral authority:** She receives +1 on reaction checks with Traladaran faithful when acting openly as a priest and not abusing their trust.

## Backstory

Irina left parish life to answer the Duke's open call after an early adventure showed her that old evils thrive where frightened people have no trusted witness. Her home, family circumstances, and exact reason for remaining with the company are yours to define.

## Shared company history

Irina has adventured with every selected party member since 1st level. Decide with the group:

- Which companion once trusted Irina's judgment when the evidence looked bad?
- Whose wound did she fail to prevent, even though she later healed it?
- What shared mistake still causes disagreement between her and one companion?

## Character direction

- **Belief:** Stefan may become a good ruler, but conquest alone did not make him rightful.
- **Strength:** She can distinguish genuine faith from someone exploiting its language.
- **Fault:** Once she recognizes a sacred obligation, she can become inflexible about it.
- **Customize:** Name her home parish and one Traladaran custom she refuses to abandon.
