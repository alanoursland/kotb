# Progression

The campaign's core narrative as a sequence of goals with leeway around each — a critical-path spine the players advance along, surrounded by optional threads and multiple valid approaches.

> **Status:** framework only. The beats below are placeholders to be filled from discussion. We define the *shape* here so the content has somewhere to land.

## Design vocabulary

Terms we'll use consistently so the structure stays legible:

- **Beat** — a discrete goal or situation on the critical path. The player knows (or discovers) what the next beat is.
- **Gate** — what must be true to move from one beat to the next. Gates can be soft (the story nudges) or hard (this literally can't proceed until X). Prefer soft gates; reserve hard ones for the genuine turning points.
- **Approach** — a distinct valid way through a beat (sword, gold, truth, alliance). Every beat should have at least two. This is the leeway.
- **Thread** — optional content that branches off a beat: a faction, an NPC's personal arc, a side mystery. Threads can be skipped without breaking the spine, but they change its texture and the available approaches downstream.
- **Curdle** — a victory that reveals a complication on a delay (per the overview's "let victories curdle"). A scripted consequence that fires later, turning a clean win into a deeper problem.
