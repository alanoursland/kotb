# 6. Unharvested Buck

**Zone:** central woods | **Feature:** conspicuous carcass | **Clue:** edible food deliberately wasted

> A fine buck lies on its side beneath the hazels. Its antlers are intact. No hide was taken, no haunch cut away, and no predator opened the belly. Only flies have claimed it.

## Run this area

- The buck died two or three days ago from an axe-like blow behind the shoulder and a second crushing strike to the neck.
- Stelian can state that no predator killed it and no competent hunter handled it afterward.
- Tavin estimates that the carcass could have fed the whole company for several days. Hide, sinew, antler, and meat were all abandoned.
- Danica reconstructs two attackers: one drove or blocked the buck; the heavier one delivered the killing blows. Neither knelt to butcher it.
- Sorina's turn-long occult examination finds faint necromantic contamination around the wounds, consistent with contact by an animated corpse rather than a spell cast on the animal.
- A red fiber is caught in dried blood. It resembles the thread at the foresters' camp but is not enough by itself to identify anyone.
