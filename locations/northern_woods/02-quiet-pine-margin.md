# 2. Quiet Pine Margin

**Zone:** outer woods | **Light:** dim beneath pines | **Clue:** animals fled rather than starved

> Pine needles soften every footfall. Cones lie unopened and berries hang dark on their stems. There is food here, but almost nothing moves to eat it.

## Run this area

- The mast, berries, and ground plants are healthy. This is not a blight.
- Tavin can say that the untouched food should support squirrels, birds, and deer. Scarcity did not empty this patch.
- Sereniel recognizes a woodland under repeated disturbance: creatures have learned that returning is dangerous.
- Stelian finds old animal tracks heading south and fresher tracks only at the edge. The retreat happened over weeks, not in one panic.
- Sorina or Alexios can rule out a persistent magical field. There may be necromancy elsewhere, but the silence itself is learned fear.
