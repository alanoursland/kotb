# Northern Woods

**Location:** north of the road between Riverfork Keep and the Caves of Chaos | **Mode:** loose exploration network | **Areas:** 18

> The road noise fades behind you, but the woods do not take its place. No thrush calls. No squirrel scratches bark. Wind moves the high branches above a forest that seems to be holding its breath.

These pages treat the northern woods like a text adventure, not a measured wilderness map. The links describe useful directions and recognizable routes. Traveling to an adjacent area usually takes **20–40 minutes** while exploring. The company can abandon the search and regain the road at any time; do not make them retrace every link merely to leave.

## What is happening

The silence is not an illusion or a permanent magical effect. Undead from the [Shrine](../caves/k-entry-passage.md) sweep these woods at night, killing game, smashing nests and dens, and driving surviving animals away. They leave edible carcasses to rot because the object is not food—it is **famine**. The cult wants the Caves hungry enough to raid and the surrounding frontier frightening enough to escape close scrutiny.

The party can establish the truth in layers:

| Conclusion | Redundant clues |
|---|---|
| Animals are leaving or being killed, not merely hiding from the party. | [Quiet Pine Margin](02-quiet-pine-margin.md), [Empty Game Trail](04-empty-game-trail.md), [Empty Salt Lick](08-empty-salt-lick.md) |
| The deaths are deliberate waste, not hunting or ordinary predation. | [Unharvested Buck](06-unharvested-buck.md), [Carrion Hollow](11-carrion-hollow.md), [Foxless Den](15-foxless-den.md) |
| Human-shaped, organized killers work at night. | [Blackwater Rill](05-blackwater-rill.md), [Old Watch Foundation](09-old-watch-foundation.md), [Bone-White Clearing](13-bone-white-clearing.md) |
| The killers are undead directed by the cult. | [Carrion Hollow](11-carrion-hollow.md), [Moonlit Kill Ground](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md), [Shrineward Escarpment](17-shrineward-escarpment.md) |
| The patrols originate at the Shrine in the Caves. | [Ravine Overlook](12-ravine-overlook.md), [Moonlit Kill Ground](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md), [Shrineward Escarpment](17-shrineward-escarpment.md) |

Do not ask for a roll to notice a clue after the party spends a turn examining its location. Rolls answer questions such as **how quickly**, **while pursued**, or **before darkness**, not whether the campaign's evidence exists.

## Likely scenarios

### Survey the unnatural quiet

Start at the [Roadside Verge](01-roadside-verge.md). Sereniel, Stelian, or any player who studies the forest can establish that the quiet deepens toward the Caves. Tavin can show that edible plants remain untouched while game has disappeared: this is not ordinary over-foraging.

### Follow the dead animals

The clearest daylight route runs from the [Empty Game Trail](04-empty-game-trail.md) to the [Unharvested Buck](06-unharvested-buck.md), then through [Carrion Hollow](11-carrion-hollow.md) to the [Bone-White Clearing](13-bone-white-clearing.md). It proves scale and intent without requiring a night encounter.

### Search at night

The first time the party deliberately searches after full dark, they **encounter undead** after one or two area transitions. Use the tracks or distant branch-clacking to draw them toward the [Moonlit Kill Ground](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md). This is guaranteed, not a wandering-monster roll.

### Shadow a patrol to its source

Surviving, turning, following, or avoiding the night detail opens a route through the [Shrineward Escarpment](17-shrineward-escarpment.md). The trail ends at the Shrine entrance. This shows where the undead come from; it does not by itself reveal why the cult is doing it.

### Look for the Mad Hermit

The hermit is not a random encounter. Mention his trace at the [Windfall Maze](07-windfall-maze.md), [Ruined Vael Steading](10-ruined-vael-steading.md), and [Split-Limestone Rise](14-split-limestone-rise.md), but do not offer a route to his home unless the players say they are looking for him. After they find **two hermit clues** and make an active search, Stelian finds the trail automatically in fair conditions; another careful tracker can find it with a Wisdom check. Only then reveal [the Hidden Hollow Oak](18-hidden-hollow-oak.md).

### Fast table version

For a 30–45 minute wilderness sequence, use four beats:

1. Begin at the [Roadside Verge](01-roadside-verge.md) and montage the growing silence through [area 2](02-quiet-pine-margin.md) or [area 4](04-empty-game-trail.md).
2. Present the [Unharvested Buck](06-unharvested-buck.md) as the first undeniable piece of waste.
3. Let investigation lead to the [Bone-White Clearing](13-bone-white-clearing.md), where the operation begins to look organized.
4. If the party waits for dark, run the [Moonlit Kill Ground](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md) and let survivors or tracks lead to the [Shrineward Escarpment](17-shrineward-escarpment.md).

This preserves the complete deduction—unnatural silence, deliberate waste, organized undead, Shrine origin—without requiring the party to traverse the whole network.

## Character spotlights

- **Stelian:** follows animal flight, separates undead patrol tracks from Lucan's trail, and tails the patrol to the Shrine.
- **Sereniel:** reads the broken woodland pattern, notices that no seasonal or Callarii explanation accounts for it, and can scout without announcing the party.
- **Tavin:** quantifies the wasted food and recognizes deliberate destruction of the forest's supply rather than hunger-driven hunting.
- **Danica:** reconstructs positions, repeated routes, cord knots, caches, and the fact that separate incidents belong to one operation.
- **Sorina:** distinguishes ordinary death from lingering necromancy and preserves cloth, cords, soil, and remains as evidence.
- **Irina:** recognizes corrupted funerary handling and symbols alien to the Church of Traladar; she and Marcus can turn the night patrols.
- **Marcus:** organizes a defensible watch and can turn undead; his institutional standing helps convert findings into action at the Keep.
- **Leontes:** recognizes files, rally points, command positions, and the systematic shape of the sweep.
- **Brynja:** reads the old watch foundation, concealed stone cache, maintained ascent, and cheap iron fittings.
- **Alexios:** maps apparently separate routes to one source, tests the absence of a forest-wide spell, and identifies good observation and retreat points.
- **Father Demetrios:** the Concordant Staff does not affect undead. He warns the company that it cannot protect them here and withdraws with the relic if they commit to battle.

## Night procedure

- Normal visibility and light rules apply. A visible lantern or campfire lets the undead locate the party but also prevents ordinary surprise.
- A camp prepared by Stelian reduces ordinary wilderness surprise as described on his sheet. The [Old Watch Foundation](09-old-watch-foundation.md) is the strongest fixed campsite.
- After the guaranteed first encounter, use additional undead only when they add information or pressure. Repeated attrition fights will obscure the investigation.
- Turned undead retreat toward [area 17](17-shrineward-escarpment.md). A quiet party can follow them.
