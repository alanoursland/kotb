# 11. Carrion Hollow

**Zone:** deep central woods | **Feature:** accumulated carcasses | **Clue:** the campaign of killing spans months

> The ground sinks into a leaf-choked hollow. Bones and hides emerge at every depth: hare, fox, boar, three deer, and things too far gone to name. The only steady sound is flies.

## Run this area

- The animals died at different times across several months. This is a pattern, not a single slaughter.
- Some have blade wounds; others have crushed skulls or broken spines. None was properly butchered.
- Tavin can estimate that the recoverable meat originally represented hundreds of meals. Someone spent labor to destroy food.
- Stelian finds no feeding marks on the newer bodies. Older bones show only late scavenging after the killers stopped returning regularly.
- Sorina finds necromantic contamination on several wound edges but no ritual circle and no spell centered on the hollow. Animated dead carried the taint here.
- Danica can sort three delivery routes by mud, fibers, and damage. They converge from different parts of the woods and continue eastward, proving a coordinated operation.
- Irina sees that the bodies were discarded without hunting custom, sacrifice, or funerary meaning. This is desecration through indifference, not a recognizable Traladaran rite.
