# 13. Bone-White Clearing

**Zone:** deep eastern woods | **Feature:** undead formation ground | **Clue:** cult direction and military organization

> White splinters crunch beneath the leaves. The clearing is almost circular, its brush trampled into two straight ranks. A blackened beech at the northern edge bears a loop of iron on a faded red cord.

## Run this area

- The white fragments are animal bone crushed under repeated feet, not a constructed altar.
- Leontes recognizes a rally and assignment point. Two files arrive, stand in ranks, and depart along different killing routes.
- Danica finds repeated station marks: the same individuals occupy the same places. One heavier walker moves behind both ranks like an overseer.
- The iron loop and cord are crude but standardized. They resemble the Shrine's iron-ring symbols and the fittings in the bugbear station, although that significance requires comparison.
- Irina can say the object is not a symbol of Halav, Petra, Zirchev, or an ordinary Traladaran funerary practice. Sorina senses weak necromancy from handling by undead, not a powerful enchantment in the ring itself.
- Taking the cord is useful evidence. Sketching it in place first preserves that it marked a command location rather than merely lying in the woods.
- At night, distant movement leads north to [area 16](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md). If the party waits here, a patrol may rally before the encounter.
