# 10. Ruined Vael Steading

**Zone:** far western woods | **Feature:** burned homestead | **Thread:** the Mad Hermit's former life

> Apple trees gone wild surround a foundation blackened by an old fire. Nettles fill what was once a garden. Two low fieldstones stand together beneath a rowan, their names softened by weather.

## Run this area

- The destruction is years old, not part of the present animal killings. This is the former Vael steading.
- The stones mark Lucan's wife and daughter. Careful cleaning reveals the daughter's name: **Anneke**.
- A small carved horse lies wedged beneath the collapsed hearth. It is worn smooth by a child's hand and has no sale value.
- Irina recognizes a hurried but sincere frontier burial. Familiar funerary elements were improvised after no priest came.
- There is no body for Lucan. Old records at Riverfork would identify him as the surviving homesteader who disappeared into the forest.
- If the party actively searches for the hermit, Stelian finds occasional recent bare footprints that approach the graves, stop for a long time, and leave without entering the ruin. Mountain-lion tracks keep watch from the orchard edge.
- Finding the keepsake or the name **Anneke** can create a lucid pause during the encounter at [area 18](18-hidden-hollow-oak.md). It does not cure Lucan.
