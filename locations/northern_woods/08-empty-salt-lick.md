# 8. Empty Salt Lick

**Zone:** western woods | **Feature:** destroyed game-gathering place | **Clue:** long-term supply sabotage

> A shallow basin of pale earth lies among hoof-scored stones. The ground should be churned by deer and boar. Instead, the mineral crust has been hacked apart and scattered into wet leaf mold.

## Run this area

- This was a natural salt lick used for years. Recent tool blows ruined it and rain is carrying the mineral away.
- Tavin recognizes an attack on future food. A hungry hunter camps here; someone creating scarcity destroys it.
- Stelian finds stiff, repeated boot and bare-foot tracks circling the basin. No animal was butchered here.
- Brynja can tell the blows came from heavy iron tools wielded without care for edge or balance—axes used by strength, not skilled labor.
- A separate sign crosses the western edge: a mountain lion's scrape and one cut length of rabbit snare. It belongs to a living hunter and should not be folded into the undead pattern.
