# 5. Blackwater Rill

**Zone:** central approach | **Feature:** narrow stream and mud | **Clue:** repeated human-shaped patrol route

> Tea-dark water slips over roots and pale stones. The mud beside it is crowded with footprints, but there is no fire pit, bed, cut branch, or other sign that living travelers ever stopped here.

## Run this area

- Several sets of human-shaped tracks cross northeast and southwest. Some wear ruined boots; others are bare, broad, and unnaturally flat.
- Stelian can separate at least four passages on different nights. The same feet repeat the route with nearly identical spacing.
- The gait is wrong: one walker drags a foot without ever compensating, while another plants exposed heel bone deeply and leaves almost no toe impression.
- Leontes recognizes two files using a crossing point, not scattered hunters.
- Fish, insects, and frogs still live in the water. The destruction concentrates on land animals that can be driven or caught.
- Following the tracks at night eventually draws the party to [the Moonlit Kill Ground](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md), even if they take another geographic exit first.

## Exits

- [Go southwest to the Quiet Pine Margin](02-quiet-pine-margin.md) — Follow the rill toward softer ground.
- [Go southeast to the Abandoned Forester's Spur](03-abandoned-foresters-spur.md) — Take the water-fetching path.
- [Go northwest to the Unharvested Buck](06-unharvested-buck.md) — Follow the repeated tracks upstream.
- [Go northeast to the Old Watch Foundation](09-old-watch-foundation.md) — Climb the stony bank along the northeast-bound file.
- **Leave the northern woods** — Follow the rill downstream until it meets the roadside ditch.
