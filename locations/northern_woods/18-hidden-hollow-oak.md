# 18. Hidden Hollow Oak

**Zone:** concealed western interior | **Occupants:** Lucan Vael and a mountain lion | **Access:** active hermit search only

> A thorn bush conceals a black opening in the trunk of an enormous oak. Rough furniture and a bed of leaves occupy the hollow. Above you, a heavy branch bends without wind—and two yellow eyes open among the leaves.

## Finding this area

Do not place the hollow oak on the party's ordinary route. It is found only when all of the following are true:

1. The players say they are searching for the Mad Hermit or deliberately following the **living** trail.
2. They have found at least two clues from the [Windfall Maze](07-windfall-maze.md), [Ruined Vael Steading](10-ruined-vael-steading.md), or [Split-Limestone Rise](14-split-limestone-rise.md).
3. A tracker follows the concealed route. Stelian succeeds automatically in fair conditions when given time; another careful tracker may make a Wisdom check.

Failure or casual exploration returns the party to one of the connected areas without revealing why the tracks vanished.

## Run the encounter

- **Lucan Vael:** 3rd-level thief; AC 4; hp 15; attack +2; dagger 1d4+2; ML 10; move silently 30%; hide in shadows 20%. A strike from behind uses the module's full bonus and damage.
- **Mountain lion:** AC 6; HD 3+2; hp 15; three attacks for 1d3/1d3/1d6; ML 8. It begins above the entrance and can leap for +2 to its first attack routine.
- Lucan is ill and unpredictable, not a cultist. He may begin with a lucid welcome, a warning, or an ambush depending on how the party approaches.
- Among his ravings, the name **“Anneke”** returns clearly. Showing the carved horse or using her name creates a pause and a lucid flicker; it does not cure him or make him safe.
- The mountain lion is Lucan's remaining bond. Calm, food, distance, and Father Demetrios's staff provide social approaches. Killing it makes peaceful removal nearly impossible.
- The cult patrols usually avoid this patch because Lucan and the lion attack lone stragglers. He does not understand the famine plan and is not a useful conspiracy witness.

## Things Lucan might say

Lucan shifts between warning, memory, suspicion, and brief lucidity. He rarely answers a question directly. Use one line, leave space for the players to respond, and let his attention slide rather than delivering several quotations as a speech.

| When or if… | Lucan says… |
|---|---|
| He first sees the party | “Stop there. The ground after that root belongs to me.” |
| Someone advances after being warned | “One more step and the tree bites. I taught it where the throat is.” |
| Someone looks into the branches | “Don't stare at him. He thinks staring is the beginning of hunger.” |
| Asked his name | “Lucan Vael. That was the name on the grant. It may still be mine.” |
| Asked whether he lives here | “No. I wait here. Living was at the house.” |
| Asked about the ruined steading | “Good soil. Apples in the third year. Smoke in the last.” |
| Asked about his family | “My wife is inside. Keep your voice down or you'll wake her.” |
| Anneke's name is spoken | “Anneke? She knows not to come this far into the trees. Go fetch her.” |
| Shown Anneke's carved horse | “Where did you get that? Put it down gently. She'll know if you broke it.” |
| Offered food | “Set it on the stone and walk away. Gifts with hands attached are traps.” |
| Asked about the mountain lion | “He found me after everyone else stopped looking.” |
| Someone threatens the lion | “Hurt him and there will be no Lucan left to speak with.” |
| Asked about the silence | “The woods learned quiet. The loud ones were found first.” |
| Asked about the dead animals | “Dead men kill them and leave them whole. Wasteful things. Worse than wolves.” |
| Asked where the undead go | “Uphill before dawn. Always uphill. I don't follow bells.” |
| Asked who commands the undead | “A voice, perhaps. Dead men don't need reasons; living men lend them reasons.” |
| Asked about the Caves | “Mouths in the hill. Hungry mouths. We fed them once, and they ate the years after.” |
| Asked about Riverfork Keep | “Walls make promises at a distance. Up close they have arrow slits.” |
| Offered healing or a cure | “Cure what? The fire happened. Cure that first.” |
| Reached during a lucid moment | “I know you mean to help. Meaning well is not the same as knowing what to do.” |

The clearest factual statements he can provide are that **dead figures kill animals without taking the meat** and return **uphill before dawn**. He has heard a distant bell but does not know the Shrine, the cult, or the reason for the killings. Everything else should require corroboration.

## Care, capture, or violence

- A peaceful solution is getting Lucan to protected care through Father Branislav or another sanctuary, not recruiting him.
- He offers no useful service and cannot reliably promise future safety. Helping him is care without payoff.
- The module's buried chest remains under his leaf bed: 31 gp, 164 sp, a potion of invisibility, and a dagger +1. It is **his property**, not a reward for helping him. A sanctuary will not demand it as payment. Finding or taking it requires deliberately searching his home.

## Exits

Once discovered, the concealed trails can be followed reliably:

- [Go southeast to the Windfall Maze](07-windfall-maze.md) — Follow the log-hidden trail.
- [Go south to the Empty Salt Lick](08-empty-salt-lick.md) — Follow the mountain lion's western circuit.
- [Go south to the Ruined Vael Steading](10-ruined-vael-steading.md) — Follow Lucan's path to the graves.
- [Go southeast to the Split-Limestone Rise](14-split-limestone-rise.md) — Follow the smokeless-fire trail.
- **Leave the northern woods** — Guide Lucan or withdraw along the clearest known trail to the road.
