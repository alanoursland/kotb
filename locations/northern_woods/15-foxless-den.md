# 15. Foxless Den

**Zone:** deep eastern woods | **Feature:** destroyed predator den | **Clue:** eradication rather than game hunting

> A sandy bank has been torn open around a fox den. The vixen lies outside with a broken spine. Inside, the den is silent.

## Run this area

- The adult and kits were killed and left. Foxes are poor food compared with the nearby deer; this was not efficient hunting.
- Stelian can tell the den was dug open with an axe head and bare hands. One set of prints shows exposed finger bones pressed into damp sand.
- Sereniel understands the ecological consequence: foxes control rodents and mark a functioning woodland. Destroying dens makes the forest poorer and stranger beyond the immediate loss of meat.
- Sorina's examination finds the same faint necromantic contact as the buck and Carrion Hollow.
- Tavin can put the pattern plainly: whoever did this is not feeding anyone tonight. They are making sure fewer things can be eaten tomorrow.
- Tracks split northwest toward the active kill ground and northeast toward the route home.
