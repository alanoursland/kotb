# 4. Empty Game Trail

**Zone:** western woods | **Feature:** broad animal run | **Clue:** one-way movement

> The path is not made by boots. Bark is polished at flank height and the earth is pressed by generations of hooves—yet no droppings, fresh browsing, or recent beds mark the trail.

## Run this area

- Stelian finds overlapping animal tracks from several weeks ago. Most point south or southwest; almost none return.
- Two old snares were not merely sprung. Their stakes were pulled up and their cords chopped, as if someone meant to prevent later use.
- Tavin recognizes that a hunter would preserve a working trail and its snares. Destroying both sacrifices future food.
- Sereniel notices browse recovering on the northern side while the same plants remain cropped near the road. Game still exists; it has abandoned this ground.
- If the party waits quietly for a turn, nothing larger than an insect appears.

## Exits

- [Go southeast to the Quiet Pine Margin](02-quiet-pine-margin.md) — Follow the broad trail toward younger pines.
- [Go northeast to the Unharvested Buck](06-unharvested-buck.md) — Follow a sour smell and scattered flies.
- [Go northwest to the Empty Salt Lick](08-empty-salt-lick.md) — Follow the oldest hoof-worn branch uphill.
- **Leave the northern woods** — Turn south and follow the abandoned animal run to the road.
