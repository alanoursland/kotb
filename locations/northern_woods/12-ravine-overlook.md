# 12. Ravine Overlook

**Zone:** northern ridge | **Feature:** concealed view of the Caves of Chaos | **Use:** surveillance

> Through a screen of juniper, the land breaks into the Caves' ravine. Dark mouths puncture the slopes at different heights. At the western end, twisted trees cluster around one broad entrance like fingers around a wound.

## Run this area

- The overlook gives a partial view of the [Ravine Floor](../caves/ravine.md) without requiring the party to expose itself below.
- The twisted trees stand at the Shrine entrance. Ordinary animal paths bend away from them; human-shaped night tracks approach from the woods.
- At dusk, a patient observer sees stiff silhouettes leave the western entrance in files. Before dawn, survivors return by the same route.
- Alexios can map separate woods tracks against visible terrain and show that they converge near the Shrine rather than Brundt's cave or Khaeros's labyrinth.
- Sereniel can choose a concealed observation point that does not silhouette the party against the sky.
- Brundt and Khaeros may know that things leave the Shrine at night, but neither needs to know the scale or purpose of the woodland sweep.

## Exits

- [Go southwest into the Windfall Maze](07-windfall-maze.md) — Descend behind the ridge through fallen timber.
- [Go southwest by the lower path to Carrion Hollow](11-carrion-hollow.md) — Follow the smell into the central depression.
- [Go northeast to the Moonlit Kill Ground](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md) — Follow the ridge parallel to the night patrol route.
- [Descend to the Ravine Floor](../caves/ravine.md) — Take a steep game path into the Caves of Chaos.
- **Leave the northern woods** — Withdraw south along the ridge and regain the road.
