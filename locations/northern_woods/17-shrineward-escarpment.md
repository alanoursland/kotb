# 17. Shrineward Escarpment

**Zone:** northeastern edge | **Feature:** converging undead route | **Destination:** Shrine entrance

> The forest climbs through red-streaked stone and black roots. Tracks from several directions compress into one worn ascent. Above, the trees are twisted and swollen; beyond them waits a broad cave mouth breathing cold air.

## Run this area

- This is the final physical link between the woodland killings and the Shrine.
- Stelian can show that patrols leave after dark and return before dawn. The same dragging feet recur night after night.
- Alexios can reconcile the woods map: the rill crossing, rally point, kill ground, and return track all center on this ascent.
- Brynja sees deliberate maintenance. Loose stones have been moved aside so stiff walkers do not fall, and the path has been widened from the cave outward.
- Danica can match fibers, cord knots, axe wedges, and footprints from the earlier scenes. Together they describe one supplied operation.
- Irina recognizes the red-and-black presentation as alien to ordinary Traladaran worship. Sorina identifies accumulated necromantic passage, strongest toward the cave.
- Noise near the entrance can draw the Shrine's zombie response as described in the cave file.

## What this proves

The party can now make a strong factual claim: **undead based in the Shrine systematically kill and disperse the northern woods' animals.** The motive—starving the caves into raids—still requires observation of the caves, testimony, cult evidence, or Mara Sokol.
