# 14. Split-Limestone Rise

**Zone:** eastern high ground | **Feature:** concealed patrol cache | **Threads:** Shrine route and optional hermit trace

> A seam of pale limestone rises through the soil and splits into a narrow cleft. Loose stones inside have been stacked too evenly to be a natural fall.

## Run this area

- Brynja notices the reset stones automatically when she examines the cleft. Another careful search finds them without a roll if the party spends a turn.
- Behind the stones are replacement axe handles, a whetstone, tallow, red-and-black cloth strips, and cheap iron loops. The tools maintain equipment; there is no food, water, or bedding for living patrols.
- Brynja can identify the iron as common, inconsistent with jewelry or valuable religious metalwork. The uniformity suggests one workshop or supplied pattern.
- The main stiff-legged track heads northeast toward the Shrineward Escarpment.
- A separate living trail leaves ash from a tiny smokeless fire, rabbit bones split for marrow, wood shavings, and mountain-lion hair. Unlike the cult trail, this person eats what he kills and conceals his presence with skill.
- If the players are actively looking for the hermit and have another clue, this living trail can be followed to [area 18](18-hidden-hollow-oak.md).
