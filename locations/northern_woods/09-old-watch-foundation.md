# 9. Old Watch Foundation

**Zone:** eastern woods | **Feature:** ruined ducal lookout | **Uses:** campsite, evidence of organization

> Waist-high stones outline a square tower long since stripped of timber. From inside the foundation, the land falls away on three sides and every approach crosses broken rock.

## Run this area

- The ruin predates the current Keep garrison. Its weathered ducal survey mark is genuine; this is not a Black Eagle position.
- Brynja can identify a stable corner, an old drain, and one section of recently reset stone. The concealed niche holds spare red-and-black cord, cheap iron loops, two axe wedges, and a lump of tallow.
- Danica sees a supply cache, not valuables. Nothing identifies its owner, but the cord matches fibers found elsewhere.
- Leontes recognizes regularly spaced heel marks where six to ten human-sized figures formed two files before departing northwest and northeast.
- This is the best fixed campsite in the woods. A prepared watch can see an undead detail assemble below after moonrise and then move toward [area 16](16-moonlit-kill-ground.md).
- The old walls give cover and prevent attack from more than two sides at once. Do not make the position perfectly safe if the party displays a bright fire.

## Exits

- [Go south to the Abandoned Forester's Spur](03-abandoned-foresters-spur.md) — Follow the old work road downhill.
- [Go southwest to Blackwater Rill](05-blackwater-rill.md) — Descend along the ruined drain.
- [Go northwest to Carrion Hollow](11-carrion-hollow.md) — Follow the western file of tracks.
- [Go northeast to the Bone-White Clearing](13-bone-white-clearing.md) — Follow the eastern file of tracks.
