# 1. Roadside Verge

**Zone:** southern edge | **Access:** open from the road | **Primary use:** begin or end exploration

> Wagon ruts and hoofprints score the road behind you. Ahead, two narrow traces enter the trees. Birdsong follows you for the first dozen paces, then stops as cleanly as if you had crossed a wall.

## Run this area

- Nothing is waiting in ambush. Let the sudden boundary of the silence be the encounter.
- The quiet is stronger northward. Returning to the verge restores ordinary roadside insects and birds within minutes.
- *Detect Magic* finds no forest-wide spell. The effect is behavioral and ecological, not an illusion or magical silence.
- Sereniel or Stelian notices that the last alarm calls point **out of** the woods rather than following the party inward.
- A careful search finds many small tracks coming south and almost none going north.

## Exits

- [Go northwest into the Quiet Pine Margin](02-quiet-pine-margin.md) — Follow the softer path under close-grown pines.
- [Go northeast along the Abandoned Forester's Spur](03-abandoned-foresters-spur.md) — Take the old two-rut work track.
- **Leave the northern woods** — Step back onto the road; exploration ends.
