# 3. Abandoned Forester's Spur

**Zone:** outer woods | **Feature:** disused work road | **Clue:** lawful activity stopped in fear

> Twin ruts end beside three marked pines. A rusted wedge remains driven into a felled trunk, and a collapsed brush shelter still holds blackened firestones. Whoever worked here expected to return.

## Run this area

- The camp was used by Riverfork woodcutters or foresters, not bandits: timber marks are regular, rubbish was buried, and the road points openly toward the Keep.
- Danica can reconstruct an abrupt departure. Tools too cheap to invite murder were still worth retrieving; fear, not robbery, ended the work.
- Scratched into a shelter pole are groups of night-counts. The last three are followed by a crude walking corpse: a frightened worker's record, not a cult symbol.
- Stelian finds no fight and no blood. Several people ran for the road in boots, carrying loads badly.
- A carefully wrapped scrap of red-and-black thread caught on the wedge does not match the foresters' plain gear. Alone, it proves very little.
