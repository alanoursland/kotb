# 16. Moonlit Kill Ground

**Zone:** northern woods | **Encounter:** undead harvest detail | **Best time:** night

> The trees open around a narrow meadow pinched between thorn and stone. Churned earth forms a funnel through the grass. By daylight it is merely wrong. Under moonlight, branches begin clacking in the woods on both sides—and frightened animals run toward the waiting dead.

## Daylight

- Stelian recognizes a game drive: beaters move through the trees and force animals into the narrow meadow.
- Leontes sees a planned killing ground with blockers at the north end and sweepers on both flanks.
- Old blood, axe marks, and animal tracks end here, but no butchering station, fire, cart rut, or meat cache exists.
- The party can prepare an observation point, barrier, ambush, or animal escape lane before dark.

## First night encounter

The first time the party deliberately searches the northern woods after full dark, bring them here after one or two transitions.

- **Four skeleton beaters:** AC 7, HD 1, hp 3 each, clubs or swords 1d6, ML 12.
- **Two zombie killers:** AC 8, HD 2, hp 8 each, battle axes 1d8, ML 12; always lose initiative as normal zombies.
- The skeletons clack branches and advance in a line. The zombies wait at the funnel and kill whatever emerges. None harvests a carcass.
- Unless commanded otherwise, the undead attack witnesses, then retreat toward [area 17](17-shrineward-escarpment.md) if turned.
- Each zombie wears a red-and-black cord with a plain iron loop. The cords protect them weakly: turn them as ghouls. The skeletons turn normally.
- If the detail is destroyed, tracks and equipment still lead toward area 17. If followed, the undead return there before dawn.

## Concordant Staff

The Concordant Staff has **no effect on undead**. It cannot break this attack, translate the patrol's orders, or question the skeletons and zombies. Demetrios knows this limitation and warns the party rather than invoking the staff. If the company chooses battle, he withdraws with the relic; the player clerics' turning, a prepared ambush, or quietly following the patrol are the intended approaches.

## Exits

- [Go southwest to the Ravine Overlook](12-ravine-overlook.md) — Follow the ridge toward a view of the Caves.
- [Go south to the Bone-White Clearing](13-bone-white-clearing.md) — Follow the beaters' approach route.
- [Go southeast to the Foxless Den](15-foxless-den.md) — Follow the eastern sweep line.
- [Go east to the Shrineward Escarpment](17-shrineward-escarpment.md) — Follow retreating undead or their deeply worn trail.
- **Leave the northern woods** — Break contact and withdraw south to the road.
