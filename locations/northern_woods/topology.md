## Investigation routes

- **Fastest daylight evidence:** 1 → 2 → 4 → 6 → 11 → 13.
- **Best observation route:** 1 → 3 → 9 → 13 → 16 → 17.
- **View the Caves without entering:** 1 → 2 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 12.
- **Hermit search:** gather two clues at 7, 10, or 14; then unlock 18.
- **Tail the undead:** any night encounter → 16 → 17 → [Shrine Entrance](../caves/k-entry-passage.md).

## Exit rule

From any numbered area, the party can turn south, use its own back trail, and regain the road. Allow roughly one hour from the outer woods (1–10) or two hours from the deep woods (11–18), adjusted for darkness, pursuit, or weather.
