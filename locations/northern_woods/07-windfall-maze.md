# 7. Windfall Maze

**Zone:** deep western woods | **Feature:** tangled fallen trees | **Uses:** chokepoint, ambush, optional hermit trace

> Storm-felled trunks lie across one another in a waist-high maze. Something has cut two narrow ways through, one broad enough for a stiff-legged walker and one so subtle it vanishes whenever you look straight at it.

## Run this area

- Alexios or Leontes can identify the eastern gap as the best place to observe or block a patrol. A web, barrier, missile line, or prepared retreat would control it.
- The broad route carries the same dragging and exposed-bone tracks seen at Blackwater Rill.
- The subtle route is unrelated: a barefoot human carefully uses logs and roots to conceal his passage. Mountain-lion prints sometimes parallel it from above.
- If the party is **not looking for the hermit**, describe the subtle signs and let the route disappear. It does not lead to an encounter by accident.
- If the party is actively searching and has found one other hermit clue, Stelian can follow this route to the [Hidden Hollow Oak](18-hidden-hollow-oak.md). Another careful tracker makes a Wisdom check.

## Exits

- [Go southeast to the Unharvested Buck](06-unharvested-buck.md) — Follow broken brush out of the deadfall.
- [Go southwest to the Empty Salt Lick](08-empty-salt-lick.md) — Take the older hoof path around the western trunks.
- [Go east to Carrion Hollow](11-carrion-hollow.md) — Follow the stiff-legged passage and growing smell.
- [Go northeast to the Ravine Overlook](12-ravine-overlook.md) — Climb along the highest fallen trunks toward open sky.
- [Search for the Hidden Hollow Oak](18-hidden-hollow-oak.md) — **Only** after the hermit route has been unlocked.
- **Leave the northern woods** — Backtrack south through the windfall and regain the road.
