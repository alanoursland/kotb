# 11. Black Widow Web

> Webbing shutters the hollow from trunk to trunk. A dark body shifts above the path, legs unfolding farther than seems possible. Beneath a drift of leaves, a round shield rim and one white finger show through the mold.

## Encounter

Two black widow spiders defend the web and attack anything entangled.

| Creature | AC | HD | hp | Attack | Move | Save | ML |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| Black widow spider | 6 | 3* | 11 | bite, 2–12 + poison | 20' (40' in web) | F2 | 8 |
| Black widow spider | 6 | 3* | 10 | bite, 2–12 + poison | 20' (40' in web) | F2 | 8 |

Characters who did not heed [area 10](10-spiderwood-edge.md) test for surprise normally. A warned party sees the first spider before entering the densest web.

## The Concordant Staff

The spiders are animals and possess no form of language the staff can translate. The Concordant Staff **does not affect them**: it cannot break their attack, make them listen, or turn their instinctive movements and web vibrations into speech.

Demetrios recognizes the limitation immediately. The party must avoid, drive off, or fight the spiders normally.

## The victim

The skeleton is a Callarii elf, dead for years. Clothing and ordinary equipment have rotted. A filthy shield beneath the body appears worthless; cleaning and oiling reveals a **shield +1**.

Sereniel recognizes old Callarii tack holes and a river-knot carved inside the grip. Returning the remains to Callarii kin may matter more than the shield.
