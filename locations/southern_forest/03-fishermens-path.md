# 3. Fishermen's Path

> The path runs between knee-high sedge and old willow stumps. A pole has been laid across the worst mud, and small hooks cut into the bark mark safe footing. The river shows in silver pieces through the trees.

## At a glance

This is Niko Voss's ordinary fishing route. If he has described it, the party recognizes his landmarks without a roll. Niko himself can guide them here, though Maren and Hadrian strongly prefer that he remain at the Keep.

## Discoveries

- **Automatic:** Old boot prints, line cuttings, fish scales, and a concealed dry box establish regular human use.
- **Reconstructing Maren's route:** The landmarks in her account lead along the safe path to [area 4](04-split-willow-channels.md). Niko's smaller, older prints turned off at a side channel and returned by way of [area 9](09-netmakers-lagoon.md); mother and son passed through at different times and both later returned safely to the Keep.
- **Tavin:** The catch here is good. The southern forest is not suffering the north's collapse in game.
- **Search the dry box:** Waxed twine, two bone floats, and a note scratched by Niko—`deep pool past split willow`—confirm why he kept going.

Nothing here suggests an abduction. Niko followed fish; Maren followed Niko; their routes missed each other.
