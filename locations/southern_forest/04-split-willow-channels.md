# 4. Split-Willow Channels

> A split willow stands above three braided channels. A strip of faded blue wool has been knotted high on one branch. Beyond it, the footing becomes uncertain and the reeds shoulder out the sky.

## At a glance

**Maren is not missing. She returned safely and is living at home in Riverfork Keep.** She described this willow to the party and explained that she tied the blue wool here while searching for Niko. This is the point where she left the channels familiar to the Voss family and unknowingly entered lizardfolk territory.

The party is reconstructing a completed incident, not searching for Maren.

## Discoveries

- **Automatic:** Signs of Maren's passage continue southeast. Her old trail pauses and doubles back because she was calling for Niko and checking side channels.
- **Tracker:** Niko never crossed this point. His path looped south from [area 3](03-fishermens-path.md) to [area 9](09-netmakers-lagoon.md) and returned home by another channel. Mother and son simply missed one another.
- **Lizardfolk trace:** A three-toed print crosses Maren's old path but does not pursue it. A second appears farther on, moving parallel. The lizardfolk observed her before collecting the toll and directing her out of the marsh.
- **Careful search:** A loop of reed braided around a white shell hangs low over the southeast channel. It is the first territorial marker, placed for marsh-level eyes rather than human ones.
