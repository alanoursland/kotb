# 2. Forking Forest Track

> A broad alder leans over a patch of firm ground where three faint ways divide. One follows higher roots toward the river. One disappears under pale web-strands. The third looks like no path at all until a boot turns up the dark side of a fallen leaf.

## At a glance

This is the practical hub of the northern half of the location. Let the party choose by what they are seeking, not by compass trivia.

- The riverward route is used by fishers.
- The web-marked route enters dangerous woodland.
- The concealed western trace belongs to the bandits.

## Discoveries

- **Stelian or Sereniel:** The concealed route is maintained by a dozen or fewer humans who vary their steps. It is not a game trail.
- **Alexios:** The three paths curve around the same wet basin and may reconnect deeper south.
- **Listen:** A wooden clapper may sound once from the west. It is a bandit signal at [area 13](13-alder-sentry-blind.md), not a lizardfolk instrument.
- **At night, while bandits remain:** A distant challenge and brief clash may be heard west or south—the bandits intercepting something that does not answer.
