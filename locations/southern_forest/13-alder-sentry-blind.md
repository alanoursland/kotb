# 13. Alder Sentry Blind

> A fallen alder has been hollowed beneath its roots. From the road it is only storm damage; from behind it, a narrow bench commands two approaches through slits of woven brush. A cord disappears into the leaves at ankle height.

## At a glance

One bandit lookout watches here by day; a second relieves them near dusk. Use **Garrow** or one of the sisters, Wren or Pell. They are alert, frightened, and more interested in warning the camp than fighting alone.

## The warning system

The ankle cord runs to a wooden clapper farther west. Cutting it silently is possible. Stepping over it avoids this trigger but not the hidden watcher.

- **Stelian or Sereniel:** Finds the cord and blind automatically while deliberately scouting.
- **Leontes:** Recognizes overlapping observation lanes and predicts the lookout's withdrawal route toward [area 16](16-smugglers-transfer-bank.md).
- **Brynja:** Can jam the clapper or cut the line without sounding it.

If the lookout sees a superior force, they withdraw and signal. If captured and treated decently, Garrow names Aldric but begs the party not to deliver anyone to the Baron. Wren or Pell says only that the other sister is at camp.

## State change

Mark the bandits **Alerted** if:

- the clapper sounds;
- the lookout escapes;
- the blind is openly searched and abandoned; or
- the party leaves obvious tracks toward the camp.

An alerted band begins moving immediately. The party can still reach it today. By the next visit it is **Gone**.
