# 12. Robbed Caravan Turnout

> The road widens around the blackened ring of an old cookfire. One wheel-rut leaves the gravel and ends beside a stump scarred by an axe. In the ditch, rain has exposed a snapped buckle and three pale grains of spilled barley.

## At a glance

This is the cleanest starting point for the bandit investigation. A small caravan was stopped here weeks ago. The bandits took food, blankets, cord, and one pack animal; they did not burn the wagons or kill everyone.

## Reconstructing the raid

- **Automatic after examination:** The caravan halted in the road rather than crashing. Goods were sorted beside the stump.
- **Danica:** Cuts on straps show selective theft. Dense luxury cargo was left while food and survival gear were taken.
- **Leontes:** Four people showed themselves; at least two more covered from the woods. The command position was west of the turnout.
- **Stelian:** Old traffic leads through [area 14](14-bandit-hunting-ground.md). A subtler path climbs toward a lookout at [area 13](13-alder-sentry-blind.md).
- **Search the ditch:** The buckle bears an old Black Eagle device filed nearly smooth. It belonged to Barony military equipment, but one buckle does not prove who now wears it.

If the bandits are **Gone**, fresh wagon crews have begun using the turnout again. After the undead increase, teamsters report figures standing motionless beyond their fires.
