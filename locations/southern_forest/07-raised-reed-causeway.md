# 7. Raised Reed Causeway

> Beneath an inch of brown water lies a deliberate road: bundles of reed, brush, and river stone laid between deeper pools. Narrow canoe cuts cross it at intervals. The lizardfolk move along it without looking down.

## At a glance

This is controlled access to Sessrik's settlement. Characters escorted from the [Tribute Pool](06-tribute-pool.md) are safe. Anyone reaching it uninvited is observed before approaching the mound.

## Discoveries

- **Brynja:** The causeway is maintained communal infrastructure, renewed in sections as the marsh shifts. This is settled country, not a monster trail.
- **Tavin:** Fish traps, eel baskets, and harvested reeds show abundance and planned use. The lizardfolk are not starving and have no reason to raid the road for food.
- **Alexios:** The visible path is only one branch of a larger water-road system. An invading force would become lost and strung out while the residents moved freely by canoe.
- **During a peaceful escort:** A young lizardfolk carries a cracked clay pot with both arms. An elder painstakingly splices frayed cord. The desired trade goods should be obvious before Sessrik names them.

## Conduct

The wardens expect visitors to remain on the raised way, keep weapons lowered, and avoid touching traps or boats. A mistake brings a warning. Theft or leaving the causeway after warning turns the escort back.
