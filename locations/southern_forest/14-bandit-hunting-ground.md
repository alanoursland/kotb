# 14. Bandit Hunting Ground

> Low brush has been cut into narrow shooting lanes around a clearing of tough grass. A skinning frame stands empty beside cold ashes. One snare holds only a twist of gray hair where something tore free.

## At a glance

The bandits hunt here because the game north of the road has vanished. Even this southern ground is producing less than it did. Their camp discipline is visible before their identities are.

## Discoveries

- **Tavin:** The frame has been scrubbed and every usable scrap taken. These people waste nothing.
- **Stelian:** A dozen humans use the clearing in shifts. Two are noticeably lighter-footed; one older walker favors the left knee.
- **Leontes:** The shooting lanes also form a fallback position. Aldric has arranged a place where a small force can disengage rather than stand and die.
- **Search the ashes:** A charred human finger bone bears old necromantic discoloration. It came from a destroyed undead body, not the bandits' cooking pot.
- **Observe unseen:** Mother Coll or Brennan may collect traps while two armed escorts watch. They speak about low stores and “another pale walker at the ford.” This is a chance to understand the camp before judging it.

If approached openly, the bandits warn the party away and retreat. Pursuit alerts the camp. A promise to speak with “the Captain” gets a suspicious escort if reaction is favorable or the Concordant Staff has broken the confrontation.
