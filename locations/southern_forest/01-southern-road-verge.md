# 1. Southern Road Verge

> The road rides a low gravel back above wet woods. To the south, alder and willow replace the northern pines. Frogs call from hidden water, midges dance in the light, and fresh deer slots cross the ditch. This forest is wary, but it is alive.

## At a glance

This is the normal entry and exit. The contrast with the [Northern Woods](../northern_woods/index.md) is immediate: animals still live here because Aldric's band has been destroying undead patrols that cross south of the road.

A weathered fingerpost points west toward Riverfork Keep and east toward the rough approach to the Caves. There is no official sign south.

## Discoveries

- **Automatic:** Boot prints leave the road at more than one point. Some are lone fishermen; others belong to groups that deliberately step on roots and stones.
- **Tracker:** Stelian separates Niko and Maren Voss's older route toward [area 3](03-fishermens-path.md) from a rotating armed watch toward [area 12](12-robbed-caravan-turnout.md).
- **Military eye:** Leontes notices one patch of road can be observed from the woods without exposing the observer. Someone watches traffic rather than wandering at random.
- **After the bandits are gone:** Within 1d3 nights, fresh, dragging human-shaped tracks cross from north to south. The living animal sign begins to thin.

## Exits

- [Follow a narrow riverside path southeast toward the fishermen's fork](02-forking-forest-track.md).
- [Go west along the road to the old caravan turnout](12-robbed-caravan-turnout.md).
- **Return to the road and leave the southern forest.**
