# 15. Broken Undead Patrol

> Bones lie scattered across wet leaves, each long bone cracked with the same deliberate stroke. A swollen corpse in rotten homespun has been pinned facedown beneath a forked branch and beheaded. No grave has been dug.

## At a glance

While the bandits remain, this is evidence of their unrecognized service to the region: they destroy undead entering the southern woods. The remains are **three skeletons and one zombie**, already inert.

## Discoveries

- **Sorina:** Necromancy animated all four. They were destroyed within the last ten days and came from north or northeast.
- **Irina or Marcus:** The treatment is practical, not devotional. Whoever did this knew enough to break joints, separate skulls, and watch for renewed movement.
- **Leontes:** The ambush used a rope trip, two heavy clubs, and an axe. At least six living fighters attacked together under command.
- **Danica:** A scrap of sackcloth tied to the zombie's wrist carries the same cheap black dye found in Shrine-related evidence, but no complete symbol.
- **Stelian:** Small goblin and kobold tracks approach later, inspect the destruction, and turn away from both the bandit route and the marsh.

## After the bandits leave

After **1d3 nights**, replace the inert scene with a moving patrol: **four skeletons and two zombies**. They are probing south from the cult's sphere and use this clearing as a rally point.

The Concordant Staff has no effect on them. Turned undead retreat north through [Spiderwood Edge](10-spiderwood-edge.md), not toward the marsh.

Destroying one patrol does not stop the increase. It demonstrates the work the bandits had been doing repeatedly.
