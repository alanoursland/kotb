# 6. Tribute Pool

> The channel ends in a round pool beneath leaning willows. Ripples touch the bank from three directions at once. Then scaled heads rise among the reeds and stone-tipped spears settle toward the company—not thrusting, but leaving no unguarded way back.

## At a glance

This is the first formal encounter with Sessrik's people. Use **three lizardfolk border wardens** unless violence escalates; more are close enough to answer a warning call.

They have not set an ambush for prey. They are collecting customs from people who crossed a marked border.

## The demand

Without a shared language, a warden points back to the boundary, then to the party's worked goods, and holds out an open hand. Their terms are:

> **You crossed held water. Give a useful thing, or leave.**

Ordinary tribute is **one meaningful worked item from the traveling party**, not one item per person: a coil of rope, metal knife, bundle of hooks, sealed jug, good cloth, salt, or equivalent. Once paid, the party may pass for the present visit.

If the party explains that it has come to discuss **Maren's necklace**, the warden reclassifies them as prospective traders and escorts them inward. **No tribute is owed for this visit once they agree to negotiate trade.**

## The Concordant Staff

The staff affects the living lizardfolk. If a misunderstanding becomes an attack, activation stops everyone within 30 feet for one full turn and lets them understand one another. Demetrios may ask one question each round while everyone else converses freely; continued concentration can keep the parley open indefinitely. The wardens listen because the staff compels a pause, not agreement, answers, or honesty.

Good questions for Demetrios:

- **“Why did you take the necklace?”** — “She crossed held water. She paid and left living.”
- **“What do you want from us?”** — “Name your purpose. Guest, trader, or trespasser.”
- **“Will you return it?”** — “A finished payment is not returned. A possession may be traded.”

They do not lie, but neither do they explain customs nobody asks about.

## If violence begins

The wardens withdraw through water while defending themselves and sound wooden clappers. Do not have the entire settlement charge into a slaughter. Mark the relationship **Hostile**; passage to the mound now requires capture, pursuit, or later reparations.
