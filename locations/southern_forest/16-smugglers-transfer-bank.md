# 16. Smuggler's Transfer Bank

> The river bends against a shelf of tamped gravel. Two sawn posts stand at wagon height, and a third lies half-submerged beside a channel deep enough for a narrow boat. Old wheel ruts arrive from the west and end here.

## At a glance

This was one transfer point on the Black Eagle Barony's concealed northern route. Cargo came by cart, then continued by shallow boat or on pack frames through wet country where ordinary patrols could not follow.

Aldric's band uses it as an escape route and water source but did not build it.

## Evidence

- **Brynja:** The posts supported a simple lifting spar. Their height and wear fit heavy, awkward cargo—including a barred transport crate—rather than fishermen's baskets.
- **Danica:** A surviving cargo tally uses Barony quartermaster abbreviations. One line reads `B—live / 1 / north transfer`, with no species named.
- **Search underwater:** A broken chain staple is sized to secure a large cage. Khaeros recognizes its forge pattern if shown.
- **Search the bank:** Two gold-washed lead **Eagles** lie in the mud. One has lost enough wash to show black lead beneath. They are Barony scrip, not ducal coin.
- **Stelian:** The oldest route continues west beyond this location; current bandit traffic divides between [area 13](13-alder-sentry-blind.md) and [area 17](17-aldric-camp.md).

Taken alone, this proves clandestine transport linked to the Barony. Combined with Aldric's account, the camp papers, and Khaeros, it reconstructs one segment of the trafficking route.

## If the camp is alerted

The bandits move their most incriminating gear here first. If the party delays, a small boat has already carried people and supplies downstream. Do not use the boat to stage a second encounter; the bandits leave the campaign.
