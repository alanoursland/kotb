# 17. Aldric's Camp

> Twelve bedrolls lie beneath patched lean-tos around a smokeless cooking trench. Spears stand within reach, bows rest beneath waxed cloth, and every pack is tied for sudden travel. A gray-haired woman guards a stewpot while a scarred man in an old soldier's coat rises slowly from a map scratched into the earth.

## At a glance

This is the camp of [Aldric Venn](../../campaign/the-bandit-camp.md#the-leader--aldric-venn-the-captain) and eleven other outcasts. It is defensive, hungry, and ready to disappear. They are criminals with blood on their hands, not a death cult and not a fight to extinction.

Each carries 3d6 sp; Hesk also has 1d6 gp; Aldric has 2d6 gp. Camp stores include a cask of wine, arrows, bedrolls, poor rations, and cleaned game. Reassign exact weapons freely when characterization matters more than the old B2 array.

## First contact

If unaware, Aldric challenges the party from cover and demands they lower weapons and leave. If alerted, packs are already moving toward [area 16](16-smugglers-transfer-bank.md); Hesk may assume the party are Baron's hunters and force an attack.

The Concordant Staff affects the living bandits within 30 feet. When the close line stops, Aldric orders Wren and Pell—usually beyond the aura—not to fire through their companions. This creates the full turn of dialogue the staff promises, and Demetrios can maintain the pause while conversation continues. He may ask one question per round; everyone else converses freely. The staff does not compel answers, honesty, surrender, or trust in the Duke.

**Mother Coll** is the easiest route to peace. **Korsk** is the likeliest to break it. **Leontes** can recognize Aldric's deliberate escape lanes; **Marcus** can frame terms as an honorable withdrawal rather than humiliation.

## Morale and surrender

Check morale:

- when the first bandit falls;
- when Aldric is incapacitated or visibly abandons the fight; and
- when half the band can no longer fight.

A failed group check means **surrender**, not flight and not a fight to the last. Aldric fights until his own morale fails first, then orders every survivor to put down arms. Individual sentries may have escaped before the battle; those present obey him.

## What Aldric tells them

If conversation succeeds—or after surrender if he is promised his people will not be slaughtered—Aldric gives a soldier's account:

- He served the Black Eagle Barony as a sergeant-at-arms.
- He, Hesk, and Tomil moved a captive minotaur north along a covert route. Khaeros escaped near Riverfork and killed men during the break.
- Cargo landed under Barony protection, traveled by cart, and changed conveyance at sites like [area 16](16-smugglers-transfer-bank.md).
- Handlers used plain iron rings or a ring-shaped mark as authentication. Aldric knew it meant authority close to the Baron, though he was never sworn into the organization.
- The Barony pays servants in Eagles and accepts them back through controlled traders. He knows the scrip is nearly worthless outside that circuit.
- Captives were called “acquisitions” or “live loads.” He saw enough to know Khaeros was not unique.
- The band occasionally crossed goblin or kobold scouts. Both sides usually displayed weapons and withdrew; none of the living groups wanted a second war in these woods.

He does not know the Shrine's plan, Vesna, the Hierophant, or the bugbear station's full operation.

## Why he will not testify

Aldric refuses to return to Riverfork, sign a confession, or appear before Stefan.

> “The Duke left his cousin a coast, soldiers, seals, and years. You call that ignorance because you need him innocent. I call it permission. And I drove the cart. His law hangs me before my words reach Specularum.”

Stefan is not secretly allied with Ludwig, but Aldric's inference is understandable: ducal inaction enabled the Baron's crimes. Aldric also knows his own conduct was illegal under ducal law. No offer of routine amnesty overcomes both fears.

Danica may record his account, and the party can repeat it, but Aldric will not become a formal witness. Physical evidence must carry the case where his presence cannot.

## Resolution

- **Released after parley or surrender:** Aldric promises to leave and never return. The band is gone by dawn and does not recur.
- **Detected but not confronted:** They identify the breach in their screen and evacuate by the next visit.
- **Captured and forced toward the Keep:** They attempt escape when a safe opportunity appears. Aldric does not voluntarily cooperate merely because he was defeated.
- **Killed:** Their local suppression of undead ends immediately.

In every version where the camp ceases to operate, mark **Gone**. After 1d3 nights, increase undead activity at [area 15](15-broken-undead-patrol.md), this camp, and [area 18](18-old-river-ford.md).

## Physical evidence

Searchers find:

- a filed Black Eagle badge and pieces of old Barony livery;
- several worn Eagles, including one with black lead exposed;
- a ring-shaped authentication mark on a scrap carried by the Quiet One;
- a fragmentary order referring to `the beast`, `north transfer`, and a failed delivery;
- a rough route sketch joining the western approach, the transfer bank, and a northern handoff.

If the bandits fled, most supplies are gone, but the badge, scorched order fragment, and one Eagle remain. Preserve the clue path even though Aldric's account is lost.

## After the bandits leave

The empty shelters feel safer for one night. Then the dead begin using them. On or after the third night, a patrol of **four skeletons and two zombies** may stand motionless beneath the lean-tos before moving toward the road. The Concordant Staff does not affect them.
