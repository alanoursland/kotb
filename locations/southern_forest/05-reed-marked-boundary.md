# 5. Reed-Marked Boundary

> The channel widens into black, still water. Bundles of cut reed stand at intervals along the opposite bank, each bound with cord and capped by a pale clay bead. No fence blocks the way. Everything beyond them has been tended.

## At a glance

This is a border post. Humans have failed to recognize it because it uses marsh materials and faces the water rather than the road.

## Reading the boundary

- **Any deliberate examination:** The markers repeat at regular intervals and are repaired. This is not debris.
- **Stelian, Sereniel, or Alexios:** Narrow canoe channels converge on the marked line but do not casually cross it. The inhabitants are controlling movement.
- **Tavin:** The cord is precious here and has been reused until nearly worn through. Good cordage would be a meaningful gift.
- **Brynja:** The clay beads are fired but coarse. Sealed, durable jugs would be superior to anything made locally.
- **Draconic or kobold connection:** The repeated notch means approximately *held water / our water*. It is territorial, not a threat of sacrifice.

Crossing without announcing the party causes two hidden lizardfolk to shadow them toward [area 6](06-tribute-pool.md). Calling out, displaying an offered gift, or waiting respectfully brings the same meeting without surprise.
