# 8. Sessrik's Mound

> A low island rises above the reeds, its sides strengthened with woven mats and driven stakes. Smoke escapes through covered vents. Canoes lie upside down above the waterline, children watch from a burrow mouth, and six armed adults place themselves between the visitors and every entrance.

## At a glance

This is the B2 lizardfolk mound recast as the center of a small marsh polity. It is a home with guarded burrows, nests, stores, and work areas—not a lair waiting to be cleared.

**Leader:** [Sessrik, the Marsh Matriarch](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#lizardfolk-leader--sessrik-the-marsh-matriarch)

## Population and statistics

- **Seven adult males:** AC 5, HD 2+1; hp 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, 7, 5; damage 2–7; Save F2; ML 12.
- **Three adult females:** AC 5, HD 2+1; hp 8, 6, 6; attack as 1+1 HD; damage 2–7; ML 12. Sessrik is the 8-hp adult unless a stronger bespoke profile is needed.
- **Eight young and six eggs:** noncombatants. The adults defend them and their escape routes.

Only three border wardens normally meet visitors. The rest remain dispersed through the mound and fishery.

## The necklace

The largest border warden wears Maren Voss's 1,100-gp necklace as a token of a completed toll. He removes it without reluctance when Sessrik agrees to trade it; jewelry has no sacred meaning here.

Sessrik's opening position is:

- six 50-foot coils of sound cordage;
- four weighted fishing nets; and
- twelve lidded clay or ceramic jugs.

She will accept reasonable substitutions in durable worked goods. She will not “return stolen property,” because by lizardfolk law the toll was lawfully completed. She will **trade a possession** and recognize the party as traders.

Once terms are agreed, the party may leave without paying tribute. Mark **Trade promised**. On delivery, Sessrik returns the necklace, marks the party **Recognized traders**, and identifies [Netmaker's Lagoon](09-netmakers-lagoon.md) as the regular exchange point.

## What Sessrik explains

- The marsh is held land. The boundary markers are sufficient to anyone taught to read them.
- Guests announce themselves and bring gifts. Unannounced trespassers owe tribute.
- Traders on agreed business do not pay tribute; exchange itself is the relationship.
- Maren was frightened but unharmed. She paid one assessed toll and was directed out.
- Her people know small scaled cave-dwellers at a distance but have no regular alliance with them.
- Night-dead rarely enter deeply. Water and dispersed food make the marsh hard to strip, and armed humans in the western woods destroy many that come south.

Sessrik can be friendly, but she is not deferential. She is negotiating with another polity, not asking permission to exist.

## Treasure and restraint

Beneath a guarded nest are 112 cp, 186 sp, a 90-gp gold ingot, a potion of healing, and a poison potion. These are household reserves. Include them for fidelity to B2, not as an invitation to rob a nursery.
