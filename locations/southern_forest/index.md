# Southern Forest and Northern Fens

**Location:** south of the road near Riverfork Keep | **Mode:** loose exploration network | **Areas:** 18

> South of the road, alder roots knot the black earth and every low hollow holds water. The woods are not quiet. Frogs call from the fens, insects worry the reeds, and somewhere beyond sight a wooden clapper knocks twice and stops.

These pages treat the southern forest and the northern edge of Blight Swamp like a text adventure, not a measured wilderness map. The links describe recognizable routes rather than exact bearings. Moving to an adjacent area usually takes **20–40 minutes** while exploring. The company can abandon the search and recover the road at any time; do not require them to retrace the entire network merely to leave.

## What is happening

Three communities overlap here without sharing the land.

- **Sessrik's lizardfolk** live well in the marsh. They regard its pools, reed beds, and channels as sovereign territory. Visitors who announce themselves and bring useful gifts are guests; traders are traders; people who cross the marked water without either are trespassers and owe tribute.
- **Aldric Venn's bandits** hide in the western forest. They rob to survive, keep military watches, and destroy the cult's undead when those patrols enter the south. They are criminals, but they are not committed to dying as criminals.
- **The Shrine's undead** test the forest at night. The bandits have kept their numbers low here. Once the bandits leave—or are killed—the patrols spread into the southern woods within a few nights.

The lizardfolk and bandits know the other group exists and ordinarily avoid one another. Small goblin and kobold parties also leave tracks at the margins, but the living have learned not to crowd one another's paths.

## Location state

Record only these two tracks.

### Lizardfolk standing

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Unknown** | The party has not established terms. Entering claimed water brings a demand for tribute. |
| **Trade promised** | Sessrik has accepted the necklace bargain. The party may depart without tribute and return with the agreed goods. |
| **Recognized traders** | The goods were delivered and Maren's necklace returned. The party may use the agreed route without tribute while conducting trade. Courtesy gifts remain wise. |
| **Hostile** | The party attacked, stole from the mound, or broke its word. Passage is resisted until reparations are negotiated. |

### Bandit state

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Unaware** | The camp does not know the party has found its southern screen. |
| **Alerted** | A sentry escaped, a warning line was disturbed, or the party left unmistakable signs. The camp begins evacuating. |
| **Confronted** | The party has fought or spoken with Aldric. If allowed to leave, the band departs and never returns. |
| **Gone** | The camp is abandoned. After **1d3 nights**, increase undead activity at areas 15, 17, and 18. |

If the party locates the perimeter but never confronts the bandits, mark them **Alerted**. Unless the party reaches [Aldric's Camp](17-aldric-camp.md) that same day, mark them **Gone** by the next visit. They find evidence but lose Aldric's account.

## Likely scenarios

### Recover Maren Voss's necklace

Maren is safely home at Riverfork Keep and can describe what happened. Niko Voss can place the party on the [Fishermen's Path](03-fishermens-path.md). The company reconstructs Maren's completed journey at the [Split-Willow Channels](04-split-willow-channels.md), after which the route crosses the [Reed-Marked Boundary](05-reed-marked-boundary.md) and reaches the lizardfolk at the [Tribute Pool](06-tribute-pool.md).

The lizardfolk demand tribute for trespass, not surrender. The [Concordant Staff](../../characters/father-demetrios-kallistos.md#the-concordant-staff) works on them and can stop the first attack long enough to translate the demand. Demetrios may ask one question each round while everyone else converses freely; the staff neither compels an answer nor makes it truthful. If the party says they have come to negotiate for the necklace, they become prospective **traders**, not trespassers, and no tribute is charged for that visit.

Sessrik's clean bargain is:

- **six 50-foot coils of sound cordage**;
- **four weighted fishing nets** suitable for channel fishing; and
- **twelve lidded clay or ceramic jugs** that can be carried in a canoe.

The party may negotiate quantities, substitute similarly useful worked goods, or arrange delivery through the Keep. Sessrik returns the necklace only when the trade goods arrive. Once the exchange is honored, mark the party **Recognized traders**. This is a favorable bargain for both sides: humans value the heirloom as jewelry, while the lizardfolk value durable tools they cannot readily make.

### Find the bandit camp

Start with the [Robbed Caravan Turnout](12-robbed-caravan-turnout.md). Tracks and military observation positions lead through the [Alder Sentry Blind](13-alder-sentry-blind.md), [Bandit Hunting Ground](14-bandit-hunting-ground.md), and [Smuggler's Transfer Bank](16-smugglers-transfer-bank.md) to [Aldric's Camp](17-aldric-camp.md).

The Concordant Staff works on the living bandits. At the camp it stops the close fighters for one turn; Aldric orders the two outer bowmen to hold fire rather than shoot through their own people. Demetrios can maintain the pause while conversation continues.

A fight is still likely if the party demands unconditional surrender, Korsk forces the issue, or Hesk panics. The bandits do **not** fight to the last. Check morale after the first bandit falls, when Aldric is incapacitated, and when half the band can no longer fight. A failed group check means surrender; Aldric orders every surviving bandit to put down arms.

If talk succeeds, Aldric tells the party what he knows about Khaeros's transport, the Baron's concealed route, the transfer bank, and the handlers' iron-ring signs. He will not return to Riverfork or testify before the Duke. He believes Stefan's years of inaction toward his cousin amount to complicity, and he knows his own service to the Baron included crimes under ducal law. He will not place himself in ducal custody.

If released, Aldric promises the band will leave and never return. He keeps that promise. The party will not meet these bandits again.

### Follow the Baron's route

The [Smuggler's Transfer Bank](16-smugglers-transfer-bank.md) is where cart loads became boat or pack loads. A Black Eagle badge, worn Eagles, cargo marks, and a fragmentary order at the camp can be assembled into a physical case even without Aldric. Danica and Brynja are particularly useful here.

### Watch the woods at night

While the bandits remain, the party finds evidence of undead destroyed at the [Broken Undead Patrol](15-broken-undead-patrol.md) but encounters few active dead. Once the bandits are gone, fresh undead begin crossing the [Old River Ford](18-old-river-ford.md), occupying their empty camp, and moving north toward the road. The Concordant Staff does not affect undead.

### Fast table version

For a 45–60 minute necklace sequence:

1. Use [area 3](03-fishermens-path.md) and [area 4](04-split-willow-channels.md) to reconstruct Maren's completed journey.
2. Let a tracker recognize the border at [area 5](05-reed-marked-boundary.md).
3. Run the demand and translation at [area 6](06-tribute-pool.md).
4. Conduct terms with Sessrik at [area 8](08-sessriks-mound.md), then montage the return with trade goods.

For a 45–60 minute bandit sequence:

1. Establish the raiders' discipline at [area 12](12-robbed-caravan-turnout.md).
2. Let the party evade or capture the lookout at [area 13](13-alder-sentry-blind.md).
3. Show the destroyed undead at [area 15](15-broken-undead-patrol.md).
4. Run the confrontation at [area 17](17-aldric-camp.md), then resolve departure and the delayed undead consequence.

## Character spotlights

- **Stelian:** identifies Maren's route, reads the lizardfolk's boundary as intentional, and follows bandit patrol rotations without tripping their screen.
- **Sereniel:** scouts the alder canopy, detects the black-widow web before entering it, and recognizes that the dead elf was Callarii.
- **Tavin:** understands why nets, cordage, and sealed jugs are more useful than jewelry; can organize the return cargo and frame the first durable trade.
- **Danica:** reconstructs the caravan ambush, sorts Barony evidence from ordinary bandit gear, and records Aldric's account even though he will not testify.
- **Sorina:** distinguishes destroyed undead from ordinary corpses and notices the south's necromantic residue is recent and intermittent.
- **Irina:** recognizes the lizardfolk encounter as a boundary custom rather than worship or sacrifice; she and Marcus can turn the later undead patrols.
- **Marcus:** keeps a tense surrender orderly and can offer honorable terms without pretending the crimes did not happen.
- **Leontes:** reads Aldric's perimeter as a soldier's defense, predicts morale breaks, and can keep the party from turning surrender into a massacre.
- **Brynja:** identifies the transfer bank's improvised cargo works and estimates what kinds of loads moved through it.
- **Alexios:** maps the channels, separates real paths from flood traces, and identifies how the forest, ford, and river route fit together.
- **Father Demetrios:** can use the Concordant Staff to begin dialogue with language-using peoples such as the lizardfolk and bandits. It cannot affect the animal spiders or the undead.
