# 10. Spiderwood Edge

> The alders ahead grow too close together. Pale ropes join branch to branch above the ground, each thick as fishing line. No flies turn in the shafts of light beneath them, and a shed deerskin hangs six feet up without touching a thorn.

## At a glance

This is the warning perimeter of the black widows' hunting web. A careful party need not blunder into [area 11](11-black-widow-web.md).

## Discoveries

- **Automatic if moving cautiously:** These are webs, not mist or ordinary cobwebs.
- **Sereniel or Stelian:** Two large predators use overlapping lanes. The safest retreat is back along the roots; hacking the first strands alerts them.
- **Alexios:** The webbed hollow blocks the direct line between Maren's route and the deeper western forest, but a southern ford goes around it.
- **Search the margins:** Human boot prints stop, turn, and continue west. The bandits know the lair and use it as a natural barrier.
- **Lizardfolk sign:** A small reed warning bundle is tied on the wet side. Sessrik's people avoid this patch rather than trying to clear it.

Fire can destroy webbing but risks a spreading woodland fire in dry weather and produces smoke visible from the road and bandit sentries.
