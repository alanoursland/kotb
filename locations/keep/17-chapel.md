# 17. Chapel

**Zone:** northern outer bailey | **Access:** public worship; private cellar | **Primary use:** Church of Traladar, healing, counsel, and spiritual legitimacy

> A steep-roofed chapel stands opposite the Guild House. Bare wooden pews face an eastern altar beneath a tall colored-glass window showing three stars above a crowned warrior, a shield-bearing queen, and a cloaked huntsman. Three shallow prayer niches honor Halav, Petra, and Zirchev. The room is simple, old, and unmistakably cared for.

## What players encounter

- One large worship hall fills the building.
- An offering box stands on a heavy pedestal in the southeast corner.
- A concealed stair behind the pews descends to the Curate's and three acolytes' quarters.
- The chapel is the Keep's Church of Traladar, not merely a generic military shrine.

## The faith in brief

The Church of Traladar teaches that goodness, courage, and communal duty preserved the Traldar people through catastrophe and will one day restore something of their lost Golden Age. Its central sacred narrative is **The Song of King Halav**, the story of Halav, Petra, and Zirchev rallying the people against the Beastmen and giving everything to save them.

The Church is concerned less with abstract theology than with **how people behave**. Its ordinary teachings are direct:

- Do not wrong another person or damage what is theirs.
- Repent wrongdoing, make restitution where possible, and accept justice proportionate to the harm done.
- Protect the home, the community, and the Traladaran people.
- Resist evil in its many forms; when warning and endurance are not enough, resistance may require force.
- Magic is not inherently suspect. Zirchev's place beside Halav makes wise magic, folk practices, and magical learning part of the accepted tradition.
- Remember the dead, care for the ill, preserve old customs, and teach the next generation who they are.

Local practice varies considerably. Villages and families emphasize different stories, feast days, and aspects of the Immortals, while still recognizing the authority of the High Patriarch in Mirros on major questions of doctrine. Branislav follows the broad parish tradition and venerates all three patrons rather than belonging to one of their specialized orders.

## The Three

| Immortal | Place in the faith | Common aspects invoked here |
|---|---|---|
| **Halav** | King, champion, lawgiver, and defender who united the Traldar against the Beastmen. Invoked for courage, just judgment, leadership, and the strength to confront a necessary danger. | Beast-Slayer, the Eternal Champion, the Just, Divine Judge |
| **Petra** | Warrior-queen, defender of the people, healer, and guardian of hearth and community. Invoked for protection, healing, endurance, family, and the defense of those who cannot defend themselves. | Defender of the People, Sacred Healer, Hearth-Maiden, the Fair Warrior |
| **Zirchev** | Huntsman, counselor, magician, and watcher of the wild places who brought warning of the coming invasion. Invoked for foresight, woodland travel, good counsel, magic, and seeing through deception. | Mageborn, the Wild Huntsman, Elf-Friend, Unerring Councillor |

The three prayer niches contain modest frontier images rather than costly icons: Halav with a notched sword, Petra behind a round shield sheltering a family, and Zirchev carrying a bow beneath carved leaves. Worshippers often address a particular aspect suited to the immediate need without regarding it as a separate Immortal.

## Worship and community life

- The principal service is held weekly. It includes readings or recitations from *The Song of King Halav*, prayers for the ill and the dead, and a formal rite of confession, repentance, and forgiveness.
- The chapel remains open at all hours. A few soldiers, travelers, or residents can usually be found praying quietly even when no service is underway.
- Branislav and the acolytes conduct funerals, blessings, healing, counsel, children's instruction, and traditional rites such as the **Shearing**, the Traladaran passage into adulthood.
- The congregation marks **Beasts' Day** and the sacrifice that ended the Beastman invasion, the **Feast of the Three Stars** honoring the patrons' ascent to Immortality, **Queen's Day** in Petra's honor, and the solemn **Days of Darkness** preceding Beasts' Day.
- The chapel is both religious and cultural ground. Even Traladarans who attend irregularly may come here for a funeral, a family rite, an old song, or counsel from someone who remembers customs the ducal institutions do not.

## The Curate

- The most influential person in the Keep after the Castellan.
- Grim, careful, and protective of the congregation rather than eager to impress travelers.
- Usually carries a ring of protection and a snake staff; armor appears only when the Keep is threatened.
- Prepared spells: *cure light wounds, detect magic, bless,* and *hold person*.
- Healing normally goes first to members of the congregation. Trust can widen that circle.
- Questioned closely by a friend, may reveal distrust of [Father Ariston](../../characters/father-ariston-vale.md), the visiting priest in [area 7](07-private-apartments.md).

## Campaign function

- The Curate can recognize the Shrine's symbols as alien to Traladaran faith.
- The Curate's presence gives a cleansing of the Shrine native legitimacy rather than the appearance of ducal religious suppression.
- The chapel is a political counterweight to the fortress: moral memory opposite military command.
- The Shrine's doctrine fails the Church's most basic tests: it harms the innocent, rejects proportionate justice, exploits grief, and turns defense of the people into their destruction. Branislav can explain that without needing to identify Thanatos immediately.
- Halav, Petra, and Zirchev are remembered here as protectors who stood against annihilation. This is why Branislav recognizes the Shrine's fixation on death and ruin as something alien wearing Traladaran language.

## DM-only: hidden treasury

Beneath the offering-box pedestal is a secret compartment found only by moving the pedestal. It has two locks; the Curate and Castellan hold the only keys. It contains three potions of healing, one potion of ESP, one potion of gaseous form, and a scroll with *cure disease, hold person,* and three *cure light wounds* spells.
