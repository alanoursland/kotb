# 25. Fortress Towers

**Zone:** fortress roofline | **Access:** garrison only | **Primary use:** protection of the fortress corners

> Two square towers rise forty feet at the outer corners of the fortress. Battlements and arrow slits cover the northern approaches and the wall walks leading toward the Keep's larger towers.

## Run this area

- Each tower houses fortress soldiers and supports the central tower garrisons.
- Arrow slits protect the eastern and western corners of the main building.
- These are defensive posts with little reason for an unescorted visitor to enter.
- During an alarm, soldiers use the towers to keep attackers away from the fortress roof and upper chambers.
