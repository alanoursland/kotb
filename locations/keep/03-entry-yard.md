# 3. Entry Yard

**Zone:** outer bailey entrance | **Access:** public but supervised | **Primary use:** registration, dismounting, and routing visitors

> The gate opens into a narrow paved yard crowded by soldiers, stable hands, and a writing desk. A board of painted arrows points west toward lodging and food, southwest toward shops and banking, and north toward the stable.

## Run this area

- All nongarrison visitors dismount here.
- A scribe records every person entering or leaving.
- Stable hands take mounts to the [Common Stable](04-common-stable.md); unattended cargo goes to the [Common Warehouse](05-common-warehouse.md).
- First-time travelers are shown toward the [Travelers Inn](14-travelers-inn.md).
- Returning player characters are recognized, logged, and escorted north through the inner gate for their debrief before they are released into the hub.

## People here

- The corporal of the watch: plate, shield, magic sword; grouchy, unimpressed by status, but responsive to direct courage.
- One scribe at the entry ledger.
- Two plate-armored soldiers with pole arms.
- Stable hands arrive when animals or baggage need moving.

## Signposts

- **Lodging, meals, fountain:** west.
- **Provisioner, trader, smith, bank:** southwest through the outer bailey.
- **Guild House and chapel:** northwest.
- **Command and inner bailey:** north, by permit or escort.

## Exits

- [Leave through the Main Gate](01-main-gate.md) - Go east through the guarded gate passage.
- [Stable mounts](04-common-stable.md) - Go north to the long stable against the east wall.
- [Store bulky goods](05-common-warehouse.md) - Go southwest to the chained warehouse doors.
- [Follow the lodging sign to the Travelers Inn](14-travelers-inn.md) - Go west across the public outer bailey.
- [Follow the food-and-drink sign to the Tavern](15-tavern.md) - Go west, past the fountain, to the busy public room.
- [Report to the Keep Fortress](24-keep-fortress.md) - Go north under escort through the [Inner Gatehouse](18-inner-gatehouse.md).

## DM-only

This is the Keep's procedural heartbeat. Use the ledger to establish who came and went, the corporal to make authority personal, and the signposts to let players navigate without asking an NPC for every ordinary destination.
