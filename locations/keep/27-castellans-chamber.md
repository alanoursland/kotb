# 27. Castellan's Chamber

**Zone:** upper fortress | **Access:** private invitation | **Primary use:** confidential command conversations and residence

> The commander's private chamber rises above the main roof behind battlements. Heavy furnishings, a silver mirror, a malachite bowl, and a fox robe make it comfortable without softening the fact that this is the room of a frontier soldier.

## Run this area

- Routine reports and audiences occur downstairs in the [Keep Fortress](24-keep-fortress.md), not here.
- Use this chamber when the Castellan wants privacy: politically dangerous testimony, a request involving the Baron, the chapel treasury, employment of a cave-dweller, or terms that cannot yet be announced to the garrison.
- The Castellan answers honest questions as fully as security allows.
- Bravery and honesty are absolute; cleverness is real, but decisions can be hasty.

## Campaign use

- Track the Castellan's trust through results reported over the campaign.
- A successful peaceful mission does not “win” the Castellan at once, but it changes the next private conversation.
- The strongest arguments are operational: safer road, dependable patrols, fewer dead, accountable supplies, and ground the Duke need not garrison.
- The Castellan holds one of the two keys to the [Chapel](17-chapel.md) treasury.

## Statistics and possessions

Fighter 6, AC -3, hp 48, Dexterity 16, sword +2, dagger +1, plate +1, shield +1, ring of protection +1, elven cloak and boots, potion of levitation, and potion of healing. The chamber contains valuable art objects, coin, gems, and a spear +1.

## Exits

- [Return to the Keep Fortress](24-keep-fortress.md) - Descend to the great hall and public command rooms.
- [Go through the Central Towers](26-central-towers.md) - Pass the staff offices and guarded upper barracks.
- [Request the Chapel](17-chapel.md) - Descend through the fortress and cross the inner and outer baileys under escort.

## DM-only

Do not use this room merely because the Castellan is present. Moving a scene upstairs tells the players that the matter is personal, secret, or dangerous to say aloud.
