# 10. Trader

**Zone:** southern shop row | **Access:** public | **Primary use:** weapons, armor, bulk goods, and purchasing valuables in kind

> This deeper shop smells of oiled leather, wool, salt, spice, and wood shavings. Weapons and armor share space with cloth bolts, rare timber, trade bundles, and samples from larger stock kept elsewhere.

## Run this area

- Sells armor, weapons, and bulk goods at standard prices.
- Buys ordinary goods from adventurers at 50% of listed value.
- Pays full stated value for furs when the seller insists.
- The trader and two adult children can arm themselves with leather, shields, pole arms, and swords if needed.
- Bulky purchases such as cloth, furniture, a wagon, or a large food order are brokered through local craftspeople, the [Guild House](16-guild-house.md), and the [Warehouse](05-common-warehouse.md).

## Campaign use

- This is a natural stop during a peaceful cave-dweller's first shopping trip.
- A customer buying a bed, clothing, provisions, and a wagon demonstrates demand the Keep has never served before.
- Refusal, nervous commerce, and genuine welcome can all appear here among different merchants without turning the whole Keep into one attitude.

## DM-only

The three residents are normal people with hp 2, 3, and 3. Hidden beneath their apartment floorboards are 500 gp and 1,110 sp. Each carries 2d6 gp.

## Exits

- [Go east to the Provisioner](09-provisioner.md) - Step next door for smaller expedition gear.
- [Go south to the Loan Bank](11-loan-bank.md) - Follow the guarded path toward money-changing and credit.
- [Go northwest to Fountain Square](13-fountain-square.md) - Leave the shops for the public crossroads.
- [Arrange bulk trade at the Guild House](16-guild-house.md) - Go northeast to the guild post.
