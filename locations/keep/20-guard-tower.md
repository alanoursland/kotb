# 20. Guard Tower

**Zone:** western inner wall | **Access:** garrison only | **Primary use:** barracks, supplies, and western wall defense

> A fifty-foot tower rises above the western cliff and the wall between the baileys. Its upper floor is crowded with weapons, oil, and food stores; the lower floors are working barracks.

## Run this area

- Twenty-four guards are quartered here, organized like the force at the [Inner Gatehouse](18-inner-gatehouse.md).
- A corporal commands the post.
- Supplies on the upper floor support an extended defense without leaving the tower.
- The tower watches the cliff, western outer bailey, and inner wall approach.

## Command

The corporal has AC 0, Fighter 1, hp 9, sword, dagger +1, and morale 11. The tower does not handle routine visitor business; it responds to alarms and military orders.

## Exits

- [Go east along the wall to the Inner Gatehouse](18-inner-gatehouse.md) - Follow the guarded battlement passage.
- [Enter the Inner Bailey](21-inner-bailey.md) - Descend the north-side stair; military access only.
- [Look south toward the Tavern quarter](15-tavern.md) - Use the outer-wall positions; public access is below and reached through the gatehouse.
