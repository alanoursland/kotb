# 16. Guild House

**Zone:** northeastern outer bailey | **Access:** public office; hospitality by status | **Primary use:** Merchants' Guild dues, permissions, contracts, protection, and trade coordination

> The two-story Guild House looks less like a shop than a small administrative post. Clerks watch traffic, guards watch visitors, and ledgers turn every wagon and craft into a fee, permission, or obligation.

This is a frontier office of the **Merchants' Guild of Karameikos**, the powerful realm-wide organization headed by **Anton Radu** of the Traladarian Radu clan. The local Guild Master is **Mihail Radu**, a distant kinsman and trusted factor rather than an independent clan lord.

## Run this area

- Traveling guild members may receive lodging and hospitality.
- Traders pay dues equal to 5% of their merchandise's value and receive Guild protection.
- Craftspeople need Guild permission to enter or leave the region, paying 2d6 gp according to the value of their trade.
- Guild protection includes contracts, convoy intelligence, access to guarded caravans, and recourse through the Guild's substantial private security force.
- Gems, jewelry, ingots, and questionable precious-metal goods are referred to **[Hadrian Voss](07-private-apartments.md#7a--voss-jewel-household)** for specialist appraisal before the Guild records a value or makes an offer.
- The lower floor holds the Guild Master's quarters, two clerks' rooms, and the office.
- The upper floor has two private guest rooms and a dormitory.
- Four loyal men-at-arms are always on duty, two on each floor.

## Campaign use

This is the practical engine of a future caves-Keep market. The Guild can:

- contract wagons and drivers;
- aggregate bulk food or materials from regular ducal and private convoys;
- establish prices, inspection, and payment;
- object that cave trade evades dues or threatens members;
- recognize that safer roads and new labor create profit.

A temporary experiment can be approved locally. A permanent trade compact with the caves creates a new class of customer and caravan destination inside territory the Guild considers its commercial sphere; Mihail will report that precedent to Anton Radu and seek terms that preserve Guild dues and control.

**Hadrian Voss is the office's outside expert, not its master.** The Guild pays him to authenticate gems and precious metal offered by travelers, but he is also permitted to trade on his own account. His frontier advantage is information: he sees valuable finds before merchants in Specularum, Kelvin, Darokin, or Thyatis know they exist. Mihail values his judgment and watches his private purchases closely.

The food bridge is possible because the Keep is supplied. The conflict is who authorizes the extension, who pays, and whether the Guild treats the caves as customers, contractors, or enemies receiving tribute.

## The wider Guild

- Anton Radu's organization operates caravan and shipping networks linking Karameikos to foreign markets.
- Its guards protect member shipments along dangerous roads; the four retainers here are the local edge of that larger private force.
- Guild interests extend beyond shops into northern logging, mining, bulk transport, and finance.
- The Guild has enough wealth and organization to influence policy alongside the Church and the great noble houses. Mihail cannot dictate to Valerian, but he can make commerce easy, expensive, delayed, or politically visible.

## Local influence

Mihail Radu is socially powerful because his office reaches far beyond the Keep. Favor improves treatment across the commercial post and can open protected caravan capacity. Hostility produces inspections, unfavorable scheduling, higher costs, reports to Specularum, and closed doors without requiring open lawbreaking.

## DM-only

Mihail carries a gold Guild ring and coin; a strongbox under his bed holds 712 gp. He and the clerks are normal people but keep chain, crossbows, and swords in a closet. The four guards are AC 6, Fighter 1, hp 6 each, morale 8. Their first loyalty is to the Merchants' Guild and its Radu leadership, not to the Keep's military chain of command.
