# 8. Smithy and Armorer

**Zone:** southeastern shop quarter | **Access:** public | **Primary use:** repairs, shoeing, and custom metalwork

> Heat and hammer-noise spill from a twenty-foot stone shop with a defensive parapet. The ground floor is crowded with forge, bellows, racks, half-finished work, and the sharp smell of hot iron.

## Run this area

- The smith shoes horses and mules, makes weapons, and repairs armor.
- Two assistants keep routine work moving while the smith handles difficult jobs and customers.
- Finished stock on hand: 2 swords, 1 mace, 1 suit of man-sized chain mail, and 11 spears.
- The family and assistants live upstairs.
- Custom work is possible when time and materials permit. Oversized fittings, wagon hardware, and unusual commissions become small public events rather than impossible requests.

## At the table

- Quote a repair time and collect the damaged item.
- Let the smith assess unfamiliar metalwork or identify whether damage came from neglect, battle, or sabotage.
- The forge is one of the first places a curious outsider may stop simply to watch skilled work.

## If trouble starts

- Smith: AC 7, Fighter 1, hp 11, hammer for 1-6, morale 8.
- Two assistants: AC 8, hp 5 each; they seize nearby tools or weapons.
- Fire, hot metal, frightened animals, and the open forge complicate any fight.
