# 6. Bailiff's Tower

**Zone:** southeastern wall | **Access:** official business | **Primary use:** outer-bailey administration and enforcement

> A forty-foot tower closes the southern end of the east wall. Arrow slits cover every approach. The lower floor is an office; the upper levels are quarters, stores, and crowded barracks.

## Run this area

- The bailiff supervises the outer bailey and shares the ground-floor office with a scribe.
- Bring disputes over warehouse access, market conduct, guild permission, property, and public order here.
- The bailiff is heavily armed, personally formidable, and accustomed to being obeyed.
- Twelve soldiers occupy the fourth floor: six with leather, shields, pole arms, and hand axes; six with chain, crossbows, and swords.

The bailiff and the office scribe are civilians for roster purposes; the twelve soldiers report through their sergeant to Captain Bohdan.

## What the office can decide

- Release or impound stored goods.
- Enforce Guild dues and movement permissions.
- Assign an escort or public-order detail.
- Refer military or diplomatic questions upward to the Castellan.

## DM-only details

- Bailiff: AC 1, Fighter 3, hp 22, sword +1 or plate +1, longbow available, morale 12.
- The scribe owns an ink pot worth 100 gp that looks worthless beneath dried ink.
- Fifty gold pieces and three arrows +1 are hidden among the bailiff's personal effects.
- The soldiers need no encouragement to defend the tower, but they do not abandon their post for a tavern quarrel unless ordered.
