# 1. Main Gate

**Zone:** outer gate | **Access:** public but controlled | **Primary use:** arrival, departure, and first security check

> A drawbridge crosses a deep crevice to a granite gatehouse flanked by two battlemented towers. Beyond the portcullis, a narrow murder-hole passage ends at a second pair of gates. Crossbows remain trained on the road even when the bridge is down.

## Run this area

- Two plate-armored wardens approach with pole arms before anyone enters.
- Visitors must sheathe weapons. The wardens escort them through the passage rather than waving them in.
- The party's ducal authority is known here. It speeds the procedure; it does not remove it.
- On every return from the borderland, word is sent ahead and the party is routed toward a command debrief.
- A peaceful cave-dweller stops the gate cold. Spears lower, an officer is summoned, and the party must explicitly accept responsibility before admission is considered.

## People here

- Two gate wardens in plate mail: AC 3, Fighter 1, hp 5 each, pole arms, morale 10.
- Crossbow crews watch from both [flanking towers](02-flanking-towers.md).
- During an unusual arrival, the captain or corporal of the watch reaches the gate within minutes.

## Questions the guards ask

- Who are you, and under whose authority do you travel?
- What are you bringing into the Keep?
- Are any travelers wounded, cursed, captive, or dangerous?
- Will the party answer for an unconventional guest's conduct?

## Exits

- [Enter the Entry Yard](03-entry-yard.md) - Go west through the murder-hole passage under armed escort.
- [Leave for the borderland](../../campaign/the-borderland.md) - Go east across the drawbridge and descend the road from the Keep.
- [Climb to the Flanking Towers](02-flanking-towers.md) - Take the guarded stair up; garrison access only.

## DM-only

The gate should feel formidable, not hostile. Its soldiers are correctly cautious. The campaign's thesis becomes visible here whenever the party asks the Keep to distinguish a person from a feared body.
