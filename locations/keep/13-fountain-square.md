# 13. Fountain Square

**Zone:** western outer bailey | **Access:** public | **Primary use:** orientation, meetings, and open-air commerce

> A broad fountain splashes at the center of the Keep's public crossroads. Travelers fill skins, residents trade news, soldiers cut across between watches, and painted signs point toward beds, meals, shops, the chapel, and the gate.

## Run this area

- The fountain is the easiest neutral meeting place in the Keep.
- On holidays, farmers and tradespeople set up booths around the square.
- On ordinary days, the square still draws messengers, children, off-duty soldiers, servants, and anyone waiting for somebody else.
- A conspicuous outsider is seen here by almost everyone. Reactions can range from fear and refusal to curiosity and eager commerce.

## Signposts

- **Tavern:** north.
- **Travelers Inn:** east.
- **Trader, provisioner, smith:** southeast.
- **Loan bank:** south.
- **Guild House and chapel:** northeast.
- **Main gate:** east across the bailey.

## Campaign use

Run public demonstrations here: the first peaceful cave-dweller crossing the Keep, the market watching an ogre control justified anger, a returned expedition announcing success, or merchants arguing over a new trade route. The square lets private choices acquire witnesses.
