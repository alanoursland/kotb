# 24. Keep Fortress

**Zone:** northern inner bailey | **Access:** audience, duty, invitation, or custody | **Primary use:** command, debriefs, administration, and fortress residence

> The fortress rises in tiers behind a solid iron door. Round towers flank a lower front, battlements crown every level, and armored guards control a great hall furnished for command rather than spectacle.

## Run this area

- The lower floor contains the great hall, cavalry armory, and side chambers for meetings and meals.
- The Castellan and assistants conduct daily business and hold audiences here.
- Eight plate-armored crossbowmen watch the walls; eight more guards with plate and shield stand inside.
- The cellars contain ducal provisions, servants' quarters, a cistern, and four stout dungeon cells.
- The second floor houses heavy and medium cavalry, two couriers, and two special guest rooms.

## The debrief loop

Every return from the borderland comes here first.

1. The party reports what happened, including deaths, prisoners, agreements, and new threats.
2. The Castellan tests claims against practical results: safer roads, fewer raids, useful intelligence, or new risks.
3. Command offers reasonable support: supplies, information, limited troops, chapel access, or a mission.
4. The party is released to rest, resupply, and decide what to do next.

This is not an interrogation. It is how a competent frontier commander learns what is happening beyond the walls.

## Supply and politics

The Grand Duchy supplies the Keep regularly and well. The fortress cellars and warehouse network can support a food bridge to the caves, but doing so requires authority, accounting, convoy capacity, and a political decision to provision former raiders. No garrison starvation is required.

## Likely scenes

- First presentation of the Duke's letter.
- Reports after each ravine expedition.
- Requests to admit, shelter, employ, or trade with a cave-dweller.
- Assignment of the lizardfolk, bandit, hermit, and road-security missions.
- Open local issue: Maren Voss reports that known but poorly understood swamp lizard people took one of her necklaces while she searched for her overdue son; Valerian wants the property recovered and the threat assessed.
- Negotiation of the eventual food bridge as payroll for patrol and frontier service.

## DM-only

The special guest rooms provide a controlled alternative when an unusual visitor cannot safely or comfortably use the public inn. This lets the Keep be hospitable without pretending security concerns vanish.
