# 19. Small Tower

**Zone:** eastern inner wall | **Access:** garrison only | **Primary use:** inner-wall overwatch

> A compact tower anchors the eastern end of the wall dividing the two baileys. Two crossbowmen watch from the top while the remainder wait in the chamber below.

## Run this area

- Eight chain-armored guards are stationed here.
- Two remain on the tower top at all times; six occupy the chamber below.
- All carry crossbows and swords. Shields stored below improve AC from 5 to 4 in hand-to-hand combat.
- The base is solid except for the narrow stair, making the post difficult to rush.

## Response

The tower covers the [Entry Yard](03-entry-yard.md), eastern outer bailey, and approach to the [Inner Gatehouse](18-inner-gatehouse.md). An alarm brings aimed crossbows first and reinforcements second.

## Exits

- [Go west along the wall to the Inner Gatehouse](18-inner-gatehouse.md) - Follow the guarded battlement passage.
- [Enter the Inner Bailey](21-inner-bailey.md) - Descend the north-side stair; garrison or escorted access only.
- [Look south toward the Chapel](17-chapel.md) - Use the outer-bailey firing positions; there is no public entrance from the tower.
