# Garrison of Riverfork Keep

> **At a glance:** 223 uniformed personnel. This is a well-paid, regularly supplied ducal garrison, not a starving levy. The soldiers protect a frontier rest stop, keep the road open, escort supply traffic, and provide the Grand Duchy's armed presence in the district.

Use this as the master personnel sheet when the party asks who is on a post, requests an escort, raises an alarm, or begins to know the rank and file. **Current job** means the soldier's ordinary alert-state assignment; watches rotate, so move names between equivalent files when the fiction requires it.

### Count reconciliation

The original area descriptions sometimes name troops where they are housed and again where they stand duty. This roster treats the sixteen fortress guards in areas 24 and 25 as **one unit** housed in the corner towers, not thirty-two different soldiers. It treats the command staff separately from the troops they command. Civilian workers are also separate.

## Military hierarchy

```text
Castellan Valerian — commander of the Keep
└── Captain Bohdan “the Wall” — deputy; captain of the guard
    ├── Captain of the Watch — gate, outer towers, outer-bailey patrols
    │   └── Tower/section sergeants → corporals → men-at-arms
    ├── Master Sergeant — inner guard and armory readiness
    │   └── Post/section sergeants → senior corporals/corporals → men-at-arms
    ├── Lieutenant of Horse — stable guard and mounted troop
    │   └── Mounted sergeants → corporals → troopers and couriers
    ├── Lieutenant-Scribe — adjutant, records, correspondence, chaplain duties
    └── Staff Advisor — intelligence and arcane/woodland counsel
```

- **Castellan** is a ducal appointment and the final military and civil authority inside the Keep.
- **Captain of the Guard** is the operating deputy and commands all routine security. Bohdan can move soldiers between posts without waiting for Valerian.
- **Captain of the Watch** runs the outer defensive system. The title describes a post, not a second independent company.
- **Lieutenants** are commissioned specialists. The Lieutenant of Horse commands mounted operations; the Lieutenant-Scribe turns Valerian's decisions into orders, rolls, reports, and correspondence.
- **Master Sergeant** is the senior enlisted soldier. Sergeants run detachments and sections; senior corporals run large posts; corporals lead watches, files, and small posts.
- Some twenty-four-person tower forces are locally commanded by a senior corporal. That is deliberate frontier practice: the captain and master sergeant are close at hand, while scarce officers are kept free for mobile and Keep-wide duties.

The Thyatian majority reflects the Grand Duchy's officer culture and recruitment network; the substantial Traladaran minority reflects local enlistment. Mixed-heritage soldiers are ordinary in a ducal institution that has now operated in Karameikos for a generation. The Callarii, Vyalia, and Highforge dwarves are Karameikan peoples, not recent recruits from Alfheim or Rockhome. Other foreigners generally arrived through trade, specialized military skills, or the promise of recognized ducal employment.

**Ironvein** and **Flintvein** are household bynames within the larger **Stronghollow clan**. Both dwarves grew up in predominantly gnomish Highforge and are familiar with its mixed dwarven-and-gnomish craft traditions and trade ties.

## Duty state and support staff

- On an ordinary day, staffed gates and battlements stay manned while off-watch soldiers eat, sleep, drill, clean equipment, haul routine military stores, or take recreation in the outer bailey.
- The mounted troop sends short road patrols daily and meets important supply convoys before they reach the Keep. A serious alarm recalls riders and resting tower files.
- The garrison eats regular ducal issue supplemented by purchases from local merchants. Supply work is routine and professional, not desperate.
- Not counted here: the bailiff, entry scribe, stable hands, farriers, smiths, warehouse laborers, servants, civilian clergy, inn staff, merchants, or adventurers. They support the post but are not soldiers.
- [Khaeros](../../characters/khaeros-the-minotaur.md), if he later chooses Valerian's probationary offer, serves under Bohdan and is **not** included in this baseline roster. His Writ of Peace and Passage does not depend on accepting the post; his likely first contract is guarding a Guild caravan to Specularum.
