# 7. Private Apartments

**Zone:** southern outer bailey | **Access:** residents and invited guests | **Primary use:** private housing and two important visitors

> A row of doors and upper windows faces the outer bailey. Most apartments are modest family homes. Two larger suites are conspicuously better furnished and watched more carefully than the rest.

## Run this area

- Five small apartments house families connected to the outer bailey's shops and offices.
- The two large suites are occupied by a jewel merchant's household and a visiting priest with two silent acolytes.
- These are homes, not public shops. Knocking, invitations, and the hour of day matter.

## 7a. Jewel merchant

- Buys gems for 60%-90% of value and sells for 110%-140%.
- The merchant's spouse wears valuable jewelry that is also for sale.
- Two Fighter 2 guards in chain and shield protect the household; each controls a trained war dog.
- The household is waiting for a caravan back toward more settled lands.

**DM-only:** An iron box holds 200 pp and 100 gp. Ten 100-gp gems are concealed in the merchant's belt. The guards have hp 17 and 12; the dogs have AC 6, HD 3, hp 12 and 11.

## 7b. Visiting priest

- Jovial, generous with drink, an excellent listener, and loudly opposed to evil.
- Offers to accompany expeditions to the Caves.
- The two robust acolytes never speak; their silence is presented as a religious vow.
- The chapel's Curate distrusts this visitor, while the Curate's own acolytes admire him.

**DM-only campaign truth:** The visitor and acolytes are enemies operating inside the Keep. The visiting priest is a witting foreign agent of Thanatos, separate from the Iron Ring and the Shrine's local cult. He joins an expedition to sabotage it at a crucial moment. Do not signal this through cartoon villainy; his charm and usefulness are the disguise.
