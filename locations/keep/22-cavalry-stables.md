# 22. Cavalry Stables

**Zone:** eastern inner bailey | **Access:** cavalry and authorized visitors | **Primary use:** war horses and mounted readiness

> A long stable runs beneath the northern and eastern walls. Rows of war horses stand ready behind broad doors, with tack organized for speed rather than display.

## Run this area

- Thirty war horses are maintained here, along with 1-4 riding horses.
- Two stable hands tend the animals.
- Two armed guards protect the stable and monitor anyone approaching from the bailey.
- Unlike the public stable, this is a military facility. Animals are assigned, not casually rented.
- The Keep's regular ducal supply keeps feed, replacements, and cavalry equipment dependable.

## Statistics

- War horses: AC 7, HD 3, hp 11 each, two attacks for 1-6/1-6, morale 8.
- Riding horses: AC 7, HD 2, hp 8 each, two attacks for 1-4/1-4, morale 7.
- Guards: AC 4, Fighter 1, hp 4 each, morale 8.
