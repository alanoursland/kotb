# 14. Travelers Inn

**Zone:** western outer bailey | **Access:** public | **Primary use:** lodging, rest, and a stable base inside the walls

> A long, low inn faces the fountain court. Its common room is quieter than the tavern, with travel chests under benches, boots drying near the wall, and a stair to a small family loft.

## Lodging

| Accommodation | Capacity | Price |
|---|---:|---:|
| Private room | Five rooms | 1 gp per night |
| Common sleeping room | Twelve sleepers | 1 sp per night |

Servants sleep in the [Common Stable](04-common-stable.md), not in the inn.

## Run this area

- This is the party's default home inside the Keep unless the Castellan grants fortress quarters.
- Guests can leave ordinary baggage here, exchange road news, and learn who else is staying in the Keep.
- The innkeeper's household occupies a small loft and avoids violence.
- Baths, laundry, barbering, tailoring, furniture, and other comforts are not all performed under this roof, but the inn can arrange them through local households and the Guild.
- An unusually large or feared guest requires advance approval, an escort, and practical improvisation. The Keep has resources; the challenge is permission and social nerve.

## Exits

- [Go west to Fountain Square](13-fountain-square.md) - Step into the public crossroads.
- [Go north to the Tavern](15-tavern.md) - Follow the adjoining passage toward meals and drink.
- [Go southeast to the Provisioner](09-provisioner.md) - Cross the court to buy expedition gear.
- [Go northeast to the Guild House](16-guild-house.md) - Ask for commercial arrangements or specialist services.
- [Return to the Entry Yard](03-entry-yard.md) - Follow the east-pointing gate signs across the bailey.

## DM-only

The innkeeper and family are normal people with no meaningful fighting ability. Their value is continuity: they notice absences, changed equipment, new scars, unusual guests, and whether the party comes back triumphant or silent.
