# 26. Central Towers

**Zone:** upper fortress | **Access:** staff, garrison, and escorted official business | **Primary use:** command staff and reserve troops

> Two central structures rise twenty feet above the fortress roof. Their upper stories are barracks; their lower rooms hold the people who turn the Castellan's decisions into orders, records, and advice.

## Run this area

- Each tower houses twelve off-duty soldiers in its upper two stories.
- In each group, six wear plate and carry crossbows and swords; six use plate, shield, and sword.
- Off-duty troops need one turn to arm and organize.
- The lower floors hold the Castellan's scribe and advisor.

## The scribe

- Cleric 2, AC 2, hp 11, plate, shield, and mace.
- Carries a *hold person* scroll and can cast *light*, including against an opponent's eyes.
- Keeps austere quarters, a gold holy symbol worth 150 gp, and 48 gp.

## The advisor

- Elf 3, AC 0, hp 18, Dexterity 16, plate +1.
- Carries a short bow, ten arrows +1, and a ring of fire resistance.
- Prepared spells: *charm person, read magic,* and *web*.
- The advisor's rooms are richly furnished and visibly more comfortable than the scribe's.

## Exits

- [Descend to the Keep Fortress](24-keep-fortress.md) - Take the internal stair to the audience and meeting rooms.
- [Go inward to the Castellan's Chamber](27-castellans-chamber.md) - Pass through the staff-controlled upper corridor; invitation required.
- [Go outward to the Fortress Towers](25-fortress-towers.md) - Cross the roof passage to the corner defenses.
