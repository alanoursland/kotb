# 2. Flanking Towers

**Zone:** gate defenses | **Access:** garrison only | **Primary use:** overwatch and rapid gate response

> Twin round towers command the drawbridge and entry passage. Crossbows rest on the battlements, shields stand close at hand, and every floor smells of oil, bowstrings, and crowded soldiers.

## Run this area

- Four crossbowmen stand ready atop each tower.
- Four more armed soldiers are on duty inside each tower; eight rest and need one turn to arm themselves.
- Stores include bolts, arrows, spears, rocks, and barrels of oil for repelling an assault.
- Pallets, stools, pegs, and small tables make the towers feel like working barracks rather than empty fortifications.

| Group | AC | hp | Arms | Readiness |

## Exits

- [Descend to the Main Gate](01-main-gate.md) - Take the internal stair down to the gate passage.
- [Look into the Entry Yard](03-entry-yard.md) - Go to the inner firing positions overlooking the paved yard; soldiers do not normally descend this way while on watch.

## DM-only

If violence begins at the gate, these crews make the danger obvious before initiative is rolled. They call warnings, cover the drawbridge, and shoot only when ordered or when an attack is unmistakable.
