# 12. Watch Tower

**Zone:** southwestern wall | **Access:** watch personnel and official visitors | **Primary use:** outer-wall command and bank overwatch

> A forty-five-foot round tower rises where the south wall meets the western cliff. Archers watch both the outer approaches and the bank below; upper embrasures reveal stacked spears, stones, and oil.

## Run this area

- Six chain-armored soldiers carry bows and swords.
- Six leather-armored soldiers carry shields and pole arms.
- One archer is always assigned to watch the [Loan Bank](11-loan-bank.md).
- The watch captain occupies the first floor; the second and third are barracks.
- The upper floor stores 200 arrows, rocks, two barrels of oil, and 24 spears.

## Command

The watch captain is a serious combatant: AC 2, Fighter 3, hp 20, dagger +1 or sword +2, morale 11. The tower responds to alarms in the outer bailey and coordinates wall defense, but routine market order remains the bailiff's responsibility.
