# 9. Provisioner

**Zone:** southern shop row | **Access:** public | **Primary use:** adventuring equipment and consumables

> Shelves, pegs, bins, and ceiling hooks pack this low shop with rope, spikes, lamps, packs, tools, and trail gear. Painted signs direct customers east to the smith and west to the trader for anything this shop does not carry.

## Run this area

- Sells ordinary adventuring equipment at standard prices.
- Weapon stock is limited to spears, daggers, arrows, and bolts.
- Usually has seven shields, but no armor or mounts.
- Buys used equipment at 50% of listed value.
- The proprietor's family lives in a small apartment in the building.
- Regular ducal and caravan traffic keeps common goods available. Very large orders require a Guild arrangement rather than an empty shelf crisis.

## At the table

- Resolve ordinary purchases quickly from the [reference equipment tables](../../reference/reference-tables.md#cost-of-equipment-and-weapons).
- Use the shopkeeper to point players toward the correct specialist instead of saying only “not available.”
- This is where expedition plans become visible through shopping lists: oil and rope suggest one plan; sacks and trade goods suggest another.

## DM-only

The proprietor is a normal person with hp 3. If the walls are threatened, leather and shield give AC 6 and a spear provides 1-6 damage. A strongbox holds 100 gp, 16 ep, and 30 cp.
