# 21. Inner Bailey

**Zone:** inner Keep | **Access:** permit, invitation, or escort | **Primary use:** drill ground and approach to command

> A broad grass-covered court opens beyond the inner gates. Soldiers drill in formation, practice weapons, and work horses through jousting lanes. The fortress rises at the northern end above the open ground.

## Run this area

- During daylight, at least a dozen soldiers are training here.
- The space is open enough that an escorted visitor is visible from the gatehouse, stables, towers, and fortress.
- Crossing the bailey for an audience is routine; stopping to inspect defenses or approach restricted buildings draws immediate attention.
- Demonstrations, musters, and formal arrivals happen here when the event is military rather than public.

## Campaign use

- A returning party crosses under escort before every debrief.
- A prospective recruit from the caves may be tested here under the eyes of the garrison.
- The Castellan can frame cooperation in military terms here: patrol routes, road security, response times, and who holds which ground.
