# 23. Great Tower

**Zone:** northwestern inner wall | **Access:** garrison only | **Primary use:** heavy tower garrison and northern defense

> A sixty-foot round tower dominates the northwestern corner. Its crews can fire along the cliff, across the inner bailey, or toward the fortress approaches.

## Run this area

- Twenty-four guards are quartered here.
- One third carry crossbows, one third bows, and one third pole arms.
- A corporal commands the tower.
- Use the [Inner Gatehouse guard details](18-inner-gatehouse.md) for ordinary statistics and readiness.

## Tactical role

The Great Tower is a strongpoint, not a social location. It locks down the northwestern wall and supports the fortress if the inner bailey is breached. Visitors are challenged before reaching the stair.
