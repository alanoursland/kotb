# The Keep

The Keep is a well-supported ducal frontier post and a safe stopping place for soldiers, merchants, craftspeople, and adventurers. Regular convoys keep its garrison supplied; travelers find beds, meals, stabling, repairs, banking, and a small but vigorous market behind formidable walls.

Use these pages as a text-adventure map. Exit links describe an action and a direction. North is the top of the published Keep map.

> **Campaign rhythm:** On every return, the party enters through the [Main Gate](01-main-gate.md), is logged in the [Entry Yard](03-entry-yard.md), and is escorted to report at the [Keep Fortress](24-keep-fortress.md). After the debrief, the outer bailey is their resupply and social hub.

## Running principles

- The party's letter buys access and cooperation, not unquestioned obedience.
- The Keep is comfortable and regularly supplied by the Grand Duchy. It is a rest area with a military spine, not a settlement under austerity.
- Feeding the caves is a political and logistical expansion of that supply line, not a choice to starve the garrison.
- Peaceful cave-dwellers are unprecedented guests. Admission requires the party's guarantee, an escort, and the Castellan's approval.
- Civilian residents are identified by role in this first pass. The soldiers are named in the [garrison roster](garrison.md).
