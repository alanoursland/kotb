# 18. Inner Gatehouse

**Zone:** divide between outer and inner baileys | **Access:** permit, invitation, or escort | **Primary use:** security checkpoint, armory, and guard command

> The inner gatehouse is a fort inside the fort: iron-bound gates, arrow slits along a rising passage, two tiers of battlements, and guards who stop every unfamiliar face before the slope reaches the grass above.

## Run this area

- Six guards are always on duty: two in the gateway, two on the lower battlement, and two above.
- No visitor passes north without invitation or a special permit.
- The first floor is the Keep's main armory, with dozens of shields and weapons of every ordinary kind.
- The captain and sergeant have sparse rooms here; twenty-four guards occupy the northern second story.
- A returning party headed for debrief is expected and escorted through. Wandering off the route is not tolerated.

## Command staff

- **Captain:** AC 0, Fighter 3, hp 24, sword +2, spear +1; kind, friendly, and an excellent leader.
- **Sergeant:** AC 2, Fighter 2, hp 16, Strength 17, sword +1, dagger +1; hard fighter who enjoys drink and brawling.
- **Guards:** 24 total, divided among crossbows, long bows, and pole arms; AC 4 with shield or 5 while shooting, hp 5 each, morale 10.

## Campaign use

The captain is the party's day-to-day military contact and the likely escort for an unconventional guest. Do not name the role in this pass. Let competence, scale, and changing behavior establish the relationship first.

## Exits

- [Return south to the public outer bailey](13-fountain-square.md) - Descend through the gates and go southwest toward the fountain.
- [Go north into the Inner Bailey](21-inner-bailey.md) - Pass the checkpoint under escort or present a valid permit.
- [Go east along the wall to the Small Tower](19-small-tower.md) - Take the military wall passage; garrison only.
- [Go west along the wall to the Guard Tower](20-guard-tower.md) - Take the military wall passage; garrison only.
- [Proceed to the Keep Fortress](24-keep-fortress.md) - Cross the inner bailey north for an audience or debrief.

## DM-only

The sergeant has a potion of healing hidden beneath a spare cape. The captain sometimes walks the outer bailey disguised as a mercenary to observe the Keep without ceremony.
