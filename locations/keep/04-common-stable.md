# 4. Common Stable

**Zone:** eastern outer bailey | **Access:** public | **Primary use:** stabling mounts and recovering travel gear

> A long, low stable hugs the wall north of the entry yard. Its flat roof is battlemented, its gateward wall is pierced for archery, and its stalls hold a changing collection of horses and mules.

## Run this area

- Five to eight stable hands tend animals and tack at all hours.
- Visitors' servants sleep here rather than at the inn.
- Typical stock on hand: 2-8 light horses, 2-8 draft horses, and 1-4 mules.
- The workers know which patrols returned muddy, which merchants arrived light, and whether anyone left in a hurry. They do not know command secrets.
- Ducal supply traffic keeps fodder and ordinary stable necessities readily available.

Five to eight of these eight workers are present at a time. Milos or Tessa is always the senior hand on duty.

## If trouble starts

Stable hands are unarmored, with 1-4 hp each. They defend animals and one another using pitchforks and other tools as pole arms. Panicked animals, fire, and blocked exits are greater dangers than the workers.

## Useful interactions

- Stable or reclaim a mount.
- Inspect tack, wagons, hoofprints, or an animal's condition.
- Hear mundane arrival-and-departure gossip.
- Arrange shoeing or repair at the nearby [Smithy](08-smithy-and-armorer.md).
