# 64. Lysandra's Cell

**Faction:** Shrine prisoner | **Resident:** [Lysandra](../../characters/lysandra-the-medusa.md) | **Role:** rescue gamble and thesis limit case

> An iron door is locked and barred, with a small viewing window. Skeletons remain chained to the wall. Around a corner, a scantily clad woman's body is visible only to the shoulders.

## Lysandra

- Medusa: AC 8, HD 4, hp 20, bite 1d6 plus poison, ML 8.
- Looking directly at her requires a save or petrification. A successful bite also requires a poison save or death.
- She was recently captured by zombie guards. The cult intended to remove her snakes, blind her, and sacrifice her.

## Run this area

- Lysandra can spare one or two rescuers from her gaze and promise a cure if freed.
- She carries enough *stone-to-flesh elixir* for six people but does not initially intend to surrender it.
- The printed outcome is betrayal. The campaign makes that outcome real but not inevitable: engineered safety, blinds, mirrors, an intermediary, and behavior she can trust may change the choice.
- The polished mirror in [area 60](60-shrine-guest-chamber.md) is an available tool, not an automatic solution.
- Use [Lysandra's full profile](../../characters/lysandra-the-medusa.md) to preserve both danger and personhood.
