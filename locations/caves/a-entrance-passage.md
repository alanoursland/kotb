# Cave A — Trapped Entrance Passage

**Faction:** kobolds | **Access:** watched | **Hazard:** concealed pit 30 feet inside

> A low cave tunnel disappears beneath the eastern wall. Thirty feet in, dust and loose grit conceal a fitted section of floor.

## Run this passage

- Eight kobolds may attack from trees above the mouth: 2-in-6 chance on first entry.
- The 10-foot-deep covered pit catches the front rank on 3-in-6; the second rank on 1-in-6 if crowded behind someone who falls. Falling causes 1d6 damage.
- The lid closes. Prisoners need help from outside. The noise brings occupants from [1](01-kobold-guard-room.md) and [2](02-giant-rat-refuse.md).
- Planks stored in area 1 allow residents and accepted guests to cross safely.

## Exits

- [Go deeper to the Kobold Guard Room](01-kobold-guard-room.md) - Cross the pit and follow the guarded passage.
- [Follow the refuse stink toward the giant rats](02-giant-rat-refuse.md) - Take the side opening near the first junction.
- [Return outside to the Ravine Floor](ravine.md) - Back away from the covered pit and leave the cave.
