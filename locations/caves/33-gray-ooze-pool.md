# 33. Gray Ooze Pool

**Zone:** Shunned Cavern | **Hazard:** three concealed gray oozes | **Treasure:** jeweled goblet

> Damp stone shines around a broad shallow pool where white blind fish move. A jeweled goblet lies beneath the water.

## Run this area

- Three gray oozes, or two if one already wandered into area 32: AC 8, HD 3, hp 15, ML 12.
- First attack from floor level deals 1d8 while destroying foot and leg protection; later attacks deal 2d8 and destroy armor. Attacks from above deal 2d8 immediately.
- One ooze waits at the pool's south edge, one on the southwest ceiling, and the optional third on the ceiling left of the entrance.
- Only a 1-in-20 chance notices one without probing the approach or fully lighting the ceiling with at least two torches.
- The goblet is worth 1,300 gp.

## Exits

- [Return to the Shunned Gallery](32-shunned-gallery.md) - Leave the wet chamber by the carrion-strewn passage.
