# Cave A Population — Yiznak's Kobolds

**Faction:** Yiznak's kobolds | **Population:** 71 kobolds + 18 giant rats | **Leader:** [Yiznak the Deepwright](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#kobold-chief--yiznak-the-deepwright)

> **At a glance:** 63 adults and 8 young, plus the refuse-room rats. Thirty-five adults occupy assigned ambush, guard, or leadership posts; the other 28 adults have combat statistics and can defend the lair. No prisoners. The tribe digs, maintains, and traps; it does not raid for captives.

Use this sheet when you need the whole tribe at once: a head count after a fight, an alarm cascade, or the stakes of a negotiation. Room-by-room details stay on the area sheets.

**Playable interaction branches:** [Kobold Negotiations](../../scenarios/kobold_negotiations/index.md)

### Count reconciliation

The room count is exact: 8 ambushers + 6 outer guards + 3 inner guards + Yiznak and 5 adults + 48 in the common chamber = **71**. The 18 rats are counted separately as kept animals. B2 does not state the sex of the ambushers or guard groups, so do not infer it from their jobs. All adults have combat statistics; the eight young never fight.

## Named figures

- [Yiznak the Deepwright](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#kobold-chief--yiznak-the-deepwright): AC 5, HD 2, hp 8, battle axe 2d4, ML 8. Carries the key to area 3. Wants engineering, excavation, traps, and maintenance recognized as valuable work; knows the marsh lizardfolk are kin.

## Prisoners

None held. The covered pit in the [entrance passage](a-entrance-passage.md) can *create* captives — the lid closes and prisoners need help from outside — but the tribe keeps no pens and no chains.

## Beasts

- [Area 2](02-giant-rat-refuse.md): Seventeen giant rats: AC 7, HD 1/2, hp 2, bite 1d3 plus disease, ML 8. Rat leader: AC 5, HD 1-1, hp 4, **two attacks for 2–4 damage each**, ML 8. Its silver chain and five gems are worth 400 gp intact, or 300 gp if broken into components. These are the kobolds' waste-eaters and semi-kept animals, not evidence of a raiding economy. Their weight does not trigger the pit.

## Alarm and reinforcement flow

1. Pit noise or fighting in area 1 → area 1 responds; rats rush from area 2.
2. One runner from area 1 warns [area 4](04-kobold-inner-guard-room.md) and [area 6](06-kobold-common-chamber.md).
3. Area 4 holds the corner (–2 to missiles against them), buying time for the common chamber to react.
4. Under safe conduct the same chain runs as a checkpoint: planks laid at the pit, names and business demanded, escort to [Yiznak](05-yiznaks-chamber.md); area 4 is the formal disarm-or-wait point.

## Relations

- Famine: [area 3](03-kobold-food-store.md) runs low — show it. The cult's manufactured famine hits this tribe's forty-eight-person common chamber first.
- Yiznak can broker the marsh lizardfolk connection.
- Campaign reading: this room count is the party's best argument against treating cave A as disposable.
