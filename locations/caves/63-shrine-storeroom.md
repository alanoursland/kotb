# 63. Shrine Storeroom

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Threat:** gelatinous cube | **Secret:** unknown route to area 50

> Boxes, crates, barrels, and sacks fill a mundane temple storeroom. The supply corridor beyond is suspiciously clean.

## Run this area

- Remaining longer than 3 rounds brings a gelatinous cube down the corridor to block the exit.
- Cube: AC 8, HD 4, hp 22, damage 2d4 plus paralysis, ML 12.
- Inside: 1d12 each cp, sp, ep, gp, and pp; victim bones; and a *wand of enemy detection* with nine charges.
- The wand must be removed within 1 turn after the cube's defeat or digestive fluids ruin it.
- Behind ordinary lamp oil and grave wrappings are two prepared **framing packets**, less any already used in play. One contains hobgoblin insignia and personal tokens; the other contains kobold tunnel markers and small tools. Each is wrapped with ducal-style arrowheads, wax, and cloth intended to make a staged attack look like the work of the party or its soldiers.
- Tags on the packets repeat the prisoner counts in [Vesna's papers](59-vesnas-chambers.md#command-papers) and the [torture register](61-torture-chamber.md): three hobgoblins, three kobolds. The tokens were taken by Vargoth's guards and delivered through the guest chamber.
- If the [Broken Culvert sabotage](../../scenarios/kobold_negotiations/09-broken-culvert-sabotage.md) has occurred, the kobold packet is gone. Its labeled shelf space, wrapping scraps, and the intact hobgoblin packet still show how the planted evidence was assembled and match material recovered from the culvert.
- These materials make the planned manipulation discoverable before it occurs. Presented to Karrgan or Yiznak with either set of records, they are persuasive evidence that the bugbears meant harm to both communities. The packets prove a local cult–bugbear operation, not who commands it from outside the ravine.
- The secret door to [area 50](50-maruks-chamber.md) is unknown to the gnolls. Tracks, moved stores, or Shrine maps can reveal that the cult had a hidden observation or access route into Maruk's home.

## Exits

- [Return to the Lower Shrine Hall](k-lower-hall.md) - Move before the cube blocks the supply corridor.
- [Use the secret passage to Maruk's Chamber](50-maruks-chamber.md) - Only after discovering the concealed door.
