# 63. Shrine Storeroom

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Threat:** gelatinous cube | **Secret:** unknown route to area 50

> Boxes, crates, barrels, and sacks fill a mundane temple storeroom. The supply corridor beyond is suspiciously clean.

## Run this area

- Remaining longer than 3 rounds brings a gelatinous cube down the corridor to block the exit.
- Cube: AC 8, HD 4, hp 22, damage 2d4 plus paralysis, ML 12.
- Inside: 1d12 each cp, sp, ep, gp, and pp; victim bones; and a *wand of enemy detection* with nine charges.
- The wand must be removed within 1 turn after the cube's defeat or digestive fluids ruin it.
- The secret door to [area 50](50-maruks-chamber.md) is unknown to the gnolls. Tracks, moved stores, or Shrine maps can reveal that the cult had a hidden observation or access route into Maruk's home.

## Exits

- [Return to the Lower Shrine Hall](k-lower-hall.md) - Move before the cube blocks the supply corridor.
- [Use the secret passage to Maruk's Chamber](50-maruks-chamber.md) - Only after discovering the concealed door.
