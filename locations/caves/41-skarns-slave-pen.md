# 41. Skarn's Slave Pen

**Faction:** Vargoth's captives | **Key NPC:** [Skarn](../../characters/skarn-the-rebel-bugbear.md) | **Threat among captives:** “Sir Garran”

> Another chained iron door holds hobgoblins, gnolls, one enormous bugbear, and a huge shaggy human whose staring eyes promise violence.

## Prisoners

- Three hobgoblins: AC 8, HD 1+1, hp 6.
- Two gnolls: AC 8, HD 2+1, hp 9.
- Skarn: AC 7, HD 3+1, hp 14; fights Vargoth's cell and remains with the party in the Caves.
- “Sir Garran”: human F4, AC 9, hp 24, +4 to hit, damage 1d6+3, ML 10.

## Run this area

- The other captives fight bugbears blocking escape and then flee.
- Skarn was caged for rejecting the Iron Ring oath. He can identify the cell's methods, handlers, and the meaning of repeated procedures, but needs documents to corroborate his testimony.
- Garran has a 50% chance each combat round to strike an ally while berserk. Afterward he murders rescuers for treasure or steals the best item if they are too strong.
- Use [The Prisoners](../../campaign/the-prisoners.md) for the campaign's circumstance-versus-species control.

## Exits

- [Return to the Slave-Pen Passage](h-lower-passage.md) - Leave through the chained door.
