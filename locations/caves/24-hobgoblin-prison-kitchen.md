# 24. Hobgoblin Prison-Kitchen

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Role:** torture, playroom, and food storage | **Campaign issue:** famine made institutional

> Two enormous hobgoblins preside over a fire pit, torture implements, and six prisoners chained to the walls. Keys hang in the southwest corner.

## Guards

- Two HD 2+1 hobgoblins in chain: AC 5, hp 10 and 8, sword 1d8, ML 9. One carries a 15-foot whip that can pull a hit victim down and paralyze them for 1d2 rounds before close engagement.
- Each has 1d6 cp, sp, and ep; the larger wears a 135 gp silver armlet.

## Prisoners

- **Emmet**, merchant; **Murz**, orc prisoner; **Petrek** and another man-at-arms; **Teska**, Emmet's wife; **Renk**, traumatized gnoll.
- Their behavior, rewards, and campaign purpose are in [The Prisoners](../../campaign/the-prisoners.md).

## Run this area

- Eating captives is famine-driven but has become procedure, status, and public policy. Food can end the material need; the negotiation must let Karrgan replace the policy without appearing to lose command and must forbid taking replacement captives.
- Keys to all chains are visible on the wall; freeing everyone without assessment creates immediate complications.

## Exits

- [Return to the Western Hall](f-west-hall.md) - Leave the fire pit and chains behind.
- [Go toward the Feast Hall](25-hobgoblin-feast-hall.md) - Follow preparations for the scheduled banquet.
