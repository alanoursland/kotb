# 25. Hobgoblin Feast Hall

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Population:** 4 adult males, 5 adult females, 9 young | **Role:** meals, meetings, and revels

> Tables and benches fill a large chamber being prepared for a feast. A pewter service waits on the head table while adults and young work around it.

## Run this area

- Four males: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 5, damage 1d8, ML 8; each has 1d4 gp.
- Five females: AC 7, HD 1, hp 4, damage 1d6, ML 7; each has 2d6 sp.
- Nine young do not fight.
- Pewter dishes on the head table are worth 25 gp as a set.
- This is the strongest negotiation venue after Karrgan's private quarters. If the party changes the feast's menu from prisoners to supplied food, make that physical and public.

- [Return to the Western Hall](f-west-hall.md) - Go toward the stronghold's main corridor.
- [Approach the Prison-Kitchen](24-hobgoblin-prison-kitchen.md) - Follow the smoke and guarded side passage.
