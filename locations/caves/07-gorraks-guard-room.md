# 7. Gorrak's Guard Room

**Faction:** Gorrak's orcs | **Role:** main entrance response | **Alert:** warned by the trophy-wall watcher

> Four orcs rise from pallets beneath shabby clothing pegs. Each holds two spears: one balanced for throwing, the other kept for close work.

## Run this area

- Four orcs: AC 7, HD 1, hp 5 each, spear 1d6, ML 8; each has 1d8 ep.
- When alerted, they rush intruders and raise the alarm on sight.
- The room contains no other valuables.
- For parley, these guards demand a visible halt and send word to Gorrak. They respond better to explicit terms and demonstrated strength than soothing language.

## Exits

- [Return to the Skull Passage](b-entrance-passage.md) - Go outward beneath the trophy niches.
- [Return to the Skull Passage and turn toward the Banquet Area](b-entrance-passage.md) - Rejoin the entrance junction before going deeper.
