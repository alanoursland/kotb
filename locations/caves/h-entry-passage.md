# Cave H — The Welcome Passage

**Faction:** Vargoth's bugbear cell | **Access:** apparently open | **Primary use:** bait and intake

> Signs in Kobold, Orcish, Goblin, and other tongues promise safety, security, a hot meal, and a bed. The first guard room waits just inside.

## Run this passage

- The signs are operational bait, not ironic decoration. Vargoth's people expect frightened travelers, fugitives, and lone humanoids to report voluntarily.
- Negotiators are treated as potential customers or inventory. Skarn can identify the welcome system as the first step in the slave intake process.
