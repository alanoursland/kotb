# 26. Hobgoblin Entry Guard Room

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Role:** response force for the barred entrance

> Six hobgoblins wait behind the outer door. Three keep crossbows ready; all have maces for close fighting.

## Run this area

- Six hobgoblins: AC 7 with crossbows or 6 in melee, HD 1+1, hp 6, damage 1d6, ML 8; each has 1d4 cp, sp, and gp.
- Three fire once, drop crossbows, and close with maces.
- If the outer door is battered or its bar falls, five reach the entry in 2 rounds. The sixth warns area 27 and joins in round 4.
- With safe conduct, this is Karrgan's formal weapons-control point. Visitors are counted, stated business is repeated back, and escorts take position front and rear.

## Exits

- [Go outward to the Barred Entrance](f-entry-passage.md) - Return to cave mouth F.
- [Enter the Western Hall](f-west-hall.md) - Proceed into communal and supply areas.
- [Warn or enter the Armory](27-hobgoblin-armory.md) - Take the guarded inner route.
