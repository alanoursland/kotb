# 14. Ushka's Orc Common Chamber

**Faction:** Ushka's orcs | **Population:** 9 adult males, 8 adult females, 3 young | **Alert:** reaches entrance in 1 round

> A disorderly family chamber lies close behind the netted approach. Shields and swords stay within reach of the sleeping places.

## Run this area

- Nine males: AC 6 with shields, HD 1, hp 3, sword 1d6, ML 8; each has 1d20 sp.
- Eight females have 1d4 cp each; three young carry nothing. They do not normally fight.
- The males rush to the entrance when the alarm net falls, arriving in 1 round.
- The room is messy and holds no other valuables.
- Under parley, these are Ushka's nearest witnesses and first constituency. A deal that ignores their hunger will not survive her acceptance.

## Exits

- [Return to the Netted Entrance Passage](c-entrance-passage.md) - Go outward beneath the alarm net.
- [Continue toward the Common Hall](15-ushkas-common-hall.md) - Follow the interior passage toward meetings and meals.
