# 43. First Fire-Beetle Cave

**Zone:** Khaeros's labyrinth | **Occupants:** three fire beetles | **Secret:** concealed passage to area 36

> Three hungry beetles turn their glowing red glands toward the passage and rush the first warm bodies they see.

## Run this area

- Three fire beetles: AC 4, HD 1+2, hp 7, bite 2d4, ML 7.
- Each has two glowing glands above the eyes and one in the abdomen. Removed glands cast red light in a 10-foot radius for 1d6 days.
- No other treasure.
- The glow is a useful labyrinth landmark, but removing it changes the navigation description on later visits.
- Khaeros knows the concealed passage reaches Vargoth's chamber, although the door is not inside Khaeros's own cavern.

## Exits

- [Return to the Labyrinth Depths](i-labyrinth-depths.md) - Follow the branching passages away from the glow.
- [Seek the Second Fire-Beetle Cave](44-fire-beetle-cave.md) - Take the narrower route toward another red light.
- [Use the secret passage to Vargoth's Chamber](36-vargoths-chamber.md) - Find and operate the concealed stone door.
