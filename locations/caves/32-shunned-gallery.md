# 32. Shunned Gallery

**Zone:** Shunned Cavern | **Role:** branching carrion gallery | **Threat:** noise attracts predators

> Rotting corpses, bones, dead leaves, and old branches lie beneath an overwhelming stench. Small coins glint among the debris.

## Run this area

- A careful search finds one coin per round: 1–2 cp, 3–4 sp, 5–6 ep.
- Each noisy search can attract a visitor: 1 = owlbear from area 34; 2 = 2d6 giant rats; 3 = gray ooze from area 33; 4–6 = nothing.
- The repeated “32” spaces on the map are one connected gallery network, not separate numbered encounters.
- Cave residents avoid this entire complex. Clearing its predators is a concrete, nonverbal service to every nearby faction.

## Exits

- [Go outside to the Ravine Floor](ravine.md) - Follow fresher air through cave mouth G.
- [Enter the Shallow Pool](33-gray-ooze-pool.md) - Follow the wetter branch and blind-fish water.
- [Approach the Owlbear Den](34-owlbear-den.md) - Follow the strongest carrion smell and broken branches.
