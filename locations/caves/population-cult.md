# Cave K Population — The Shrine of Evil Chaos

**Faction:** The cult of Thanatos | **Population:** 10 living cultists + 72 undead + 1 cube | **Leader:** [Vesna, the Hierophant](../../characters/the-hierophant.md) | **Prisoner:** Lysandra, area 64

> **At a glance:** Nine clerics and a torturer make up the ten living cultists. They control 39 zombies and 32 skeletons. A sealed wight and a gelatinous cube are independent hazards, and Lysandra is their prisoner. The bell mobilizes the forty undead warriors in area 57; it does not awaken or relocate the entire population.

Use this sheet for the undead order of battle, the bell cascade, and who counts as a person in cave K. Room-by-room details stay on the area sheets.

### Count reconciliation

The controlled force reconciles exactly: zombies 8 (53) + 20 (57) + 3 (59) + 8 mobile responders (58) = 39; skeletons 12 (52) + 20 (57) = 32. That is 71 controlled skeletons and zombies. Add the sealed wight for 72 undead. The living cult consists of 4 acolytes + 4 adepts + Vesna + the torturer = 10 people. The cube and Lysandra complete the 84 beings on this sheet.

The anti-turning protection applies to the skeletons in area 52, the zombies in area 53, the ranks in area 57, and the eight mobile zombie responders. B2 does not give it to the three zombies in area 59. The wight turns as a wight. Area 52's skeletons are inert until triggered, so early turning attempts do nothing.

## Named figures

- [Vesna, the Hierophant](../../characters/the-hierophant.md): **campaign override — use her full profile and campaign-level power, not only B2's printed cleric 3 baseline.** She repositions, rallies defenders, and ultimately chooses suicide-ascension rather than capture. *Potion of gaseous form*; escape via area 51. She can command undead to attack even correctly-disguised intruders.
- Mara Sokol ([area 54](54-acolytes-chamber.md)): newest acolyte, not yet initiated. Round 1 the senior acolyte orders her to shut the door and take her place; she moves defensively but does not attack unless attacked. The other three may try to force her across the line.
- The torturer ([area 61](61-torture-chamber.md)): records or testimony from his room may identify which captives were questioned.

### Off-site Thanatos agents

[Father Ariston Vale](../../characters/father-ariston-vale.md), Corin, and Sera are witting servants of Thanatos operating in the Keep. They belong to a separate operation: they do not reside in the Shrine and are not included in this population count.

## Prisoners — area 64

- [Lysandra](64-lysandras-cell.md), medusa: AC 8, HD 4, hp 20, bite 1d6 plus poison, ML 8. Iron door locked and barred with a viewing window; skeletons chained to the wall around her.
- Recently captured by zombie guards. **The cult intended to remove her snakes, blind her, and sacrifice her.**
- Looking directly at her requires a save or petrification; her bite forces a poison save or death. She carries *stone-to-flesh elixir* for six people but does not initially intend to surrender it.
- The printed outcome is betrayal. The campaign makes that outcome real but not inevitable. The polished mirror in the [guest chamber](60-shrine-guest-chamber.md) is usable against her.

## Alarm and reinforcement flow

1. Continuous noise at the [entrance](k-entry-passage.md) brings the eight zombie responders.
2. **The great bell in [area 58](58-temple-of-evil-chaos.md) is the real alarm:** destroying a chapel relic rings it; hard-pressed adepts flee to ring it; three chanting voices ring it magically — even one chanter alerts Vesna's guards.
3. When the bell sounds, [area 57](57-hall-of-undead-warriors.md) deploys: skeletons out the south door to the temple's south wall, zombies out the north to its north wall — waves, not forty separate searches.
4. Temple robes and matching amulets defeat some standing guards, but never Vesna's direct commands.

## Relations

- The cult is **manufacturing the famine** that starves the caves into raiding — the hungrier the caves get, the better it recruits. The kobolds' emptying larder and the hobgoblins' institutional captive-eating trace back here.
- The secret door from [area 63](63-shrine-storeroom.md) into gnoll area 50 is unknown to the gnolls; shrine maps reveal the cult had covert access into Maruk's home.
- Asked to march on the cult, the federation's chiefs decline — "that's a human problem."
- Vargoth's bugbears are the Shrine's local operational partners. They supply cave intelligence, selected captives, and artifacts suitable for planting; the cult supplies warnings, targeted undead harassment, and false signs intended to deepen hostility. Vesna thinks Vargoth is a distasteful ally of Traladaran resistance. Vargoth understands that famine and division protect his slave business.
- Other cave communities do not know about this arrangement. “Only the bugbears know about the cult” means Vargoth's cell is the sole cave faction knowingly dealing with the Shrine, not that the cult is unaware of its own bugbear couriers.

## Shrine-first evidence route

If the party reaches the cult before Cave H, the Shrine points them directly at Vargoth without resolving the larger conspiracy for them.

1. [Area 60](60-shrine-guest-chamber.md) establishes repeated bugbear visits through tracks, hair, catnip, multilingual welcome text, and matching trade cord.
2. [Area 61](61-torture-chamber.md) identifies prisoners supplied by Vargoth and records intelligence gathered for a false incident.
3. [Area 59](59-vesnas-chambers.md#command-papers) names Vargoth, gives exact captive counts, and shows that the Shrine and bugbears were coordinating sabotage.
4. [Area 63](63-shrine-storeroom.md) contains the prepared framing packets—or the remains and records of a packet already used—the physical means of turning Karrgan and Yiznak against the party.
5. Mara can authenticate the traffic and explain what the cult believed, but cannot name the Iron Ring or the Black Eagle patron.

Showing this evidence to Karrgan or Yiznak before attacking Cave H prevents the attack from costing trust. Freeing and returning their missing people strengthens the case. The bugbear lair must still be searched to uncover the slave operation, Eagles, outside trade channel, and evidence pointing beyond the ravine.
