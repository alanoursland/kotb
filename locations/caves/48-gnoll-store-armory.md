# 48. Gnoll Store and Armory

**Faction:** Maruk's gnolls | **Access:** locked | **Hazard:** exceptionally good leaking ale

> Provisions and weapons fill a locked chamber. One barrel leaks ale so fragrant that tasting it becomes its own danger.

## Contents

- Seven shields; dwarf-sized chain mail; twelve hand axes; three longbows; five quivers of twenty arrows.
- One cursed sword –1.

## Ale

- A taster has a 5-in-6 chance to take a deep drink and spend 1d4 turns drinking, singing, and making noise.
- Attacks remain at –2 for the same number of turns afterward.
- In diplomacy, Maruk can warn guests plainly. Ignoring him and helping themselves is theft, not a cute trap.

## Exits

- [Return to the Gnoll Inner Passage](j-inner-passage.md) - Leave through the locked door.
