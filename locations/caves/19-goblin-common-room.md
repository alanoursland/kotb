# 19. Goblin Common Room

**Faction:** goblin ward | **Population:** 10 adult males, 14 adult females, 6 young

> Bedding heaps, tables, stools, benches, cooking gear, and low furniture crowd a chamber used for meals and meetings.

## Run this area

- Ten males: AC 6, HD 1-1, hp 3, damage 1d6, ML 7; each has 1d6 sp.
- Fourteen females have 2d6 cp each; six young do not fight.
- If the wandering patrol has not appeared elsewhere, add its six males and food bags here.
- The clutter gives goblin-sized residents familiar paths and makes rapid movement by larger intruders awkward.
- This room makes the goblin ward's dependence visible: the stolen supplies in area 21 support families, while hobgoblins skim the best goods away.
