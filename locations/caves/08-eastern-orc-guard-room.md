# 8. Eastern Orc Guard Room

**Faction:** Gorrak's orcs | **Role:** flanking reserve | **Access:** quiet branch from the entrance

> Four more spear-armed orcs hold the quiet eastern side of the lair, positioned to circle behind anyone pressing the main guards.

## Run this area

- Use the same statistics and carried treasure as area 7: four orcs, AC 7, HD 1, hp 5, spear 1d6, ML 8; 1d8 ep each.
- On alarm, they rush west and then south to flank threats near areas 7 or 9.
- If the party negotiates at the entrance, these guards remain unseen unless invited forward. Their absence is part of Gorrak's deterrence posture.

## Exits

- [Return to the Skull Passage](b-entrance-passage.md) - Rejoin the entrance junction.
- [Circle back to the Skull Passage](b-entrance-passage.md) - Take the flanking route back toward areas 7 and 9.
