# Cave K — Temple Hall

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Access:** inner shrine | **Primary use:** junction around the main ritual complex

> Red and black chambers cluster around the temple proper: clerical quarters, a bloodstained chapel, barracks of motionless undead, and the vast ritual hall beyond.

## Exits

- [Return to the Zombie Guard Room](53-zombie-guard-room.md) - Go outward through the standing defense.
- [Return to the Outer Shrine Hall](k-outer-hall.md) - Follow the broad passage toward areas 52 and 53.
- [Enter the Acolytes' Chamber](54-acolytes-chamber.md) - Take the lesser clergy door; Mara is here.
- [Enter the Chapel of Evil Chaos](55-chapel-of-evil-chaos.md) - Follow the red-stone passage toward the bloodstained altar.
- [Enter the Adepts' Chamber](56-adepts-chamber.md) - Take the senior clergy door.
- [Enter the Hall of Undead Warriors](57-hall-of-undead-warriors.md) - Approach the formed ranks of skeletons and zombies.
- [Enter the Temple of Evil Chaos](58-temple-of-evil-chaos.md) - Proceed into the great black-and-red ritual hall.
- [Descend toward the Lower Shrine Hall](k-lower-hall.md) - Take the passage serving guests, torture rooms, crypt, stores, and cells.
