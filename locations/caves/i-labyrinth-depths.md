# Cave I — Labyrinth Depths

**Zone:** Khaeros's labyrinth | **Access:** direction-confused | **Primary use:** route among areas 42–45

> Intersections repeat with small differences: a broken spur of stone, red beetle-light, old bones, the smell of an armored animal that is not an animal.
