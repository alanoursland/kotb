# Ravine Floor

**Zone:** exterior Caves of Chaos | **Access:** open and exposed | **Primary use:** choose a cave, observe movement, retreat

> Steep walls rise around a long, gloomy ravine. Tangled copses interrupt the broken ground, bones gleam among rubble and dead wood, and black cave mouths open at different heights on both walls.

## Run this area

- The ravine is about 200 feet wide at its eastern opening and runs roughly 440 feet west to a steep end.
- Cave entrances sit at different elevations. Contours, trees, and slopes matter even when two mouths look close on the page.
- Anyone crossing open ground may be watched from several lairs. A diplomatic party can be seen entering one faction's home by its rivals.
- Thick copses force single-file movement and can conceal scouts, ambushers, or observers.

## DM-only

This is diplomatic territory as much as a dungeon exterior. Track who sees the party, who gave them safe conduct, and whether they cross the ravine carrying another faction's wounded, prisoners, food, or symbols.
