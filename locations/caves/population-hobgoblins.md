# Cave F Population — Karrgan's Hobgoblins

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Population:** 63 hobgoblins—59 at home, 4 raiding goblin storage | **Leader:** [Karrgan](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#hobgoblin-chief--karrgan) | **Prisoners:** 6 in area 24

> **At a glance:** 59 hobgoblins occupy cave F: 27 ordinary adult males, Karrgan, 19 adult females, and 12 young. Four additional adult hobgoblins are away stealing from goblin storage, for a faction total of 63. Six prisoners are chained in the prison-kitchen.

### Count reconciliation

Rooms 23–31 hold 59 residents. The four hobgoblins who use the secret route to rob [goblin storage](21-goblin-storage.md) are additional faction members, not conditional creations: if the party does not meet them in area 21, they return to area 28. The older campaign shorthand "~90 + 1 ogre" describes the approximately 89 armed goblins and hobgoblins under this bloc plus [Brundt](22-brundts-cave.md); it is **not** a household population count.

## Named figures

- [Karrgan](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#hobgoblin-chief--karrgan): AC 2, HD 5, hp 22, damage 1d8+2 (3–10), ML 10; 5 pp, 31 gp, and a 600 gp silver-and-gem belt. *Wand of paralyzation* (7 charges) hidden in his fireplace kindling. The federation's natural officer — and its greatest internal danger if over-empowered.

## Alarm and reinforcement flow

1. Forcing the barred entry (three successful 1s on d6) alerts [area 26](26-hobgoblin-entry-guard.md); *knock* drops the bar loudly and gives two rounds.
2. Area 26: three fire crossbows once and close; five reach the entry in 2 rounds; the sixth warns [area 27](27-hobgoblin-armory.md) and joins in round 4.
3. Area 27: two ambush at the door, one takes the secret route to [area 31](31-hobgoblin-chief-guard.md).
4. Area 29: one woman warns [area 30](30-karrgans-quarters.md), the other [area 31](31-hobgoblin-chief-guard.md); both then fight.
5. Area 31 relays among 27, 29, and 30, and can move defenders behind an intrusion via the armory's secret route.
6. Under safe conduct, area 26 is the weapons-control point: visitors counted, business repeated back, escorts front and rear.

**Succession:** if Karrgan dies, survivors usually seek the goblin ward unless the entire ravine is visibly collapsing.

## Relations

- Cave D goblins: coerced allies — area 23 stands battle-ready against them; [area 28](28-hobgoblin-storeroom.md) holds stolen goblin goods reached by a secret route into goblin storage. Finding it gives the party leverage with the goblin ward.
- Bugbears: Karrgan's hospitality is formal, armed, and contingent — not the bugbears' false welcome.
- The armory stockpile (2 plate, 5 chain, 7 leather, 13 crossbows, 220 arrows including 14 silver-headed, and more) is why the federation both needs and fears him.
