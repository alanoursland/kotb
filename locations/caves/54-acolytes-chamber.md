# 54. Acolytes' Chamber

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Key NPC:** [Mara Sokol](../../characters/mara-sokol.md) | **Role:** playable mercy and surviving witness

> Four young clerics in rusty-red robes and black cowls occupy a severe room of hard pallets, stools, brazier, cabinet, and water pails.

## Occupants

- Four cleric 1 acolytes: AC 5 in chain beneath robes, hp 4, mace 1d6, ML 8; each has 10 gp.
- The senior acolyte carries an *amulet of protection from good* and has completed initiation.
- Mara is the newest recruit. Her robe is too large, her mace grip uncertain, and an old wooden Traladaran token is hidden beneath her iron ring.

## Run this area

- First round, the senior acolyte orders: “Mara! Shut the door and take your place!” Mara moves defensively but does not attack unless attacked.
- Do not hide her hesitation behind a check. A credible surrender plus evidence that the cult caused the famine can make her drop the mace.
- The other three fight normally and may try to force Mara across the line.
- Furniture and books have no sale value. Iron-ring pendants, copied routes, and Mara's observations are evidence.
- Use [Mara's full encounter](../../characters/mara-sokol.md#the-encounter-in-room-54).

## Exits

- [Return to the Temple Hall](k-temple-hall.md) - Leave the acolytes' quarters.
- [Move toward the Chapel of Evil Chaos](55-chapel-of-evil-chaos.md) - Follow the red-stone inner passage.
