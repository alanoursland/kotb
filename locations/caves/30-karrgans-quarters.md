# 30. Karrgan's Quarters

**Faction:** hobgoblins | **Resident:** [Karrgan](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#hobgoblin-chief--karrgan) | **Role:** command and hardest negotiation

> Heavy furniture and accumulated junk crowd the chamber. The armored chief dominates the room while four large hobgoblin women hold the space around him.

## People and stats

- Karrgan: AC 2, HD 5, hp 22, damage 1d8+2 (3–10), ML 10; 5 pp, 31 gp, and a 600 gp silver-and-gem belt.
- Four companions: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 6, damage 1d8, ML 10; each has 2d6 gp.

## Run this area

- Treat Karrgan as a peer commander. He understands chains of command, deterrence, patrol obligations, provisioning, and enforceable terms.
- The negotiation turns on prisoners as food: material shortage if you want a clean trade solution, or public authority and ritual if you want the harder cultural version.
- False bottom in the skin-filled iron box: 25 pp, 200 gp, 115 ep, 400 sp, a 100 gp gem, and potion of poison.
- Kindling by the southeast fireplace hides a *wand of paralyzation* with seven charges.
