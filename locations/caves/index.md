# The Caves of Chaos

Use these pages as a text-adventure map of the ravine. Every numbered area has its own reference sheet. Unnumbered passage pages represent meaningful junctions, trapped approaches, stairs, or long corridors; they do not add encounters to B2.

Start at the [Ravine Floor](ravine.md). When the party enters a cave mouth, follow its linked passage page and then use the exits at the bottom of every area sheet.

## Running the complex

- Cave mouths are visible from much of the ravine, but paths, trees, contours, and elevation make movement between them exposed and slow.
- Sound carries inside a lair. Alarm responses on the room sheets are more important than wandering-monster checks during a fast run.
- Residents learn. After a hostile visit, move guards, close doors, add warning lines, and let factions share information according to their alliances.
- A room cleared by violence remains empty for 1–4 weeks before survivors return or something else occupies it.
- The campaign's faction-level interpretation is in [The Caves of Chaos roster](../../campaign/caves-of-chaos.md).
