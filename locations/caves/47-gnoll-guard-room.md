# 47. Gnoll Inner Guard Room

**Faction:** Maruk's gnolls | **Population:** 3 adult males, 5 adult females | **Role:** ready reserve

> Bedding, rude furniture, hides, pelts, and a water barrel fill a defended family barracks.

## Run this area

- Three males: AC 5, HD 2, hp 8, damage 2d4, ML 8; each has 1d6 gp.
- Five females: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 5, damage 1d8, ML 8; each has 1d4 gp.
- One wall hanging is a sable cloak worth 450 gp.
- The residents are ready to fight immediately if the entry guards raised alarm.
- Peaceful contact here should feel almost anticlimactically ordinary: alert adults protecting quarters, not a moral puzzle invented to justify fear.

## Exits

- [Return to the Gnoll Inner Passage](j-inner-passage.md) - Go toward the entry and other rooms.
- [Enter the Gnoll Common Room](49-gnoll-common-room.md) - Follow the busier family route.
