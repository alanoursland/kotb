# 29. Hobgoblin Inner Guard Room

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Role:** final defense of the chief

> Two crossbowmen, two armed women, cots, a bench, and a box of soiled clothing fill the compact guard room.

## Run this area

- Two males: AC 7, HD 1+1, hp 5, crossbow or sword 1d6, ML 8; each has 2d6 sp and 2d6 cp.
- Two females: AC 7, HD 1, hp 4, damage 1d6, ML 7.
- On sighting attackers, one woman warns area 30 and the other area 31; both then fight.
- Peaceful visitors wait here while Karrgan decides whether to receive them privately.

- [Enter Karrgan's Quarters](30-karrgans-quarters.md) - Pass the final guarded door.
