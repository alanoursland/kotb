# 16. Ushka's Chamber

**Faction:** Ushka's orcs | **Resident:** [Ushka](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#orc-chiefs--gorrak--ushka-feuding) | **Secret:** route to area 13

> A guard stands inside the door before stacks of barrels, boxes, and sacks. Beyond the supplies, a large armored orc presides over a well-furnished private room.

## People and stats

- Door guard: AC 5, HD 1+1, hp 6, damage 1d6, ML 8; 2d6 sp and 1d4 gp.
- Ushka uses the B2 chief: AC 2, HD 3, hp 16, sword 1d6, hand axe +1 thrown in the same round, ML 10.
- Her mate: AC 7, HD 1, hp 5, damage 1d6, ML 10.

## Run this area

- Ushka is the shrewder chief: she calculates consequences and can open the route toward reconciliation with Gorrak.
- Valuable personal items: silver belt and gold buckle, 160 gp; sword-pommel gem, 100 gp; ivory bracelet, 100 gp.
- A drawer holds 50 pp tied with a *rope of climbing*. A tarnished silver-chased copper bowl is worth 50 gp if recognized.
- Supplies include one 400-coin-weight barrel of good wine worth 55 gp.
- The concealed connection to [area 13](13-orc-moot-room.md) is known only to the two chiefs.

## Exits

- [Return to the Common Hall](15-ushkas-common-hall.md) - Leave through the guarded visible door.
- [Use the secret door to the Orc Moot Room](13-orc-moot-room.md) - Enter the chiefs' private meeting place.
