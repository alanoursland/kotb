# Cave A — Inner Passage

**Faction:** kobolds | **Access:** defended | **Primary use:** junction between stores, guards, chief, and common chamber

> The cramped passage turns beyond the outer guard post. A locked store door breaks one wall; farther on, an angled corner creates a prepared firing lane.
