# 50. Maruk's Chamber

**Faction:** gnolls | **Resident:** [Maruk](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#gnoll-chief--maruk) | **Secret:** unknown passage to area 63

> Crude furniture surrounds a strong gnoll chief, his two sons, and four women. A fireplace alcove and one old skeleton conceal separate stories.

## People and stats

- Maruk: AC 3, HD 3, hp 17, damage 2d4+2, ML 10; two 50 gp silver armbands and 39 gp.
- Two sons: AC 4, HD 2+1, hp 10, damage 2d4+1, ML 10; each has 1d10 gp, ep, and sp.
- Four women: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 5, damage 1d8, ML 9; each has a 30 gp chain and 2d6 ep.

## Run this area

- Maruk wants a fair arrangement and peace. The challenge is whether the party can accept that there is no hidden moral obstacle.
- Beneath a fireplace flagstone: 200 cp, 157 sp, 76 ep, 139 gp.
- Just inside the unknown secret route lies a human thief's skeleton with broken leg, twelve 50 gp gems, and usable *elven boots*.
- The secret door to [area 63](63-shrine-storeroom.md) is unknown to every gnoll. Discovering it reveals that the Shrine has been able to approach their home unseen.

## Exits

- [Return to the Gnoll Inner Passage](j-inner-passage.md) - Leave through the visible entrance.
- [Enter the Gnoll Common Room](49-gnoll-common-room.md) - Go toward the tribe's families.
- [Use the secret passage to the Shrine Storeroom](63-shrine-storeroom.md) - Only after discovering the door the gnolls never found.
