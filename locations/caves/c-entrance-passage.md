# Cave C — Netted Entrance Passage

**Faction:** Ushka's orcs | **Access:** trapped | **Hazard:** alarm net 11 feet inside

> The cave mouth looks unwatched. Nearly invisible cords cross the passage just inside, beneath a tarred rope net weighted with pieces of metal.

## Run this passage

- A careful observer has a 1-in-6 chance to notice the trip cords.
- Triggering them drops a 10-by-18-foot net, traps victims for 1d4 rounds, and sounds an alarm.
- Orcs from [area 14](14-ushkas-common-chamber.md) arrive in 1 round; fighters from [area 15](15-ushkas-common-hall.md) arrive in 3.

## Exits

- [Turn toward Ushka's Common Chamber](14-ushkas-common-chamber.md) - Take the near branch where defenders muster.
- [Follow the longer passage to the Common Hall](15-ushkas-common-hall.md) - Go deeper toward cooking and conversation.
- [Return to the Ravine Floor](ravine.md) - Back out beneath the suspended net.
