# 55. Chapel of Evil Chaos

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Role:** lesser ritual chamber | **Hazard:** corrupting relics

> Red stone surrounds a black-and-red mosaic floor. Four black pillars frame a bloodstained altar before a tapestry of a skull-faced moon and demonic silhouettes.

## Relics

- Four bloodstained bronze vessels: shallow bowl, two goblets, and ewer.
- Each goblet is worth 1,000 gp; bowl and ewer 2,000 gp each—but they are cursed relics rather than ordinary treasure.

## Run this area

- Anyone picking up a vessel saves vs. Magic at –2. Success gives a strong warning of evil and permits setting it down. Failure begins a six-day corruption ending in service to the Shrine.
- Destroying a relic rings the great bell in area 58; residents arrive in 3 rounds.
- *Detect evil* makes them glow purple and repels good characters.
- Removal requires *dispel magic* followed by *bless*: 60% on day one, decreasing by 10% each day through day six.
- Father Branislav recognizes the chamber as a Thanatos corruption, not a legitimate Church of Traladar sanctuary.
