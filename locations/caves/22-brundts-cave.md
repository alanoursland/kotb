# 22. Brundt's Cave

**Resident:** [Brundt “Who-Comes-When-Paid”](../../characters/brundt-the-ogre.md) | **Role:** mercenary home, market node, and first contact | **Secret:** goblin payment door to area 18

> A strong sour odor fills the cave. A huge bearskin lies on leaves like a sleeping animal; farther in, a nine-foot ogre sits on a great leather bag.

## Brundt

- AC 4, HD 4+1, hp 25, great weapon 1d10+2 (3–12), ML 10.
- He is a contractor, not an automatic combatant. A payment covers only the work explicitly purchased.
- Run first contact from [The Ogre at the Ravine](../../campaign/first-contact-the-ogre.md), not as a surprise boss fight.

## The seven sacks

1. 287 sp.
2. Hard cheese.
3. 182 cp and 91 ep.
4. 289 gp.
5. Keg of brandy worth 80 gp.
6. 303 cp.
7. 241 **Eagles**—gold-washed lead Black Eagle scrip, physically worth 1 cp each in B2 and less than a copper by campaign valuation.

Under southern bones: six arrows +1, potion of invisibility, and cleric scroll with *cure light wounds* and *hold person*.

## Exits

- [Go outside to the Ravine Floor](ravine.md) - Leave through cave mouth E.
- [Use the secret payment door to Goblin Guard Room 18](18-goblin-ogre-guard.md) - Open the concealed passage used for contracts.
