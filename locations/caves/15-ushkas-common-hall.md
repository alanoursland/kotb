# 15. Ushka's Orc Common Hall

**Faction:** Ushka's orcs | **Role:** meetings, cooking, and secondary quarters

> Food is cooked and eaten in a long general hall. The western end holds sleeping places; two of the six armed males keep crossbows ready.

## Run this area

- Six males: AC 7, HD 1, hp 3, melee 1d6; two have crossbows firing every other round. ML 8.
- Four females are noncombatants. Each resident carries treasure as in area 14.
- The males reach the entrance net 3 rounds after it falls.
- This is the proper public negotiating room if Ushka wants witnesses without exposing her private chamber.
