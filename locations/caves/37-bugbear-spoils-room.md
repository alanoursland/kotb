# 37. Bugbear Spoils Room

**Faction:** Vargoth's cell | **Access:** heavy locked door | **Key:** Vargoth's pouch

> Good dried food, salted provisions, leather hides, ale, wine, and oil fill a locked room. A polished shield serves as an herb tray.

## Contents

- Shield +1, currently under dried catnip relished by these bugbears.
- High-quality foodstuffs, hides, three ale barrels, one tun of wine, and goods worth 400 gp if sold at Riverfork.
- One small oil keg holding twenty flasks.

## Run this area

- Breaking the lock or smashing the door brings guards from area 35 and Vargoth's household from area 36.
- These are partly stolen goods and partly Black Eagle trade stock bought back with Eagles at controlled prices.
- Marks on some containers match dates or quantities in the slave tally. The goods prove commerce; the tally and witnesses establish its purpose.

## Exits

- [Return to the Upper Living Passage](h-upper-passage.md) - Leave through the heavy door.
