# Cave F — Barred Hobgoblin Entrance

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Access:** stout barred door | **Primary use:** military choke point

> Skulls line the entry cave. More skulls decorate a heavy oaken door beneath a Common inscription inviting visitors to dinner.

## Run this passage

- A careful examiner has a 1-in-6 chance to find the outside mechanism that slides the bar.
- Forcing the door requires three successful 1s on d6 checks and alerts area 26.
- A *knock* spell drops the bar loudly but gives intruders two rounds before the guards arrive.
- In campaign play, Karrgan may open this door under flag of truce. Hospitality is formal, armed, and contingent—not the bugbears' false welcome.
