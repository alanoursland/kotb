# 59. Vesna's Chambers

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Resident:** [Vesna, the Hierophant](../../characters/the-hierophant.md) | **Role:** command suite and un-interrogatable antagonist

> Three plate-armored zombies stand in a lavish anteroom. Beyond, black wood, scarlet velvet, red carpet, and a demon idol turn private luxury into a ritual statement.

## Anteroom 59g

- Three zombies: AC 2 in plate and shield, HD 2, hp 13, damage 1d8, ML 12.
- They activate when summoned by temple chanting, when anyone enters, or on Vesna's order.
- Golden flagon worth 500 gp and nine cups worth 100 gp each.

## Vesna

- **Campaign override:** use Vesna's full profile and campaign-level power, not only B2's printed cleric 3 baseline.
- Vesna sincerely believes the Shrine serves Traladaran liberation. They do not know they are an Iron Ring asset.
- They reposition, rally defenders, and ultimately choose suicide-ascension rather than capture. They cannot provide the conspiracy explanation; Mara and the physical evidence must survive.

## Room hazards and escape

- Demon idol above the bed topples for 2d6 damage if touched by anyone but Vesna; two gem eyes worth 100 gp each.
- Secret wardrobe route spills 500 gp and fifty 10 gp gems into the room when opened, delaying pursuit.
- B2 provides a *potion of gaseous form* and routes toward area 51. Vesna uses escape only to preserve the final choice, never to become available for questioning.

## Exits

- [Return to the Temple of Evil Chaos](58-temple-of-evil-chaos.md) - Pass through the zombie-held anteroom.
- [Use the secret escape into the inner corridors](k-lower-hall.md) - Only after discovering the wardrobe route.
- [Flee toward the Boulder-Filled Passage](51-boulder-blocked-passage.md) - Vesna's gaseous emergency route.
