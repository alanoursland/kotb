# 38. Bugbear Common Room

**Faction:** Vargoth's cell | **Population:** 3 adult males, 7 adult females, 3 young

> Bedding and old clothing lie around a fireplace. A soot-blackened metal urn sits nearby, its silver hidden by grime.

## Run this area

- Three males: AC 5, HD 3+1, hp 12, damage 2d4, ML 9; each has 2d6 gp and 2d6 sp.
- Seven females: AC 6, HD 2, hp 8, damage 1d8, ML 8.
- Three young: AC 7, HD 1, hp 3, damage 1d4, ML 7.
- The silver urn is worth 175 gp if cleaned or closely inspected.
- Do not turn this household into an argument that Vargoth is redeemable. It shows that cells contain dependents and that dismantling one creates custody, resettlement, and accountability problems after the fight.

## Exits

- [Return to the Upper Living Passage](h-upper-passage.md) - Go toward areas 35–37 and the entrance.
