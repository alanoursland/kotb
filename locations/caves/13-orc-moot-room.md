# 13. Secret Orc Moot Room

**Faction:** shared by Gorrak and Ushka | **Access:** secret from both lairs | **Role:** private diplomacy

> A small table and two chairs occupy the middle of a forgotten chamber. Arms hang ready on the wall; a foul bucket sits neglected in one corner.

## Run this area

- Only Gorrak and Ushka know this room. They meet here to plan joint ventures and discuss threats despite their tribes' hostility.
- This is proof that the feud is political, not absolute. The two chiefs already possess a private institution the party can enlarge into reconciliation.
- Wooden chest: bow, twenty arrows, two swords, two daggers. Two shields hang on the south wall.
- Beneath the stagnant-water bucket are two pouches, each holding a 50 gp gem, 10 gp, and 20 sp.
- Two giant centipedes nest beneath the pouches: AC 9, HD 1/2, hp 2, bite causes illness, ML 7.

## Exits

- [Use the concealed route to Gorrak's Chamber](12-gorraks-chamber.md) - Open the secret door into cave B.
- [Use the concealed route to Ushka's Chamber](16-ushkas-chamber.md) - Open the opposite secret door into cave C.

## DM-only

If the party brokers peace, hold the first two-chief meeting here. It lets reconciliation begin in a room the chiefs themselves created rather than in a human hall.
