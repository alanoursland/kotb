# 18. Ogre-Side Goblin Guard Chamber

**Faction:** goblins | **Role:** eastern watch and Brundt liaison | **Secret:** payment door to area 22

> Four goblin guards hold the east-facing chamber. A water barrel stands beside a wall whose stones conceal a practiced transaction.

## Run this area

- Four goblins, identical to area 17: AC 6, HD 1-1, hp 3, spear 1d6, ML 7; normal carried coin.
- On “Bree-Yark,” two guards retrieve a sack of 250 gp hidden in the water barrel, pass it through the secret door to Brundt, and ask him to attack.
- Brundt accepts the printed payment unless the campaign has already changed his relationship with the party or caves.
- The secret door is a market interface: the goblins do not need to enter Brundt's home to hire him.

## Exits

- [Return to the Entry Guard Chamber](17-goblin-entry-guard.md) - Go outward toward cave D.
- [Continue into the Goblin Living Passage](d-inner-passage.md) - Follow the worked corridor toward common rooms and stores.
- [Use the secret payment door to Brundt's Cave](22-brundts-cave.md) - Only if the concealed panel is found or opened from the other side.
