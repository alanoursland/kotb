# Cave H — Upper Living Passage

**Faction:** Vargoth's bugbears | **Access:** guarded tribal interior

> The upper branch links Vargoth's private chamber, the cell's spoils, and the bugbear living room. A concealed stone route continues into the labyrinth beyond.
