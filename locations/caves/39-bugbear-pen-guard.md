# 39. Bugbear Pen Guard Room

**Faction:** Vargoth's cell | **Role:** slave-pens guard and provisioning station | **Keys:** areas 40 and 41

> Five armed bugbears control a room of bedrolls, meal sacks, a long table, and watered wine. Keys hang on the wall opposite the stairs.

## Run this area

- Two males: AC 5, HD 3+1, hp 10, damage 2d4, ML 9; each has 2d8 gp.
- Three females: AC 6, HD 2, hp 7, damage 1d8, ML 8; each has 1d10 gp.
- All carry a throwing spear in addition to normal weapons.
- The wall keys open both pen doors and the common chains.
- Campaign evidence belongs here: a practical stock tally with counts, dates, feeding allotments, an Iron Ring fitting, and a route glyph. Add “D. Sokol” or a charcoal-burner's mark if using Mara's brother clue.

## Exits

- [Go outward to the Welcome Passage](h-entry-passage.md) - Return toward the false hospitality signs.
- [Go up toward the Upper Living Passage](h-upper-passage.md) - Reach Vargoth and the bugbear household.
- [Enter the Slave-Pen Passage](h-lower-passage.md) - Take the keys toward areas 40 and 41.
