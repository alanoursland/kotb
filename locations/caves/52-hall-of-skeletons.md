# 52. Hall of Skeletons

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Role:** false-static audience hall | **Trigger:** touching dais or throne

> A throne-like chair set with four red gems stands on a southern dais. Twelve armored skeletons rest motionless against the walls.

## Run this area

- Initial turning attempts do nothing because the skeletons are not yet animated.
- Touching the dais or chair animates all twelve: AC 7, HD 1, hp 3, sword 1d6, ML 12.
- Protective amulets make them turn as zombies rather than skeletons.
- Afterward, four 500 gp garnets can be pried from the chair.
- Robes and iron-ring symbols should resemble the Shrine's other trappings but not recognized Traladaran liturgy.

## Exits

- [Return to the Outer Shrine Hall](k-outer-hall.md) - Leave the dais and motionless ranks behind.
