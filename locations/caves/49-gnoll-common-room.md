# 49. Gnoll Common Room

**Faction:** Maruk's gnolls | **Population:** 6 adult males, 11 adult females, 18 young

> Crude, battered furnishings surround a large population of adults and young. Nothing about the room resembles a raiding headquarters.

## Run this area

- Six males: AC 5, HD 2, hp 8, damage 2d4, ML 8; each has 1d6 ep and 1d6 sp.
- Eleven females: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 5, damage 1d8, ML 8; each has 1d10 sp.
- Eighteen young do not fight.
- There is no valuable room treasure.
- This is the cleanest-sheet faction made visible: families, ordinary clutter, and no hidden atrocity. Do not add one to reassure player expectations.
