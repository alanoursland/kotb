# Cave I — Labyrinth Entrance

**Zone:** Khaeros's labyrinth | **Access:** open | **Hazard:** magical direction confusion after 30 feet

> Loose equipment and low-value coins lie near the mouth. Deeper in, the branching stone seems to turn underfoot, and remembered directions stop agreeing with one another.

## Run this passage

- After 30 feet, begin naming incorrect or inconsistent directions. The physical links on these pages remain true; the compass words spoken to players do not.
- Every couple of turns, a mapping character may secretly save on a 19–20 to shake off the effect.
- Brundt knows how to reach Khaeros socially but is not immune to the labyrinth. Khaeros can guide anyone he chooses.
