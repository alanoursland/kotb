# 5. Yiznak's Chamber

**Faction:** kobolds | **Resident:** [Yiznak the Deepwright](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#kobold-chief--yiznak-the-deepwright) | **Role:** leadership and negotiation

> Battered furniture, cloth heaps, and repaired objects fill a surprisingly deliberate room. A great golden chain and gem mark the old kobold with the battle axe as chief.

## People and stats

- Yiznak uses the B2 chief: AC 5, HD 2, hp 8, battle axe 2d4, ML 8.
- Five adult kobolds: AC 7, HD 1/2, hp 2, damage 1d3, ML 8; each carries 1d6 gp.

## Run this area

- Yiznak wants the party to recognize engineering, excavation, traps, and maintenance as valuable work. Respect opens negotiations faster than flattery.
- He carries the key to [area 3](03-kobold-food-store.md).
- His neck chain and gem are worth 1,200 gp. Fifty gp are sewn into a hanging blanket's hem.
- Locked chest: 203 cp, 61 sp, 22 ep.
- A practical food-and-work agreement can be drafted here: measures of grain in exchange for route surveys, safe crossings, drainage, or labor.
