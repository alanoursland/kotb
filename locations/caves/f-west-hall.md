# Cave F — Western Hall

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Access:** interior military corridor

> This broad worked hall links the stronghold's communal rooms, prison-kitchen, armory stores, and the stairs used to supervise the goblin ward.

## Exits

- [Go toward the Entry Guard Room](26-hobgoblin-entry-guard.md) - Return toward the barred outer door.
- [Enter the Prison-Kitchen](24-hobgoblin-prison-kitchen.md) - Follow the smell of smoke and the sound of chains.
- [Enter the Feast Hall](25-hobgoblin-feast-hall.md) - Go toward tables being set for the banquet.
- [Enter the Armory](27-hobgoblin-armory.md) - Take the guarded equipment-room door.
- [Enter the Stolen-Goods Storeroom](28-hobgoblin-storeroom.md) - Follow the supply corridor toward the goblin warehouse route.
- [Take the stairs to the Hobgoblin Common Room](23-hobgoblin-common-room.md) - Descend toward the connection with cave D.
