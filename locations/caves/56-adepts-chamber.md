# 56. Adepts' Chamber

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Occupants:** four cleric 2 adepts | **Role:** senior clergy quarters

> Four beds, stands, chairs, chests, and a table share space with evil books and scrolls. Black robes and maroon cowls conceal plate armor.

## Run this area

- Four cleric 2 adepts: AC 3, or effective AC 2 against good attackers, hp 8, mace 1d6, ML 8.
- Each has an *amulet of protection from good*, copper chain with bone skull clasp worth 40 gp, 20 gp, and 5 pp.
- Spells: two have *cause light wounds*, one *light*, one *cause fear*. They cast before entering melee when possible.
- If hard pressed, they flee to ring the great bell in area 58.
- Copper dishes and vessels on the table are worth 175 gp. Evil writings have no sale value; destroying them awards the module's 600 XP unless the destroyers are evil.
- Iron rings and the books' theology make the Traladaran disguise increasingly impossible to sustain.

## Exits

- [Return to the Temple Hall](k-temple-hall.md) - Leave the senior quarters.
- [Enter the Chapel of Evil Chaos](55-chapel-of-evil-chaos.md) - Go toward the lesser altar.
