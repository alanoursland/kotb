# 31. Hobgoblin Chief's Guard Room

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Role:** inner alarm relay | **Secret:** armory route

> Four chain-armored hobgoblins occupy a bare room with two pallets, a stool, and a large water barrel.

## Run this area

- Four guards: AC 5, HD 1+1, hp 5, damage 1d8, ML 8; each has 2d6 cp, 2d6 sp, and 2d6 ep.
- They relay warnings among areas 27, 29, and 30 as required.
- The room's real value is topology: the secret armory route lets defenders move behind an intrusion and keeps Karrgan connected to the stronghold's weapons.
- If Karrgan dies, survivors usually seek the goblin ward unless the entire ravine is visibly collapsing.

## Exits

- [Return to the Chief's Passage](f-chief-passage.md) - Go toward area 29 and Karrgan's quarters.
- [Enter the Inner Guard Room](29-hobgoblin-inner-guard.md) - Follow the alarm route.
- [Use the secret route to the Armory](27-hobgoblin-armory.md) - Pass through the concealed opening.
