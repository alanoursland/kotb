# 4. Kobold Inner Guard Room

**Faction:** Yiznak's kobolds | **Role:** missile choke point | **Alert:** warned from area 1

> An angled corner breaks the passage. Three unusually large kobolds crouch behind it with bows, using the stone as cover down the approach.

## Run this area

- Three large kobolds: AC 5, HD 1+1, hp 5 each, bow or hand axe for 1d6, ML 6; each carries 2d6 gp.
- Missile attacks against them suffer –2 while they remain behind the corner.
- They are the tribe's best-equipped defenders and know they are buying time for the common chamber to react.
- A declared truce turns the bend into a formal disarm-or-wait point. Yiznak comes here; strangers do not simply walk into the family rooms.
