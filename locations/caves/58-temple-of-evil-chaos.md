# 58. Temple of Evil Chaos

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Role:** main ritual hall and climax | **Alarm:** great black iron bell

> Polished black stone veined in red fills a vast arched chamber. Purple draperies cover dull black walls; three altars face a black dais, bone chairs, and a gemmed ivory throne beneath a mirror-smooth wall of translucent red stone.

## On entry

- Eight black candelabras ignite with red radiance. Colored forms dance across the western wall.
- Anyone watching the forms for more than a moment saves vs. Spells or chants a hymn to chaos.
- Three chanting voices ring the bell magically; even one alerts Vesna's guards.
- Eight zombie guards arrive 3 rounds after entry even if the party remains quiet.

## Bell response

- The bell mobilizes [area 57](57-hall-of-undead-warriors.md): skeletons line the south wall and zombies the north.
- Destroying relics in area 55 also rings it.

## Treasure and evidence

- Throne: ten 100 gp black gems, ten 500 gp red gems, one 1,000 gp red gem.
- The pure-chaos signs, iron rings, and Thanatos rites are not authentic Traladaran worship. Father Branislav can name that distinction.
