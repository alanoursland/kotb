# Cave D — Goblin Entry Passage

**Faction:** Karrgan's goblin ward | **Access:** busy and patrolled | **Primary use:** junction to goblin rooms and hobgoblin stairs

> Natural cave quickly becomes worked stone. Small feet, shouted messages, and the scrape of sacks make this the busiest entrance in the ravine.

## Run this passage

- Until the wandering patrol is encountered, check each 30 feet traveled: 3-in-6 chance of six armed goblins carrying 1d6 bags of decent food.
- The goblins shout “Bree-Yark!” while attacking, spreading the alarm immediately.
- This ward is politically tied to Karrgan's hobgoblins. It is not a separate federation negotiation unless you want its local headman to demand one.
