# 6. Kobold Common Chamber

**Faction:** kobolds | **Population:** 17 adult males, 23 adult females, 8 young | **Role:** settlement heart

> Cloth, patched containers, tools, scraps, and sleeping nests fill a crowded family chamber. This is plainly a home, not a barracks.

## Run this area

- Seventeen males: AC 7, HD 1/2, hp 3, damage 1d4, ML 6; each has 1d6 sp.
- Twenty-three females: AC 7, HD 1/2, hp 2, damage 1d3, ML 6; each has 1d4 sp.
- Eight young do not attack. Able adults defend them if the lair is invaded.
- One piece of silk worth 150 gp is lost among the scraps and found only by searching.
- Oil causes the kobolds to retreat if possible. If attackers teach them to use flaming oil and leave supplies behind, they will copy the tactic.
- This room is the party's best visual argument against treating area A as disposable. Food, safety, and recognition affect forty-eight lives here.
