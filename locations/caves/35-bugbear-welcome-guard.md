# 35. Bugbear Welcome Guard Room

**Faction:** Vargoth's Iron Ring cell | **Role:** false hospitality ambush | **Alarm:** large gong

> Three bugbears lounge beside a smoking brazier where meat cooks on skewers. Two cots and a large gong stand behind them.

## Run this area

- Three bugbears: AC 5, HD 3+1, hp 11, damage 2d4, ML 9; each carries 2d10 gp.
- They reach for food rather than maces, eat a piece, then offer skewers to guests. Unless the targets remain alert, the skewers become surprise weapons with +2 to hit.
- If losing, one strikes the gong and alerts the entire lair.
- In campaign play, the performance is part of a repeated intake procedure. They may delay violence long enough to learn names, numbers, affiliations, and whether anyone would pay ransom.
- A party arriving openly to negotiate is not safe; Vargoth's people treat parley as intelligence collection.
