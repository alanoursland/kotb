# 42. Stirge Cave

**Zone:** Khaeros's labyrinth | **Occupants:** thirteen hungry stirges

> Squeaks and hooting echo ahead before a cloud of leathery shapes drops from the darkness.

## Run this area

- Thirteen stirges: AC 7, HD 1, hp 3, attack at +2, ML 9.
- First hit deals 1d3; once attached, each automatically drains 1d4 hp per round until killed or the victim dies.
- They are noisy 90% of the time, so the party is unlikely to be surprised.
- Khaeros catches and eats stirges; they avoid him and are exceptionally hungry.
- No treasure.

## Exits

- [Return toward the Labyrinth Entrance](i-labyrinth-entry.md) - Follow remembered landmarks; stated directions may be false.
- [Go deeper into the Labyrinth](i-labyrinth-depths.md) - Continue toward beetle-light and Khaeros.
