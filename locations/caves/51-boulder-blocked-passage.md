# 51. Boulder-Filled Passage

**Zone:** Shrine of Evil Chaos | **Role:** sealed escape or expansion route | **Status:** blocked

> Large rocks and boulders pack the passage from wall to wall. A faint draft passes through gaps too small for a person.

## Run this area

- Clearing a human-sized route requires 100 man-turns of labor.
- B2 leaves the far destination to the DM: an exterior opening southwest of the Caves or a long prepared route to the Cave of the Unknown.
- Vesna knows this route exists. In the printed baseline the chief priest uses *gaseous form* to pass or escape toward it; in the campaign, Vesna may use it to reposition before choosing suicide-ascension rather than capture.
- Freshly disturbed dust, hidden tools, or a narrow gas-sized gap can signal that the blockage is an escape plan rather than a dead geological feature.

- [Clear a route toward the southwest borderland](../../campaign/the-borderland.md) - Conditional; requires 100 man-turns and the DM's chosen destination.
