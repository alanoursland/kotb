# 20. Goblin Headman's Room

**Faction:** Karrgan's goblin ward | **Role:** local leadership under the hobgoblin alliance

> Goblin-sized furniture of surprising quality fills the room. Bows hang on the wall; a low bench and a pewter bowl sit near the chief's bed.

## People and stats

- Ward-chief: AC 4, HD 3, hp 11, damage 2–7 from strength and skill, ML 9; carries 18 gp and 2 pp.
- Three guards: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 7, damage 1d6, ML 9; each has 8 ep and 1d6 sp.
- Two adult females can fight as AC 7, HD 1-1, hp 2, damage 1d6 if the room is hard pressed.

## Run this area

- In this campaign the headman handles the goblin ward's internal affairs while Karrgan dominates military policy. He is an intermediary, not a second federation chief.
- Silver cup under the bed: 90 gp.
- Secret drawer beneath the low bench: gold-and-silver-thread tapestry worth 900 gp.
- Pewter bowl: 273 sp and 321 cp.

- [Return to the Goblin Living Passage](d-inner-passage.md) - Leave through the visible door.
- [Enter the Goblin Common Room](19-goblin-common-room.md) - Go toward the ward's families.
