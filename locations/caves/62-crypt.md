# 62. The Crypt

**Faction:** Shrine dead | **Access:** bolted door | **Threat:** wight in the sixth opened tomb

> A long, low-ceilinged hall of rough stone contains coffins and large sarcophagi belonging to servants of the temple.

## Run this area

- The door is bolted shut from outside.
- The sixth tomb opened contains a wight: AC 5, HD 3, hp 13, touch drains one level, ML 12.
- No ordinary grave goods lie with the other remains.
- Secret compartment in the wight's tomb: sword +2, *scroll of protection from undead*, *helm of alignment change*, and gem-pommeled silver dagger worth 800 gp.
- The crypt can turn reckless post-battle looting into one final severe encounter. Telegraph the sixth tomb's disturbed seal if the table needs a fair warning.

## Exits

- [Return to the Lower Shrine Hall](k-lower-hall.md) - Unbolt the door and leave the tombs.
