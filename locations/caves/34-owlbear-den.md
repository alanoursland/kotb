# 34. Owlbear Den

**Zone:** Shunned Cavern | **Occupant:** owlbear | **Campaign use:** trust-earning threat removal

> Sticks, bones, feathers, and a half-digested gnoll make a foul nest in the southern end of the cavern.

## Run this area

- Owlbear: AC 5, HD 5, hp 30, three attacks for 1d8/1d8/1d8, ML 9.
- It sleeps after a dawn kill until disturbed, then roars and rushes intruders.
- No coin treasure. A bone tube hidden in the nest holds a *scroll of protection from undead*; each searcher has a 1-in-6 chance per round to notice it.
- The scroll is unusually useful against the Shrine and can reward a party that helps the cave residents before confronting the cult.
- Returning proof of the kill earns practical trust from residents who have lost hunters and foragers here.
