# 9. Orc Banquet Area

**Faction:** Gorrak's orcs | **Role:** court, meals, and assembly

> Tables and benches fill a thirty-by-fifty-foot chamber. Charcoal glows in a great south-wall fireplace; a large chair heads the northern table.

## Run this area

- The hall is normally empty at first contact, though fighting at the entrance can turn it into an ambush position.
- After an initial hostile incursion, four orcs from area 10 take crossbows here and prepare to fire on returning intruders.
- Under truce, this is where Gorrak receives outsiders before allowing them into private quarters. Put the party on one side of the long table and his armed witnesses on the other.
- Food scarcity is visible in what is not being cooked: a large hall, a small charcoal fire, and little smell of meat.
