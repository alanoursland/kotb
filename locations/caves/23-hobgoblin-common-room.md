# 23. Hobgoblin Common Room

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Population:** 5 adult males, 8 adult females, 3 young | **Role:** goblin-border barracks

> Cloth and skin beds, crude furniture, buckets, and a small beer barrel fill the room. Armed males watch the door toward the goblin ward.

## Run this area

- Five males: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 5, damage 1d8, ML 8; each has 4d10 sp.
- Eight females: AC 7, HD 1, hp 4, damage 1d6, ML 7; each has 2d6 sp.
- Three young do not fight.
- The males are battle-ready against trouble from cave D. This is another sign that the combined polity is an alliance with coercive edges, not a harmonious household.

## Exits

- [Take the stairs to Goblin Guard Room 17](17-goblin-entry-guard.md) - Climb into cave D's defended junction.
- [Enter the Hobgoblin Western Hall](f-west-hall.md) - Go deeper into Karrgan's stronghold.
