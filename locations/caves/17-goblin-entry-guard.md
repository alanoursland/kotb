# 17. Goblin Entry Guard Chamber

**Faction:** Karrgan's goblin ward | **Role:** junction defense | **Threat watched:** entrance and hobgoblin stairs

> Six spear-armed goblins watch two passages from a chamber furnished with a small table, benches, water keg, and a barrel of spare spears.

## Run this area

- Six goblins: AC 6, HD 1-1, hp 3, spear 1d6, ML 7; each has 4d10 cp and 1d4 sp.
- The barrel contains sixty spears.
- They are alert for any intruder, including trouble from the hobgoblins to the south. In campaign terms, the goblin ward is allied to Karrgan but still watches its powerful patron.
- If the goblins flee a losing fight, survivors use the stairs to area 23 and join the hobgoblins.

## Exits

- [Go outward to the Goblin Entry Passage](d-entrance-passage.md) - Return toward cave mouth D.
- [Continue to the Ogre-Side Guard Chamber](18-goblin-ogre-guard.md) - Follow the main worked corridor deeper.
- [Take the stairs to the Hobgoblin Common Room](23-hobgoblin-common-room.md) - Descend into cave F's connected ward.
