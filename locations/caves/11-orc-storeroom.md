# 11. Orc Storage Chamber

**Faction:** Gorrak's orcs | **Access:** locked | **Role:** supplies and reserve weapons

> Stacks of ordinary supplies surround a rack of well-kept spears, axes, shields, and an old crate in the far corner.

## Contents

- Three shields, seventeen spears, and two battle axes in excellent condition.
- A forgotten crossbow and sixty bolts in the northeast crate.
- No other valuables.

## Run this area

- The door is locked. Opening it without permission during parley is treated as an attempt to disarm the tribe.
- In a trade agreement, this room identifies immediate needs: food and replacement materials matter more than coin.

## Exits

- [Return to the Inner Living Passage](b-inner-passage.md) - Leave through the locked door.
