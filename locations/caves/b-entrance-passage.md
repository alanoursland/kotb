# Cave B — Skull Passage

**Faction:** Gorrak's orcs | **Access:** watched through a false trophy niche | **Primary use:** three-way entrance junction

> Human, elven, dwarven, and one orcish head fill niches on the wall. Activity sounds from the deeper left-hand passage; the right-hand way is quiet.

## Run this passage

- A concealed watcher poses as one of the heads. On seeing intruders, the orc substitutes a goblin head and warns area 7.
- The heads are deterrence and factional messaging. The orc head is evidence of the feud inside the caves, not random decoration.
