# 28. Hobgoblin Storeroom

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Role:** stolen goblin goods | **Secret:** route to area 21

> Selected food and drink taken from the goblin warehouse wait here under guard, separated from the common stores.

## Run this area

- One standing guard: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 5, damage 1d8, ML 8; carries 2d8 ep.
- If the goblin warehouse was not interrupted, add four returning looters: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 6, damage 1d8, ML 9; 1d4 gp each.
- The supplies have practical value but no special sale value.
- Finding the secret route and the curated goods gives the party leverage with the goblin ward and a warning about Karrgan's governance.

## Exits

- [Return to the Western Hall](f-west-hall.md) - Go toward the stronghold's public interior.
- [Use the secret stairs to Goblin Storage](21-goblin-storage.md) - Follow the concealed theft route upward.
