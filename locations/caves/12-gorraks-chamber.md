# 12. Gorrak's Chamber

**Faction:** Gorrak's orcs | **Resident:** [Gorrak](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#orc-chiefs--gorrak--ushka-feuding) | **Secret:** route to area 13

> Carpets, tapestries, battered serviceable furniture, and a cot make this the best-appointed room in the lair. One hanging conceals a western cache; another hides a desperate escape.

## People and stats

- Gorrak: AC 3 with chain and shield +1, attacks as HD 4, hp 15, mace 1d6+2, ML appropriate to chief; 31 gp and a 700 gp gem ring.
- Two mates: AC 7, HD 1, hp 3, damage 1d6, ML 8; each has 2d6 gp.

## Run this area

- Gorrak is the hardline orc chief: explicit deterrence, visible strength, and suspicion first.
- Western cache: two suits of chain (human and dwarf), four swords, and locked chest with 205 cp, 286 sp, 81 gp, 13 pp. A boulder-covered niche holds a healing potion and a scroll of *fireball*.
- If his life is in grave danger, he works the concealed southern catch and seeks Ushka through [area 13](13-orc-moot-room.md).
- Finding the catch requires two simultaneous 1-in-6 successes or two consecutive successes by one searcher.

## Exits

- [Return to the Inner Living Passage](b-inner-passage.md) - Leave through the visible entrance.
- [Enter the Orc Common Room](10-orc-common-room.md) - Go toward the tribe's families.
- [Use the secret door to the Orc Moot Room](13-orc-moot-room.md) - Only after discovering and operating the concealed catch.
