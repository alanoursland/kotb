# 53. Zombie Guard Room

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Occupants:** eight zombies | **Access test:** temple robes and matching amulet

> Eight hulking corpses stand four to either end of the hall, filthy red-and-black uniforms hanging over dead flesh. Each grips a cleaver-like battle axe.

## Run this area

- Eight zombies: AC 8, HD 2, hp 8, battle axe 1d8, ML 12.
- Their amulets make turning attempts treat them as ghouls.
- They attack anyone not wearing temple garb and a matching amulet. Vesna can command them to attack disguised intruders anyway.
- The guards carry no treasure. Their amulets are evidence: iron-ring workmanship and alien religious symbols, meaningful only when compared with other clues or shown to Father Branislav.

## Exits

- [Return to the Outer Shrine Hall](k-outer-hall.md) - Go toward area 52 and the entrance.
- [Advance into the Temple Hall](k-temple-hall.md) - Pass the standing guards into the inner Shrine.
