# Cave K — Shrine Entrance Passage

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Access:** open but watched by undead response | **Primary use:** outer approach

> A foul draft leaves a twenty-foot cave mouth beyond twisted, bloated trees. Red strata and bulging black veins run through hewn walls. Footsteps echo in the deathly stillness.

## Run this passage

- Continuous noise brings eight zombie guards to investigate.
- The zombies wear protection from turning and count as ghouls for turning attempts.
- Temple robes and matching amulets can defeat some standing guards, but not Vesna's direct commands.
