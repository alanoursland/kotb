# 1. Kobold Guard Room

**Faction:** Yiznak's kobolds | **Role:** outer defense and pit crossing | **Alert:** responds to the pit

> Six small spear-carriers wait beyond the trapped approach. Rough planks lean against one wall, ready to bridge the pit outside.

## Run this area

- Six kobolds: AC 7, HD 1/2, hp 3 each, spear 1d4, ML 6; each carries 1d6 sp.
- If they win initiative, they throw spears in the first round. One runner warns [area 4](04-kobold-inner-guard-room.md) and [area 6](06-kobold-common-chamber.md).
- Under safe conduct, this is a checkpoint. The guards lay the planks, demand names and business, and escort visitors toward Yiznak rather than surrendering their defensive advantage.
- The planks are the obvious safe route across the [entrance pit](a-entrance-passage.md).

## Exits

- [Go outward to the Trapped Entrance Passage](a-entrance-passage.md) - Use the stored planks to cross the pit.
- [Follow the side opening toward the Giant Rats](02-giant-rat-refuse.md) - Go toward refuse stink and scratching.
- [Go deeper into the Inner Passage](a-inner-passage.md) - Continue toward stores, inner guards, and the tribe.
