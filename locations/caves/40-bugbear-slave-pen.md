# 40. Mixed Slave Pen

**Faction:** Vargoth's cell | **Access:** barred, chained, and padlocked iron door | **Role:** mixed captive stock

> Straw and a waste bucket lie behind an iron door. A single heavy chain binds captives of several peoples to the wall without distinction.

## Prisoners

- Three kobolds: AC 9, HD 1/2, hp 2.
- One goblin: AC 9, HD 1-1, hp 3.
- Four orcs: AC 9, HD 1, hp 5.
- Two human fighters: AC 9, F1, hp 4.
- Optional substitution: one Stronghollow dwarf from Highforge and two Callarii or Vyalia elves for two kobolds and one orc.

## Run this area

- All fight bugbears if armed. Humans may serve rescuers; optional dwarf and elves stay while the party remains in the Caves. Others leave at the first safe opportunity.
- Treat everyone as AC 9 unless equipped.
- The shared chain is the visual point: Vargoth's market recognizes no human–“monster” boundary. Everyone is stock.
- Buying the captives rewards the cell and funds replacements. Rescue, seizure of records, and destruction of the intake operation are the meaningful solutions.

## Exits

- [Return to the Slave-Pen Passage](h-lower-passage.md) - Leave through the iron door.
