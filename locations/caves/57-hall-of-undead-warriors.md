# 57. Hall of Undead Warriors

**Faction:** cult of Thanatos | **Occupants:** twenty skeletons and twenty zombies | **Trigger:** great bell or intrusion

> Four files of armored dead stand at attention: two ranks of skeletons facing south and two ranks of zombies facing north.

## Forces

- Twenty skeletons: AC 6, HD 1, hp 3, damage 1d6, ML 12; turn as zombies.
- Twenty zombies: AC 5 in chain, HD 2, hp 8, damage 1d8, ML 12; turn as ghouls.

## Run this area

- When area 58's bell sounds, skeletons leave by the south door and line the temple's south wall; zombies take the north exit and line its north wall.
- They attack intruders in this room, the temple passage, or area 58.
- Correct garments and amulets prevent attack unless Vesna orders otherwise.
- They carry no treasure. In an eight-hour run, treat the ranks as tactical waves activated by the bell rather than forty separate searches.

## Exits

- [Return to the Temple Hall](k-temple-hall.md) - Go back toward the clerical chambers.
- [Enter the Temple of Evil Chaos](58-temple-of-evil-chaos.md) - Follow the doors used by the undead formations.
