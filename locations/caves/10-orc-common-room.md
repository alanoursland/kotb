# 10. Gorrak's Orc Common Room

**Faction:** Gorrak's orcs | **Population:** 12 adult males, 18 adult females, 9 young

> Sparse furnishings and crowded sleeping places fill the tribal quarters. This room holds families as well as fighters.

## Run this area

- Twelve males: AC 7, HD 1, hp 4, damage 1d6, ML 8; each has 2d6 sp.
- Eighteen females and nine young do not normally fight and carry nothing of value.
- The room's furnishings are not valuable.
- If the lair has already been attacked, four males may be posted with crossbows in [area 9](09-orc-banquet-hall.md).
- This is the humanizing counterweight to the heads at the entrance: Gorrak's deterrent violence protects a community, though it does not excuse every method.

## Exits

- [Return to the Banquet Area](09-orc-banquet-hall.md) - Go outward toward the charcoal hearth.
- [Enter the Inner Living Passage](b-inner-passage.md) - Continue toward stores and Gorrak's chamber.
- [Approach Gorrak's Chamber](12-gorraks-chamber.md) - Take the leader's side of the living complex.
