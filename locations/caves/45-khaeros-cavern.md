# 45. Khaeros's Cavern

**Resident:** [Khaeros](../../characters/khaeros-the-minotaur.md) | **Role:** lethal first contact, possible ally, and living evidence | **Knowledge:** the secret route between areas 43 and 36

> Skulls and bones form deliberate patterns rather than a feeding heap. An enormous armored minotaur moves with trained speed, enchanted spear leveled before questions can begin.

## Khaeros

- AC 4, HD 6, hp 35, ML 12. First round spear +1 for 1d6+3; later spear or gore/bite for 1d6/1d6.
- He attacks first unless the party uses the approach in [First Contact — the Minotaur](../../campaign/first-contact-the-minotaur.md), preferably with Brundt as the proof of safety.
- He is an escaped Iron Ring captive, not Vargoth's ally.
- He knows that the concealed passage in [area 43](43-fire-beetle-cave.md) reaches Vargoth's chamber and can guide allies to it. Bugbears entering the labyrinth through that route are enemies.

## Hidden journey chest

The stone slab requires at least three humans to move. Behind it:

- Poison-needle chest: 930 gp and 310 ep.
- *Staff of healing*
- human- or elf-sized *plate +1*.
- Locked coffer: potions of *gaseous form*, *healing*, and *growth*.
- Locked chest: jewelry worth 1,600 gp, 900 gp, and 600 gp.

Read it as passage money and survival equipment, not a monster hoard.

## Exits

- [Return to the Labyrinth Depths](i-labyrinth-depths.md) - Leave by the route only Khaeros navigates reliably.
