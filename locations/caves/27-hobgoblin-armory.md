# 27. Hobgoblin Armory

**Faction:** Karrgan's hobgoblins | **Role:** military stores | **Secret:** route to area 31

> Armor stands on racks while weapons fill chests, pegs, and wall frames. Three chain-armored guards control the room.

## Guards

- Three hobgoblins: AC 5, HD 1+1, hp 6, damage 1d8, ML 8; each carries 2d4 sp and 2d4 ep.
- On warning, two ambush at the door while one uses the secret route to area 31.

## Inventory

- Plate: one human, one dwarf. Chain: three human, two elf. Seven human leather armors; eleven shields; forty-two helmets.
- Six daggers, one battle axe, four maces, three swords, two shortbows, one longbow, thirteen crossbows.
- 220 arrows, including fourteen silver-headed; 180 bolts; fifty-one spears; nineteen pole arms.

## Run this area

- This stockpile is why Karrgan is the federation's natural officer—and its greatest internal danger if over-empowered.

- [Follow the secret route directly to the Chief's Guard Room](31-hobgoblin-chief-guard.md) - Use the concealed connection the armory guard takes to raise the alarm.
