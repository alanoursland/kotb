# 3. Kobold Food Storage Room

**Faction:** Yiznak's kobolds | **Access:** locked | **Key:** carried by Yiznak

> Sacks, boxes, barrels, and low piles hold dried meat, grain, salted vegetables, and a large cask of thin, vinegary wine.

## Run this area

- The door is locked. [Yiznak](05-yiznaks-chamber.md) carries the key.
- There is no valuable treasure. Some meat includes remains of former human victims; do not let that single fact erase the visible staple food or the tribe's many dependents.
- For the campaign's famine, show the room running low: scrape marks where containers once stood, carefully retied sacks, and portions measured by a small hand.
- This is the clearest kobold proof that food aid matters. Yiznak will discuss quantities here more readily than ideals.

## Exits

- [Return to the Inner Passage](a-inner-passage.md) - Leave through the locked door.
