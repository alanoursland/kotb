# Cave H Population — Vargoth's Iron Ring Cell

**Faction:** Vargoth's bugbears (Iron Ring) | **Population:** 25 bugbears—22 adults and 3 young | **Leader:** [Vargoth "the Welcomer"](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#bugbear-chief--vargoth-the-welcomer) | **Prisoners:** 17 across two pens

> **At a glance:** 20 adults and 3 young are currently inside cave H. Two more adults are out hunting and return the day after the party first enters, giving the cell 25 bugbears in all. Seventeen captives of seven peoples are chained in two pens, including Skarn and “Sir Garran.” This is not a tribe with a slave problem; slaving is the business.

Use this sheet for the intake pipeline, the pen manifests, and what dismantling the cell leaves you holding. Room-by-room details stay on the area sheets.

### Count reconciliation

The map rooms contain 20 adults and 3 young. B2 separately places two adults out hunting, accounting for the campaign roster's **22 adult bugbears**. Adding the three young gives 25 bugbears total. Captives are never part of the faction count. Area 38's dependents are the point: dismantling a cell creates custody, resettlement, and accountability problems after the fight.

## Named figures

- [Vargoth "the Welcomer"](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#bugbear-chief--vargoth-the-welcomer): AC 5, HD 4+1, hp 18, damage 1d10+2, ML 9; pouch with the spoils-room key, 29 pp, and three 50 gp gems. Hand axe +1 on the wall — he throws it before closing. Secret stone route toward area 43; he may flee through it. Holds a packet of Eagles, collection dates, and one destination glyph. The other five chiefs are politics; he is a fight.
- [Skarn](../../characters/skarn-the-rebel-bugbear.md): AC 7, HD 3+1, hp 14, Save F3, ML 9 — caged in area 41 for rejecting the Iron Ring oath. Fights Vargoth's cell and remains with the party while they stay in the Caves.

## Prisoners

**Keys:** both pen doors and the common chains open with the keys on the wall of [area 39](39-bugbear-pen-guard.md), opposite the stairs. **Buying captives rewards the cell and funds replacements** — rescue, seizure of records, and destruction of the intake operation are the meaningful solutions. See [The Prisoners](../../campaign/the-prisoners.md).

### [40. Mixed Slave Pen](40-bugbear-slave-pen.md) — 10 captives

Barred, chained, padlocked iron door; one heavy chain binds captives of several peoples to the wall without distinction. Treat everyone as AC 9 unless equipped.

- Three kobolds: AC 9, HD 1/2, hp 2.
- One goblin: AC 9, HD 1-1, hp 3.
- Four orcs: AC 9, HD 1, hp 5.
- Two human fighters: AC 9, F1, hp 4.
- Optional substitution: one Stronghollow dwarf from Highforge and two Callarii or Vyalia elves for two kobolds and one orc.
- All fight bugbears if armed. Humans may serve rescuers; the optional dwarf and elves stay while the party remains in the Caves; others leave at the first safe opportunity.

### [41. Skarn's Slave Pen](41-skarns-slave-pen.md) — 7 captives

- Three hobgoblins: AC 8, HD 1+1, hp 6.
- Two gnolls: AC 8, HD 2+1, hp 9.
- [Skarn](../../characters/skarn-the-rebel-bugbear.md) — see Named figures.
- **"Sir Garran"**: human F4, AC 9, hp 24, +4 to hit, damage 1d6+3, ML 10. **Threat among captives:** 50% chance each combat round to strike an ally while berserk; afterward he murders rescuers for treasure or steals the best item if they are too strong.
- The other captives fight bugbears blocking escape and then flee.

## Alarm and reinforcement flow

1. [Area 35](35-bugbear-welcome-guard.md) plays host first: food offered, names, numbers, affiliations, and **whether anyone would pay ransom** — intake before violence.
2. If losing, one guard strikes the **gong** and alerts the entire lair.
3. Breaking into [area 37](37-bugbear-spoils-room.md) brings the area 35 guards and Vargoth's household from area 36.
4. [Area 39](39-bugbear-pen-guard.md) controls the [lower passage](h-lower-passage.md); its stock tally (counts, dates, feeding allotments, an Iron Ring fitting, a route glyph — add "D. Sokol" or a charcoal-burner's mark if using Mara's brother clue) is the evidence chain.

**Returning hunters:** the two absent adults return the day after the party first enters cave H, carrying a human corpse and 83 gp. If the lair remains occupied, they reinforce area 35. If the cell has fallen, they scout before entering and may flee with what they learn rather than obligingly walking into an empty guard room.

## Relations

- Everyone is stock: the welcome signs are written in Kobold, Orcish, Goblin, and other tongues; the pens hold seven peoples. Vargoth's market recognizes no human–"monster" boundary.
- [Brundt](22-brundts-cave.md) takes Vargoth's Eagles for muscle — his hoard and the bugbear pay sacks are two ends of the same trail.
- [Khaeros](45-khaeros-cavern.md) knows the secret back way from area 36 and treats armed bugbears using it as enemies.
- Do not turn the area 38 household into an argument that Vargoth is redeemable.
