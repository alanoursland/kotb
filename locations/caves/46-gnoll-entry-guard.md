# 46. Gnoll Entry Guard Room

**Faction:** Maruk's gnolls | **Role:** bow-covered entrance defense

> Four gnolls hold the point where natural cave becomes worked stone. Two keep bows trained down the approach.

## Run this area

- Four gnolls: AC 5, HD 2, hp 9, damage 2d4, ML 8; each has 1d8 cp, 1d8 sp, and 1d8 ep.
- Two shoot until melee, then run for help while the other two hold.
- Light or noise in the entrance gives them time to prepare.
- Maruk's guards are cautious but do not use false welcome or trophies. A halt, lowered weapons, and a request for terms can produce an escort instead of initiative.

## Exits

- [Go outward to the Gnoll Entrance Passage](j-entry-passage.md) - Return to cave mouth J.
- [Continue into the Gnoll Inner Passage](j-inner-passage.md) - Proceed toward the tribe and Maruk.
