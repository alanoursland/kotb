# 44. Second Fire-Beetle Cave

**Zone:** Khaeros's labyrinth | **Occupants:** two fire beetles

> A smaller branch ends in red light cast by two beetles moving across bare stone.

## Run this area

- Two fire beetles, identical to area 43: AC 4, HD 1+2, hp 7, bite 2d4, ML 7.
- Each yields three glands glowing in a 10-foot radius for 1d6 days.
- No treasure.
- If Khaeros is already friendly, he treats clearing these predators as practical housekeeping rather than heroism.

## Exits

- [Return to the Labyrinth Depths](i-labyrinth-depths.md) - Backtrack by stone shape and glow.
- [Go toward the First Fire-Beetle Cave](43-fire-beetle-cave.md) - Follow the broader lit branch.
