# 21. Goblin Storage Chamber

**Faction:** goblin ward | **Role:** warehouse | **Secret:** hobgoblin theft route to area 28

> Bales, crates, barrels, sacks, cloth, food, beer, and wine are stacked in disorder. Four crossbow guards watch from a marked post.

## Run this area

- Four goblin guards: AC 7, HD 1-1, hp 4, crossbow or sword 1d6, ML 7.
- The goods are useful but not individually valuable. Goblins continually add stolen and looted supplies.
- If anyone remains longer than 1 turn, four hobgoblins may emerge through the secret door to steal the best food and drink: AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 6, damage 1d8, ML 9; 1d4 gp each.
- The goblins do not know the route exists. Discovering it exposes the unequal alliance inside Karrgan's polity.

## Exits

- [Return to the Goblin Living Passage](d-inner-passage.md) - Go toward areas 18–20.
- [Enter the Goblin Common Room](19-goblin-common-room.md) - Follow the warehouse traffic back to the families.
- [Use the secret stairs to the Hobgoblin Storeroom](28-hobgoblin-storeroom.md) - Only after finding the hobgoblins' concealed route.
