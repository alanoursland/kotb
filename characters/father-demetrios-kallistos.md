# Father Demetrios Kallistos

**Role:** the Duke's cleric, assigned to the expedition · **Faith:** Church of Karameikos (convert from the Church of Thyatis) · **Heritage:** Thyatian · **Class/Level:** Cleric 4

> **What he's for (read this first):** Demetrios is the DM's voice in the field. He's the in-fiction reason communication is never a hard wall (the staff, below), the deliverer of exposition and the Church's framing, and a sanctioned nudge when the party stalls — all without breaking the fourth wall. He is **not** the party's leader or problem-solver. Keep his hands off player decisions; let him translate, contextualize, and occasionally *worry out loud.*

## Who he is

Demetrios was raised in the **Church of Thyatis** — the imperial faith, ordered and proud. He converted to the **Church of Karameikos** not out of disgrace but out of conviction: he came to believe the Duke's project — a nation that makes a place for unlike peoples instead of conquering them into sameness — was the truer expression of what faith is *for.* He is that rarest and most useful kind of believer: an idealist who chose his ideal with his eyes open, and serves it gladly.

That makes him an ideal envoy. He extends grace to the caves more readily than any soldier would, and he does it in good faith. If there's a gentle thematic undertone for the DM to play *if they want* — that his kindness naturally speaks in Karameikan terms, the only frame he has — it's just that: a quiet bit of character color, never duplicity and never a liability. He is exactly the ally he appears to be.

## How to play him at the table

- **Default to kindness and curiosity.** He wants the caves to succeed. He'll argue for the harder, better path when the party leans toward the sword.
- **Let him carry exposition you'd otherwise narrate.** Lore, history, what a symbol means, what the Church teaches — route it through Demetrios. He's your mouthpiece without you stepping out of the fiction.
- **Use him to nudge, not steer.** If the party is stuck, he can ask the question that reopens the door ("Did we ever learn what they *eat?*"). He does not make the plan.
- **He paraphrases warmly.** He'll naturally render a cave-dweller's blunt line into something more diplomatic — not to deceive, just because that's how he hears the world. Optional flavor; play it or don't.
- **His wisdom is real.** High WIS; his read on *people* is usually sound even when his read on *politics* is naive. Trust him on hearts, doubt him on systems.
- **One thing he can't do:** read the cult's symbols. Demetrios is Church of Karameikos, raised Thyatian — he may *sense* the Shrine is wrong, but he can't recognize the **iron-ring cord-trappings** as alien to Traladaran faith, and he's never heard the name *the Iron Ring.* That recognition belongs to a **Church of Traladar** cleric, not him. It's a gap, not a flaw — and a reason the party needs the Traladarans. See [progression beat 6](../narrative/progression.md).

## The staff (the communication device)

A plain-looking priest's staff, an heirloom of his conversion. On command it radiates **Speak Languages** as an *area* effect — everyone within roughly a parley's distance can understand and be understood, for as long as the conversation lasts.

- **Purpose:** removes "how do we even talk to them?" as a friction point. Parley is always *possible;* whether it *works* is the actual game.
- **Deliberately overpowered — and bounded by being his alone.** It's attuned to Demetrios (his faith / his WIS / call it what you like). The party can't pocket it and can't use it without him, so its power is also a *leash:* talking to the caves means bringing the Church along. Lose Demetrios and the wall slams back up — a real stake.
- **Soft limiter that turns the cheat into a feature:** it translates *words,* not *meaning.* Idiom, subtext, lies, and cultural framing still slip through — the ordinary friction of two peoples who don't share a worldview. The staff guarantees they *hear* each other; it does not guarantee they *understand.*

## Stats (B/X — light)

Fill in to taste; these are the load-bearing bits.

- **Cleric 4**, high **WIS** (15–16). Decent **AC** (chain + shield), modest **hp**.
- Spells: healing-forward (*Cure Light Wounds*, *Bless*, *Hold Person*). Note that *Speak Languages* is **not** a normal cleric spell — that's exactly why the **staff** exists.
- Not a combatant of note. He's here to keep people alive and keep people talking, in that order.

## DM notes

- **He's a tool, protect his utility.** Don't let players talk him into being a meat shield or a vending machine of *Cure* spells, and don't let him hog the spotlight. He works best as the conscience in the room.
- **His arc, if you want one:** the campaign can test his hope — let him meet a cave that lives up to it and one that doesn't, and watch a kind man learn that good intentions aren't enough without changing who he is. A story about an idealist growing wiser, not an ally turning suspect.
- **If he dies or is separated:** that's a genuine crisis (translation gone), not just a downed NPC. Worth foreshadowing so it lands.
