# Yiznak's Hearing — An Accusation Without a Battle

Yiznak gathers the involved surveyors, outer guards, one elder from the common chamber, and the party's chosen speakers in his room or at the damaged site. Weapons remain present but lowered.

## The four questions

Yiznak asks in this order:

1. **Who knew the site and schedule?**
2. **Who could obtain the human material found there?**
3. **Why does a missing kobold's tool appear in the damage?**
4. **What will the party do if the culprit is never proven?**

The fourth answer matters most. Yiznak is deciding whether the agreement can survive uncertainty, not conducting a modern criminal trial.

## What the party can establish immediately

- Their own movements and the escort's watch can be accounted for.
- Party arrows, wax, cord, and tools do not precisely match the planted objects.
- The collapse was caused by removed supports, not bad kobold design.
- The party gains nothing by destroying the project it funded.
- The missing tool means someone has access to a missing kobold or their possessions.

No single fact proves Vargoth did it.

## Helpful conduct

- Allow kobold examination of the party's equipment.
- Preserve the scene before clearing debris.
- Ask Yiznak's workers to explain the construction rather than speaking over them.
- Separate responsibility for protecting the agreement from admission of guilt.
- Offer repair, replacement food, and a mixed watch before demanding exoneration.

## Harmful conduct

- Threaten anyone who calls the material ducal.
- Claim kobolds sabotaged themselves without evidence.
- invoke the Duke as proof that ducal agents cannot be guilty;
- destroy planted objects in anger;
- insist talks resume before the ruined food or drainage is addressed.

## Resolving the hearing

### Cleared, culprit unknown

The party demonstrates inconsistency in the evidence and accepts practical responsibility for securing the next attempt. Continue with [Clearing Their Name Without the Culprit](11-clearing-their-name-without-the-culprit.md).

### Directed suspicion

### Unresolved

Yiznak does not attack. He suspends work, retains the original terms, and asks for replacement food or a neutral witness before the next meeting. Continue with [Delayed Talks](13-outcomes-and-follow-through.md#delayed-talks).
