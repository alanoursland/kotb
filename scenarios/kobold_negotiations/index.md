# Kobold Negotiations

**Faction:** [Yiznak's kobolds](../../locations/caves/population-kobolds.md) | **Leader:** [Yiznak the Deepwright](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#kobold-chief--yiznak-the-deepwright) | **Location:** [Cave A](../../locations/caves/a-entrance-passage.md) | **Campaign playbook:** [Faction Negotiations](../../campaign/faction-negotiations.md#yiznaks-kobolds)

> **What success means:** The kobolds receive food and direct political standing; their engineering becomes paid work; recognized travelers receive safe passage; hunger raids end; and the party undertakes to learn what happened to three missing kobolds. Discovering Vargoth's sabotage or rescuing the missing residents improves the agreement, but is **not required** to make it.

## Start

- [Set the scenario state](00-scenario-setup.md) — what Yiznak knows, what has changed already, and which opening the party creates.

## Player approaches

- [Approach as open emissaries](01-open-emissaries.md) — call from outside, accept kobold control of the entrance, and ask for Yiznak.
- [Lead with food and trade](02-food-first.md) — demonstrate immediate value without confusing one meal for a treaty.
- [Lead with engineering](03-engineering-first.md) — ask Yiznak to solve a real problem and treat the answer as professional work.
- [Invoke the Duke's authority](04-authority-or-ultimatum.md) — authority earns attention but not obedience; threats activate the defenses.
- [Use Brundt or the Concordant Staff](05-mediated-contact.md) — safe ways to open speech that do not settle the substance.
- [Recover from stealth, traps, or violence](06-after-a-hostile-entry.md) — a route back to diplomacy after a bad entrance.

Every viable approach eventually reaches [Negotiating the Agreement](07-negotiating-the-agreement.md).

## Order and complications

- [Apply order-dependent changes](08-order-variants.md) — kobolds first, or after Karrgan, Vargoth, the Shrine, or the food convoy.
- [Use the Broken Culvert sabotage](09-broken-culvert-sabotage.md) — an optional attempt by Vargoth and the cult to fracture a promising agreement.
- [Run Yiznak's hearing](10-yiznaks-hearing.md) — accusation as an investigation scene rather than an automatic battle.
- [Clear the party without identifying the culprit](11-clearing-their-name-without-the-culprit.md) — repair, transparency, fulfilled promises, and shared safeguards.
- [Follow the clues toward the bugbears](12-following-the-bugbear-clues.md) — an optional discovery path that points toward Cave H.

## Finish

- [Choose the outcome and follow-through](13-outcomes-and-follow-through.md) — full agreement, provisional agreement, delayed talks, or recovery after failure.
