# Following the Bugbear Clues

Investigation can expose Vargoth, but it is an optional reward for attention rather than a gate on kobold diplomacy.

## Clues at the culvert

Give clues according to what the characters examine. Three independent clues are enough to point toward Cave H; one or two create suspicion.

### Tracks and approach

Stelian or another tracker finds partial broad boot impressions made by very large humanoids. The approach came down from the upper ravine rather than from Riverfork's road. Brushed scree continues generally toward the bugbear cave.

### Structural damage

Brynja sees that the design did not fail. Supports were pulled sideways before the fitted stone was struck. The tool marks come from a kobold-made hammer, but the blows were delivered with much greater reach and force than a kobold normally applies.

### Cord and catnip

Packing cord caught under one support matches the knot and fiber used on trade bundles in the [bugbear spoils room](../../locations/caves/37-bugbear-spoils-room.md). Crushed catnip—relished by Vargoth's household—is caught in the same muddy fold.

### The missing tool

Yiznak recognizes the maker's mark of one of the three missing kobolds. This does not prove the owner participated. It suggests someone has the missing resident or their possessions.

### Planted ducal material

Danica notices that the wax, cloth, and arrowheads were assembled as a packet rather than dropped during work. Creases and residue show they traveled wrapped together. After the Shrine is searched, the materials match the [framing cache](../../locations/caves/63-shrine-storeroom.md), its wrapping scraps, or the corresponding intact hobgoblin packet.

### A witness

If useful, the bruised watcher remembers a large shaggy outline, the smell of strong herb, or two intruders using a few Goblin words. The witness did not see enough to identify an individual.

## Degrees of discovery

### Suspicion of large humanoids

Tracks and force rule out an ordinary human work crew. Yiznak accepts that the party was framed but does not yet name Vargoth.

### Direction toward Cave H

The route, cord, catnip, and welcome-system materials identify the bugbear lair as the likely source. Yiznak asks for reconnaissance or rescue, not immediate extermination.

### Proof of collaboration

Any one of these completes the local case:

- rescue the three kobolds and recover their stolen tools;
- seize Vargoth's pen tally;
- obtain Skarn's testimony;
- find Vesna's correspondence and framing packets;
- match the guest-room traces in the Shrine to Cave H.

This proves the cult–bugbear operation but not the whole Black Eagle conspiracy.

## Effects on negotiation

- Suspicion is enough to clear the party if paired with repair or fulfilled terms.
- Proof gives Yiznak exceptional trust and makes the returned kobolds witnesses.
- The kobolds may provide route knowledge or lookouts but do not volunteer to assault Cave H.
- Success against Vargoth does not eliminate the need for food, work, direct standing, and safe-passage terms.
