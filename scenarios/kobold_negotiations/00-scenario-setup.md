# Scenario Setup — What Is True When the Party Arrives

Use this page before opening whichever approach the players choose.

## Yiznak's starting position

- Cave A contains 63 adults, eight young, and eighteen giant rats.
- The [food store](../../locations/caves/03-kobold-food-store.md) is running low. Containers have been emptied, sacks carefully retied, and portions measured down.
- Three kobolds have disappeared. Vargoth holds them in the [upper bugbear slave pen](../../locations/caves/40-bugbear-slave-pen.md), but Yiznak assumes they were lost to predators, raiders, or the wilderness.
- Yiznak knows his people's excavation, traps, drainage, and repair are useful. He expects larger peoples to notice only the traps.
- He wants peace if it is materially safer than raiding, but he will not accept Karrgan or a human officer speaking for him.

## What the kobolds know about the party

Choose the most advanced state that applies:

| State | What Cave A has heard |
|---|---|
| **Unknown** | Armed strangers entered the ravine. Tree watchers report numbers and visible equipment. |
| **Brundt's guests** | Brundt says the strangers pay, talk, and have the Duke behind them. He does not guarantee their judgment. |
| **Known negotiators** | Another chief has met them. Yiznak wants to know whether that agreement places kobolds beneath someone else. |
| **Food bringers** | A delivery or convoy has occurred. Yiznak cares whether his people received food directly. |
| **Enemies of Vargoth** | Prisoners, records, or Shrine papers connect the party to the missing kobolds. |

Apply the details in [Order Variants](08-order-variants.md) after choosing the state.

## What gets Yiznak's attention

- calling from outside instead of testing the trap with a body;
- speaking Kobold or using the staff to establish language;
- meaningful food displayed as trade, not thrown as bait;
- asking what the kobolds build or maintain;
- requesting safe conduct and accepting an escort;
- returning a missing kobold or carrying evidence that one is alive.

## What alarms him

- entering silently after spotting the pit;
- cutting trees around the cave mouth;
- calling the kobolds vermin, servants, or Karrgan's subjects;
- demanding the traps be dismantled before talks;
- presenting the Duke's authority as ownership of the cave;
- offering a single sack as payment for permanent obedience.
