# Approach — Brundt or the Concordant Staff

Both mediators can make first contact safer. Neither can make the decision for Yiznak.

## Brundt as introducer

Brundt stands outside Cave A and calls badly phrased assurances in whichever shared words he has:

> “Small diggers! These ones pay. Talk before traps.”

The kobolds know that Brundt reports transactions accurately and moral questions poorly. His presence establishes that the party pays and has already refrained from killing one dangerous neighbor. It does not guarantee that their promises will last.

Use Brundt for:

- announcing the party;
- carrying an invitation or tally-stick;
- vouching that a visible sack really belongs to the party;
- confirming that Riverfork admitted him and let his coin reach the market.

Do not use him to define prisoner policy, political standing, or future enforcement. He will become bored or agree with the last concrete offer he heard.

## The Concordant Staff

The staff lets everyone within range understand and inclines living listeners toward peace. It can stop an initial spear cast, interrupt panic after someone falls into the pit, or make a formal greeting possible without a shared language.

Yiznak recognizes that the effect is magic. He accepts the opening but asks that important terms be repeated after the staff is lowered or recorded on tally-sticks. He wants proof the agreement survives without one priest concentrating on it.

The staff does not:

- compel Yiznak to answer;
- make food appear;
- prove the Duke will honor a bargain;
- prevent later sabotage;
- affect undead if they interrupt the meeting.

## Best combined use

Brundt establishes precedent, the staff establishes language, and the players establish terms. Once speech is open, move the attention away from the mediators and onto Yiznak.
