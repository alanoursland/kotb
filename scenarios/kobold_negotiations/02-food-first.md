# Approach — Food First

Food is the most obvious lever and the easiest one to mishandle. Yiznak needs enough food to change behavior, not a ceremonial handful intended to purchase gratitude.

## Presenting the food

Strong approaches include:

- opening a sack and inviting a kobold to inspect it;
- naming the quantity and how many residents it feeds;
- distinguishing an immediate gift from future wages or trade;
- asking where delivery can occur without exposing the common chamber;
- allowing kobold porters to handle and count their own allotment.

Weak approaches include throwing meat toward the cave, demanding that everyone emerge, or saying “there is more where this came from” without identifying who can authorize it.

## Yiznak's test

Yiznak unlocks the [food store](../../locations/caves/03-kobold-food-store.md) only after contact is secure. The scrape marks and retied sacks make the need visible.

He asks:

> “This feeds us today. What do you buy tomorrow?”

Useful answers are concrete:

- a route survey;
- drainage or road repair;
- marked safe passage;
- notice of hazards and cave movements;
- a temporary cessation of raids while a second delivery is arranged;
- later purchases through Riverfork under named procedures.

“Friendship” is welcome but does not fill a store.

## If the party has only carried rations

Sharing personal rations proves intent and can secure a safe second meeting. It cannot support 71 residents. Yiznak grants provisional contact and asks for a date, quantity, and accountable human official.

Tavin can estimate needs without turning play into arithmetic. A first delivery need only be large enough to demonstrate that wagons can actually arrive; the agreement then defines the bridge.

## If human remains are noticed

Some stored meat includes former human victims. Yiznak does not deny it. Hunger and raiding put it there. The right question is what arrangement makes that conduct stop.

Food plus recognized exchange ends the immediate reason to consume human dead or raid for provisions. Condemnation without an alternative confirms only that humans disapprove of what hunger requires.

## What food accomplishes

- **Personal rations shared:** safe contact and another meeting.
- **One meaningful delivery:** temporary ceasefire and serious negotiation.
- **Named recurring route or paid-work arrangement:** basis for a lasting agreement.
- **Food delivered through Karrgan:** relief, but not kobold independence; see [Order Variants](08-order-variants.md#after-karrgan-or-the-hobgoblins).
