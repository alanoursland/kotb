# Approach — Open Emissaries

The cleanest ordinary approach is to stop outside bow range, announce the party's numbers and purpose, and ask the watchers to carry a request to Yiznak.

## The kobold response

The tree ambushers remain hidden while an outer guard answers from cover. If the party accepts instruction, the kobolds reveal the pit, lay planks across it, and escort the visitors through [area 1](../../locations/caves/01-kobold-guard-room.md). They count weapons and ask that bows remain unstrung or lowered; they do not demand complete disarmament.

The guard asks:

> “How many enter? Who sent you? What do you want dug?”

The last question is not comic misunderstanding. It is the kobolds stating what they believe they can offer.

## Responses to likely declarations

| The party says… | The kobolds respond… |
|---|---|
| “The Duke sent us to make peace.” | “Then the Duke may tell us what peace pays.” Authority establishes seriousness, not trust. |
| “We want the road safe.” | “Road for whom? Safe from whom?” They want reciprocal obligations named. |
| “We brought food.” | The guards inspect it and ask whether it is a gift, wage, trade, or ransom. Continue with [Food First](02-food-first.md). |
| “We need guides or builders.” | Interest becomes immediate. Continue with [Engineering First](03-engineering-first.md). |
| “Take us to your chief.” | “Give the chief a reason.” A one-sentence purpose is enough if respectful. |
| “We know where your missing people are.” | The escort becomes urgent and heavily watched. Apply [Order Variants](08-order-variants.md). |

## At Yiznak's room

Yiznak offers stools, not food the cave cannot spare. He studies who speaks, who listens, and whether anyone examines his large gold chain as loot. His first substantive question is:

> “You want something to stop. What starts instead?”

An answer involving food, work, trade, safe passage, or exchange advances to terms. “Obedience” does not.

## Mechanical use

- No reaction roll is needed if the party follows instructions and names reciprocal business.
- If their purpose is vague, make an ordinary reaction roll after the escort reaches Yiznak.
- Speaking Kobold, bringing Brundt, or offering meaningful food can improve the reaction, but bonuses do not replace actual terms.
