# Outcomes and Follow-Through

Choose the outcome that reflects what the players actually accomplished. Do not withhold success merely because Vargoth remains undiscovered.

## Full agreement

Use this when Contact, Proof, and Terms are all established.

- Kobold attacks stop against recognized travelers and food traffic.
- Cave A receives a direct emergency allotment and a procedure for later deliveries.
- Yiznak's surveyors begin paid drainage, road, or safe-route work.
- Matched tally-sticks identify accepted envoys and project workers.
- Humans agree not to enter Cave A or tamper with unmarked defenses.
- Yiznak gains a direct place in later faction meetings.
- The party undertakes to investigate the three missing kobolds.

If sabotage occurred but the culprit remains unknown, add mixed watches and inspection procedures. The agreement is still full.

## Provisional agreement

Use this after a small food gift, recovered hostile contact, an unproven ducal promise, or unresolved concern about Karrgan.

- A short ceasefire lasts until a named date or first delivery.
- One small engineering job serves as the test.
- The party receives safe conduct only when announced.
- Yiznak sends one observer, not a full delegation, to a convoy or council.
- Full terms follow if both sides perform.

This is success with another playable step, not failure.

## Delayed talks

Use this when sabotage remains persuasive, promised food fails to arrive, or the party cannot define who stands behind its promises.

- Yiznak closes Cave A but does not declare war.
- Existing wounded may be exchanged and collected.
- He retains evidence and sends terms through Brundt or a tally-stick.
- Food, a returned missing resident, a neutral witness, or fulfilled repair reopens the meeting.

## Failed negotiation

Use this only when the party insists on submission, attacks after a clear stand-down, steals from the common chamber, or refuses all reciprocal terms.

The kobolds restore defenses, warn nearby factions that the ducal envoys cannot be trusted, and raid when hunger requires it. Recovery remains possible through returned prisoners, the exposure of Vargoth or the cult, a large food delivery with safeguards, or a respected intermediary.

## What Yiznak contributes afterward

- **To the convoy:** load inspection, drainage, marked routes, and many small porters.
- **To Karrgan's negotiation:** proof that independent bilateral terms can work; resistance to hobgoblin dominance.
- **To the lizardfolk:** recognition of linguistic and cultural kinship and a possible introduction.
- **To the Shrine investigation:** reports of missing food, night tracks, and affected sites—not soldiers for a human religious assault.
- **To Riverfork:** paid engineering, information, and the first test of whether a kobold can enter a human market as a recognized trader.

## Closing image

For a full success, end on work rather than a speech:

> Small hands drive stakes along the wet edge of the wagon ground. Yiznak changes one marker by the width of his palm, looks up at the road the humans meant to build, and waits. This time, the humans move the road.
