# Negotiating the Agreement

The opening determines tone. This scene determines what both sides will actually do.

## Put five questions on the table

### 1. What stops immediately?

- Kobold attacks on recognized travelers and food traffic.
- Human entry into Cave A without invitation.
- Tampering with kobold traps outside agreed routes.
- Retaliation while missing residents are investigated.

Yiznak can promise his own people. He cannot promise every small reptilian creature in the region is his subject.

### 2. What arrives immediately?

A first allotment should be specific enough to count: sacks of grain, dried food, oil, salt, and supplies. Personal rations can secure a second meeting; a wagon-sized delivery can secure a temporary ceasefire.

The party need not calculate exact calories. Tavin, Sorina Petrov, or Guild staff can turn a population count into loads later.

### 3. What work follows?

Choose at least one paid service:

- drainage and culvert work;
- road and wagon-ground inspection;
- tunnel reinforcement;
- hazard marking and safe-route maintenance;
- excavation, quarrying, or salvage;
- warning the Keep about dangerous movement through the ravine.

Food may be emergency relief. Continuing work should eventually be paid in goods or coin so dependence does not become disguised tribute.

### 4. Who receives and records the bargain?

Yiznak requires direct standing. Use matched tally-sticks, witness marks, and a named human official. The kobold share does not pass through Karrgan unless Yiznak explicitly chooses that arrangement.

### 5. What happens to the missing kobolds?

The party promises to investigate and return anyone found. Yiznak supplies descriptions of equipment and personal work marks rather than assuming the missing are alive.

This promise is enough. The party does not need to solve it before peace begins.

## Terms Yiznak accepts

- one immediate delivery and a date or condition for the next;
- recognized kobold surveyors and paid work;
- marked safe routes and reciprocal notice before entry;
- cessation of hunger raids while supplies arrive;
- direct kobold representation in any later faction meeting;
- investigation of the missing residents;
- a review after the first work project or delivery.

## Terms he refuses

- permanent obedience in exchange for temporary food;
- representation by Karrgan without kobold consent;
- removal of every defensive trap;
- unrestricted human inspection of the common chamber;
- collective punishment if an unknown kobold breaks the agreement;
- military service without pay, limits, or right of refusal.

## Marking success

The negotiation has succeeded when the party earns all three:

- **Contact:** safe procedure and recognized speakers.
- **Proof:** meaningful food, respected engineering, returned residents, or another act demonstrating understanding.
- **Terms:** reciprocal actions with an accountable next step.

Do not add sabotage merely to deny the players a clean win. Use it if Vargoth is active and the campaign benefits from turning this success into an investigation.
