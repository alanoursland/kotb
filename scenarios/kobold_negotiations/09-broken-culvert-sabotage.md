# Optional Complication — The Broken Culvert

Use this only if Vargoth remains active and a promising kobold agreement would benefit from pressure. Skip it in a fast run, after the bugbear cell is exposed, or when the players have already worked hard for a satisfying clean success.

## The target

Yiznak's first paid project is a shallow drainage cut and stone culvert beside the proposed wagon ground below Cave A. It protects the cave approach from runoff and demonstrates that kobold work can support the food route.

The party supplies timber braces, cord, tools, and several sacks paid at the beginning of the job. Kobold surveyors mark grade and finish the first section before dusk.

## What Vargoth and the cult do

During the night, two bugbears reach the site from the upper ravine path. They:

- tear out supports and crack a fitted stone so runoff collapses the cut;
- ruin part of the staged food with muddy water and lamp oil;
- use a stolen tool belonging to one of Vargoth's kobold prisoners;
- leave ducal-style arrowheads, wax, and cloth from the Shrine's framing cache;
- brush out the clearest tracks and withdraw toward Cave H.

No kobolds need die. One night watcher may be bruised or briefly knocked unconscious if the scene needs a witness, but the purpose is political damage, not massacre.

## What it looks like at first

The project failed immediately after humans funded it. Human military material lies in the debris. A kobold tool made some of the marks. Food promised by the party is ruined.

Yiznak does not conclude that the party personally swung the hammer. He asks whether their soldiers, employers, rivals, or careless workers can be trusted:

> “You said your side could keep a promise. Which part of your side did this?”

That question lets the relationship survive even when the culprit remains unknown.

## When the sabotage occurs

Choose one:

- **Before final terms:** Yiznak pauses the agreement and calls a hearing.
- **After signatures but before delivery:** obligations remain, but work stops until safeguards exist.
- **After one clean delivery:** Yiznak suspects an enemy of the agreement rather than the party itself.
- **After the Shrine falls:** do not stage a new attack; instead let the party find the unused framing packet and realize what nearly happened.
