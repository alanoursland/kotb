# Order Variants — What Happened Before Cave A

Apply every entry that is true. Earlier choices change Yiznak's questions and available proof; they do not replace the need for kobold terms.

## Kobolds are first

Yiznak has only tree-watch reports and whatever Brundt says. He does not know whether the Duke can deliver food or whether the party distinguishes one cave community from another.

- Ask for a small first contract or delivery before full commitment.
- The three missing kobolds appear to be an unrelated wilderness loss.
- Vargoth is quiet at first. A successful agreement may advance him to **Watching** and make [the Broken Culvert](09-broken-culvert-sabotage.md) appropriate.

## After Karrgan or the hobgoblins

Yiznak's first concern is political:

> “Did you make terms with Karrgan, or did you make Karrgan the terms?”

- Food must be delivered and recorded directly.
- Karrgan cannot appoint the kobold speaker or command their workers.
- A military role for Karrgan should be balanced by a technical role for Yiznak.
- If the party gave Karrgan control of all cave allotments, Yiznak may still accept relief but refuses a final treaty until that structure changes.

## After Cave H or the bugbears

If the party returns the three kobold prisoners, Proof is immediate. The survivors identify the multilingual welcome, the pens, Vargoth's questions, and the theft of their tools and markers.

- Yiznak grants exceptional trust but still negotiates future food and standing.
- Killing bugbears without prisoners or records earns no automatic credit.
- The returned tools can identify any earlier false evidence.
- Skarn's testimony distinguishes Vargoth's cell from bugbears as a people.

## After the Shrine

Vesna's papers name Vargoth, give exact prisoner counts, and show that kobold possessions were intended for a frame.

- Yiznak believes the missing may be alive but asks the party to rescue them.
- The framing packets can expose the Broken Culvert immediately if it already happened.
- The cult's fall stops the famine engine, but Cave A still needs emergency food while stores and hunting recover.

## After the food convoy

### Kobolds received a direct allotment

Contact and material Proof are largely complete. The negotiation focuses on work, safe passage, and political standing.

### Karrgan received their allotment

The food helped, but Yiznak treats the arrangement as evidence that humans reproduce the ravine's existing hierarchy. Direct accounting becomes the central demand.

### The convoy was sabotaged

Yiznak wants identical seals, matched tally-sticks, and kobold inspection of future loads. If undead attacked, he connects the food shortage to a deliberate enemy more readily.

## After Brundt visits Riverfork

Brundt's successful shopping trip proves the Keep can admit a feared nonhuman and allow real exchange. Yiznak asks whether the same rule applies to small kobold buyers or only to an ogre under military escort.
