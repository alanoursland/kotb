# Clearing the Party Without Finding the Culprit

The agreement does not require the party to solve Vargoth's conspiracy. It requires Yiznak to decide that the party's conduct is more reliable than the attempt to frame it.

## Ways to clear the party

Use any two strong actions, or one action backed by an already established reputation.

### Repair what was damaged

The party replaces ruined food and materials, helps clear the failed cut, and pays kobold workers for doing the reconstruction correctly. This accepts responsibility for the shared project without confessing to sabotage.

### Make the next attempt transparent

Use matched tally-sticks, kobold inspection, sealed loads, and one observer from each side. Invite Yiznak to choose the watch positions.

### Establish an alibi through procedure

The party and Riverfork escort submit equipment for comparison, produce the convoy ledger, and identify where each watch stood. They need not prove who acted to show who did not.

### Fulfill a promise despite the loss

Deliver the next food allotment on schedule, begin a different paid project, or continue searching for the missing residents. Sabotage fails when it cannot make either side abandon its obligations.

### Bring a neutral witness

Maruk, Brundt, Father Demetrios, a Guild clerk, or another trusted figure can verify delivery and procedure. The witness does not declare innocence by fiat; they make another frame harder.

## Yiznak's conclusion

When the party acts constructively without inventing an easy culprit, Yiznak says:

> “I do not know who broke it. I know who rebuilt it.”

The sabotage remains an unresolved clue in the campaign, but the kobold agreement proceeds. Record that Yiznak now believes an outside actor opposes peace. He may share the objects later if new evidence appears.

## What remains unresolved

- The three missing kobolds are still missing.
- Vargoth may attempt sabotage against a different faction.
- The cult may continue attacking food.
- The ducal material still implies that someone can obtain or imitate Riverfork supplies.

None of these prevents kobolds from making terms today.
