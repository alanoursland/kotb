# Clues Pointing Toward the Black Eagle Barony

These pieces should create a direction, not a signed confession.

## Vesna's western-patron fragments — area 59

- eighteen Eagles recorded as “western crowns”;
- collection dates and a destination glyph matching Vargoth's papers;
- a route south through the fens and then west;
- a broken black-wax seal showing an eagle wing and talon.

Vesna believed these came from foreign supporters of Traladaran liberation.

## Guest travel residue — area 60

Southern-fen mud, black-reed pollen, and maintained-road lime show a courier path from the Shrine through the wetlands and back from a western road.

## Reused supply crate — area 63

A planed-over black-eagle quartermaster brand lies beneath new paint. Hinges and cord match goods in Vargoth's spoils room.

## Why this is not conclusive alone

- Eagles circulate and are legal to possess near the frontier.
- Crates and seals can be reused or stolen.
- A partial route does not identify the sender.
- Vesna did not know the actual patron.

## How the case strengthens

- Vargoth's Eagles tie payment to captive collection dates.
- Skarn identifies the outside oath and handlers.
- Khaeros and Aldric describe the Black Eagle slave route independently.
- The southern transfer bank supplies physical geography.
- Father Branislav identifies the iron-ring trappings as foreign to Traladaran faith.

Together these pieces justify Duke Stefan beginning systematic scrutiny of the Baron.
