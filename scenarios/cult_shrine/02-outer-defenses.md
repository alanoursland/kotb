# Outer Defenses — Areas 52 and 53

## Area 52 — false-static skeletons

Twelve skeletons appear inert around a gemmed throne. Initial turning does nothing because they have not animated. Touching the dais or chair activates all twelve.

Use this room to teach the Shrine's rule: treasure and symbols are bait attached to alarms and corrupted protection. Careful players can leave the throne untouched and avoid the fight.

## Area 53 — zombie guard line

Eight zombies attack anyone lacking temple robe and matching amulet. Their protection makes them turn as ghouls. The staff does nothing to them.

Options include:

- direct combat and turning;
- disguise using cult equipment found earlier;
- distraction drawing part of the line outward;
- bypass after learning the exact garb requirement;
- retreat into area 52, risking activation of the skeletons.

## Alert consequences

- A quiet defeat of area 53 does not automatically ring the great bell.
- Escaped living cultists warn the temple and may ring it.
- Continuous entrance noise brings the separate eight mobile responders.
- Robes fool standing undead, never Vesna's direct command.
