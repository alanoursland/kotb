# Order and Alert Variants

## Cult first

The Shrine is least prepared. The party lacks cave witnesses but receives the clearest route toward Vargoth and the missing residents.

## After kobold contact

Yiznak supplies affected food sites, missing-person marks, and night-track observations. His people do not assault the human Shrine.

## After Karrgan

Karrgan supplies disciplined patrol reports and disappearance routes. He still calls it a human religious problem and declines to spend his army.

## After Cave H

Pen tallies, Skarn, Eagles, and matching fittings make Shrine clues immediately legible. If Vargoth escaped, set the Shrine to **Alerted** and remove or burn one clue cache—not the whole trail.

## After the food convoy

If undead attacked, subtract that destroyed patrol from one defensive wave. The cult understands the convoy threatens its strategy and watches approaches more closely.

## Alerted Shrine changes

- area 53 is ready rather than static;
- an adept begins closer to the bell;
- Vesna carries or burns one portion of the command papers;
- Mara is watched more closely, making her hesitation more dangerous;
- area 59's western fragments or area 63's physical evidence still survive;
- Vesna prepares the area 51 escape.

Order increases pressure. It never erases Mara, every conspiracy clue, or the possibility of cleansing the Shrine.
