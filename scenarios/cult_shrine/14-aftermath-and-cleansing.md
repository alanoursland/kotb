# Aftermath and Cleansing

## Immediate tasks

- bless or sanctify Vesna's corpse;
- disarm or contain remaining undead;
- accept and separate surrendering living cultists;
- protect Mara as a witness rather than displaying her as a trophy;
- preserve rings, papers, packets, Eagles, seals, and route material with their locations recorded;
- decide whether to risk the crypt, cube, or Lysandra after the main objective is secure.

## Religious interpretation

Father Branislav can publicly identify the Shrine as a Thanatos corruption alien to the Church of Traladar. A mixed delegation can record that finding for Valerian and the Duke. Without Traladaran legitimacy, the tactical victory remains vulnerable to propaganda.

## Mara

Custody under Branislav is stronger than immediate execution or casual pardon. She confesses, identifies raid sites and routes, and helps repair the famine she assisted. She knows facts, not the full conspiracy solution.

## Other cultists

Surrender does not equal innocence. Separate novices, initiated clergy, and the torturer; preserve testimony; let Riverfork or later ducal authority decide judgment.

## The cave factions

- Show Yiznak and Karrgan the exact prisoner counts and framing plans.
- Use the evidence to justify action against Cave H.
- Explain that ending the undead raids stops new destruction but does not refill existing stores.
- Continue the food bridge and bilateral negotiations.

## The political horizon

The Shrine's Eagles, partial seal, route residue, and reused crate point toward Black Eagle traffic. Combined with Cave H, Khaeros, Skarn, and southern evidence, they give Stefan a reason to watch the Baron systematically.

## Outcomes

- **Clean victory:** Vesna is contained, Mara survives, evidence is preserved, and Traladaran clergy cleanse the site.
- **Military victory:** the cult is destroyed but evidence, witnesses, or legitimacy are lost. The famine stops while the wider case weakens.
- **Incomplete victory:** Vesna or key cultists escape, but the Shrine is exposed and cannot continue quietly. Use surviving evidence and future pursuit.
