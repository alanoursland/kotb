# Infiltration and Disguise

Captured rusty-red or black robes plus matching amulets can pass some static undead and create seconds of uncertainty among living cultists.

## What disguise accomplishes

- area 53 zombies allow passage;
- area 57 ranks remain still unless the bell sounds or Vesna commands;
- distant cultists may mistake silhouettes for allies;
- the party can reach Mara or the lower hall before general alarm.

## What exposes it

- wrong robe rank for the claimed destination;
- failure to answer a cult phrase or Thanatos invocation;
- visible ducal or Church of Karameikos symbols;
- companions without amulets;
- opening prisoner cells or searching Vesna's papers;
- Vesna seeing the intruders directly.

## Useful skills

- Danica can observe traffic and ownership marks.
- Irina identifies what is alien to Traladaran worship but does not know cult passwords.
- Sorina or Alexios can use invisibility, light, or detection to support movement.
- Company signs let the group coordinate without speaking cult phrases.

## When the disguise fails

Give the party the position it earned. Failure causes alarm from the current room; it does not teleport every defender into place. A hard-pressed adept runs for the bell, creating a chase objective.
