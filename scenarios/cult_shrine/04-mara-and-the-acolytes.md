# Mara and the Acolytes — Area 54

Four armored acolytes occupy the room. Mara's oversized robe, uncertain mace grip, and hidden wooden Traladaran token make her hesitation visible.

## First round

The senior acolyte orders:

> “Mara! Shut the door and take your place!”

Mara moves defensively but does not attack unless attacked. Do not hide this behind a roll.

## What can reach her

- proof or testimony that undead destroyed cave food;
- a Church of Traladar priest naming the Shrine as corruption;
- a credible surrender with protection from immediate execution;
- one of the party's earlier acts of mercy;
- asking what her First Severing requires;
- showing that Vargoth holds missing residents and supplies the cult.

## What does not work

- claiming the Duke's goodness erases her grievance;
- demanding she attack Vesna immediately;
- promising pardon without authority;
- killing someone who has surrendered;
- treating Traladaran anger as ignorance.

## If she surrenders

Protect her during the continuing dungeon. She can identify routes, Vargoth's couriers, night-raid schedules, and Vesna's beliefs. She cannot disarm every trap, command undead, or name the Iron Ring's patron.

## If the party is moving fast

Run three beats: ordered to strike; offered surrender plus the famine contradiction; mace dropped. Continue to the temple.
