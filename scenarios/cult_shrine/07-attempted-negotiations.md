# Attempted Negotiations — What Talking Can Still Do

There is no treaty that leaves the famine operation intact. Talking can produce surrender, save individuals, expose motives, buy time, or clarify that the remaining conflict is irreducible.

## Mara

Mara can surrender before or during combat. She needs a contradiction she recognizes and a survivable way to act on it. See [her encounter](04-mara-and-the-acolytes.md).

## Ordinary cultists

Living acolytes or adepts may surrender after the bell force is broken, Vesna falls, or the party demonstrates custody rather than execution. They know rites, shifts, and commands, not the wider conspiracy.

The staff can hold a living attack in dialogue while the party states surrender terms. Questions receive better consideration, not compelled truth.

## The torturer

He fights while the institution stands. If captured after Vesna's fall, he can confirm Vargoth's prisoner deliveries and Shrine harassment. He bargains for his life and minimizes his own conduct.

## Vesna

Vesna will speak because they believe their cause is righteous and because delay helps the undead. They may offer:

- safe departure if the party abandons the Duke and leaves the Shrine alone;
- proof of Thyatian abuses as if it excuses the famine;
- a place in Traladaran liberation;
- release of one prisoner in exchange for the party's withdrawal;
- an end to one night raid while the underlying operation continues.

None is peace. Players can confront Vesna with starving cave families, Vargoth's slaving, Mara, or the western patron clues. Vesna rationalizes until the contradiction becomes unbearable, then chooses ascent rather than surrender.

## Lysandra

She is a prisoner, not a cult negotiator. Conversation through a barred door can create a separate conditional agreement; see [Lower Shrine](09-lower-shrine.md).
