# The Clergy Wing — Areas 55 and 56

## Area 55 — corrupted chapel

Four valuable bronze vessels are cursed. Picking one up requires the module's save; destroying one rings the great bell and brings residents in three rounds.

Father Branislav recognizes a Thanatos corruption rather than legitimate Traladaran worship. The room establishes religious evidence but is not necessary to defeat Vesna.

Player choices:

- leave vessels untouched and document them;
- attempt *dispel magic* and *bless*;
- destroy them knowingly and accept the alarm;
- carry one out at the risk of corruption.

## Area 56 — adepts

Four second-level clerics cast before melee and run for the bell if hard pressed. They have completed initiation and are less reachable than Mara, but morale or the staff can still produce surrender.

An adept offered custody after Vesna falls may testify to rites and orders. None knows the full Black Eagle relationship; they repeat Vesna's belief in western liberation supporters.

## Evidence here

- iron rings and alien theology;
- ranks and initiation structure;
- liturgical proof readable by Traladaran clergy;
- no direct Barony confession.
