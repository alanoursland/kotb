# Routes Through the Dungeon

## Fast campaign route

Use for an eight-hour run:

1. Entrance noise or area 53 establishes the undead threat.
2. Area 54 gives Mara's surrender scene.
3. Area 58 becomes the undead-wave set piece.
4. Area 59 resolves Vesna and yields command papers plus western fragments.
5. Montage areas 60, 61, and 63 as one evidence sweep.
6. Bless the corpse and leave; skip crypt and Lysandra.

## Standard dungeon route

Run outer halls, let players choose clergy rooms, allow the bell alarm to emerge naturally, resolve the temple, then explore the lower wing. Keep room alarms local until the bell.

## Infiltration route

Use robes and amulets to pass area 53, reach Mara or the lower wing, collect evidence, and then let exposure trigger the temple battle from an unusual direction.

## Evidence sweep

After Vesna falls, point to four actionable searches rather than asking for every wall:

- Vesna's desk and escape wardrobe;
- the occupied guest chamber;
- the torture register;
- the storeroom framing cache and route crate.

Finding any two plus Mara is enough to expose Vargoth. Finding western fragments plus Cave H evidence is enough to point at the Barony.

## Lower-route surprise

The secret door between area 63 and Maruk's chamber can become an alternate entry only after discovered from one side. The gnolls do not know it exists. Do not turn Maruk into an automatic assault route before that discovery.

## Completion rule

The Shrine is defeated when Vesna and organized living resistance fall, controlled undead are destroyed or contained, and the altars can be cleansed. The party need not open the wight's tomb, defeat the cube, or resolve Lysandra to complete the core objective.
