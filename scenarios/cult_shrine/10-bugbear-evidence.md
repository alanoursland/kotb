# Evidence Connecting the Shrine to Vargoth

## Mara

She copied “H sends word,” saw bugbear couriers, and knows they exchanged cave news and prisoner counts with Vesna.

## Vesna's command papers

Messages name **Vargoth, called the Welcomer**, list three missing hobgoblins and three kobolds, and preserve plans to use their possessions against the party.

## Guest chamber

Hair, boot marks, catnip, packing cord, and copied multilingual welcome phrases physically identify repeated bugbear guests.

## Torture register

Selected captives came from Vargoth's pens for questioning and were returned alive. Notes identify what would provoke Karrgan and Yiznak.

## Storeroom

Framing packets contain possessions taken from the missing residents and ducal-style material meant to implicate the party.

## What the set proves

- Vargoth knows the cult exists.
- The two operations share intelligence and prisoners.
- Both attempted to preserve division among the cave communities.
- Fighting Cave H need not cost trust once these records are shown.

It does not by itself prove the Baron ordered either operation.
