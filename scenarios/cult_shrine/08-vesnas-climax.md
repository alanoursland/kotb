# Vesna's Final Confrontation

Vesna is not available for a tidy interrogation. The climax protects that design while letting player words matter.

## Before the final choice

Give players time for one or two direct challenges:

- “Your undead destroyed food belonging to the people you claim to defend.”
- “Vargoth takes Traladarans and cave residents for the same western masters.”
- “Your western crowns are Black Eagle scrip.”
- “Mara can still leave. You could stop.”

Vesna does not suddenly understand the full conspiracy. Their answers expose grief, grievance, and the substitutions by which Thanatos captured them.

## Suicide-ascension

When capture becomes inevitable, Vesna chooses death believing it is ascent. Use their campaign-level abilities to reach the prepared moment rather than escaping the story permanently.

## The corpse

A sixth-level-or-higher cleric of Entropy returns as undead unless blessed or otherwise sanctified. Telegraph unnatural tension, blackening veins, or a second breath. A cleric who acts quickly can bless the corpse. Otherwise Vesna rises for a final compressed fight.

The staff does not work on the risen Vesna.

## What player speech changes

- Mara's decision and later testimony;
- whether wavering cultists surrender;
- what witnesses understand about Vesna's sincerity;
- whether the party preserves or destroys evidence in anger;
- the political interpretation of the Shrine's fall.

It does not create a peace agreement with Entropy.
