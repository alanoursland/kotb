# The Shrine of Evil Chaos — Dungeon and Interaction Guide

**Faction:** [Cult of Thanatos](../../locations/caves/population-cult.md) | **Leader:** [Vesna](../../characters/the-hierophant.md) | **Reachable cultist:** [Mara Sokol](../../characters/mara-sokol.md) | **Location:** [Cave K](../../locations/caves/k-entry-passage.md)

> **There is no peaceful settlement with the Shrine operating.** This is the campaign's conventional dungeon assault, but living cultists can surrender, Mara can be saved, Vesna can be confronted, Lysandra can be negotiated with, and evidence can be preserved. Conversation changes lives and meaning even when it cannot produce peace.
