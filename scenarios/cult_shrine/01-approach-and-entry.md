# Approach and Entry

The twisted trees, foul draft, red strata, and black stone veins make Cave K visibly wrong without making it visibly a temple from outside.

## Approaches

### Follow undead patrols

Tracks from the [Northern Woods](../../locations/northern_woods/17-shrineward-escarpment.md) establish that organized dead enter here. The staff gives no protection from them.

### Reconnoiter by day

Watchers can identify patrol timing, courier-sized tracks, and the twenty-foot mouth. Daylight does not disable the interior dead.

### Approach under religious authority

Visible Traladaran clergy may call for surrender. The living cult hears the demand, but zombies answer first unless Mara or another wavering member reaches the entry.

### Enter covertly

Robes and amulets can pass some guards. Continue with [Infiltration and Disguise](03-infiltration-and-disguise.md).

## Area 51 — the blocked branch

Fresh tools, disturbed dust, and a gas-sized gap reveal an escape route rather than ordinary rubble. Spending time here risks the eight mobile zombies but can prevent surprise when Vesna later flees toward it.

## Noise clock

Continuous noise at the entrance brings eight zombie responders. One brief exchange or careful search does not; hammering, prolonged argument, or clearing boulders does.
