# The Bell and Temple Battle — Areas 57 and 58

Run this as two or three tactical waves, not forty-eight individual searches.

## What rings the bell

- three magical chanting voices in area 58;
- an adept reaching it;
- destruction of a cursed vessel in area 55;
- Vesna ordering the alarm.

## Wave 1 — temple responders

Eight mobile zombie guards arrive three rounds after entry to area 58. Colored forms on the western wall force a spell save if watched too long.

## Wave 2 — area 57 deploys

- twenty skeletons take the south side;
- twenty chain-armored zombies take the north side;
- protective magic makes turning harder;
- correct robes cease to matter once Vesna commands them.

For a fast game, use one skeleton mob and one zombie mob with morale-less pressure rather than forty initiatives.

## Wave 3 — Vesna's command group

Vesna and the three plate-armored zombies from area 59 enter, reposition, or force the party toward the altars. Living adepts use fear, wounds, and the exits.

## Player objectives

- stop or silence chanting before the bell;
- hold a retreat route to the temple hall or lower wing;
- turn or bottleneck waves rather than surround themselves;
- protect Mara and clerical witnesses;
- keep Vesna from reaching the area 51 escape;
- preserve the corpse for blessing after the final confrontation.

## The staff

It can halt and open conversation with living cultists in range. It does nothing to skeletons, zombies, the wight, or an undead Vesna.
