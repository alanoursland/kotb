# The Lower Shrine — Areas 60–64

The lower wing contains more campaign evidence than tactical necessity. Search it after the main battle, infiltrate it before the bell, or use it as a pressured retreat.

## Area 60 — guest chamber

- bugbear hair, broad boot marks, catnip, welcome text, and trade cord;
- fen mud, black-reed pollen, and western road lime;
- a polished mirror useful against Lysandra.

This room connects Vargoth to the Shrine and suggests the route south and west.

## Area 61 — torture chamber

The torturer attacks unauthorized entrants. Records identify prisoners temporarily supplied by Vargoth, exact missing counts, and preparation for a false incident. Captured after Vesna falls, he corroborates cooperation but not the ultimate patron.

## Area 62 — crypt

The sixth tomb opened contains a level-draining wight and hidden magic. This is optional treasure danger, not required evidence. Telegraph the disturbed seal.

## Area 63 — storeroom

- a gelatinous cube arrives after three rounds;
- framing packets or evidence one was used;
- a planed-over black-eagle quartermaster brand;
- a secret route to Maruk's area 50, unknown to the gnolls.

## Area 64 — Lysandra

Negotiate through the door, avoid direct sight, and use the mirror or blindfolds. Her betrayal is real but not inevitable if the party creates genuine safety. Her outcome is a separate gamble, never evidence that the cult could have been peacefully integrated.
