# Preparing the Shrine

## Decide who gives the assault legitimacy

- **Father Branislav or another Church of Traladar representative:** the action reads as cleansing a corruption of Traladaran faith.
- **Only the Church of Karameikos or ducal soldiers:** tactically useful, but opponents can frame it as Thyatian suppression.
- **The party alone:** deniable and flexible, with a harder aftermath and fewer witnesses able to interpret symbols.

Irina can recognize liturgical contradictions. Father Demetrios can translate, identify evil, and open living dialogue, but his Thyatian church cannot replace Traladaran legitimacy.

## Essential equipment and decisions

- holy water, light, rope, mirrors, and a way to bless Vesna's corpse;
- who carries captured robes and amulets;
- whether the objective is destruction, prisoners, evidence, or all three;
- how the party will handle surrendering cultists;
- who protects Mara or another witness during the climax;
- whether the party knows the staff does **not** affect undead.

## Choose the Shrine alert state

- **Unaware:** no survivor or courier warned Vesna.
- **Suspicious:** undead patrols vanished or investigators were seen near the escarpment.
- **Alerted:** Vargoth escaped, a cult agent reported, or the party publicly announced an assault.

Apply exact changes in [Order and Alert Variants](12-order-and-alert-variants.md).
