# Bugbear Negotiations — The Welcome That Breaks

**Faction:** [Vargoth's Iron Ring cell](../../locations/caves/population-bugbears.md) | **Leader:** [Vargoth “the Welcomer”](../../characters/faction-leaders.md#bugbear-chief--vargoth-the-welcomer) | **Location:** [Cave H](../../locations/caves/h-entry-passage.md)

> **There is no peaceful ending with the cell intact.** Vargoth negotiates to measure, isolate, and capture the party. The social play is discovering what kind of institution is welcoming them, choosing when to stop cooperating, and protecting captives and noncombatants when the false peace breaks.
