# Evidence in Cave H

No single object proves the entire conspiracy. Let characters collect related pieces and preserve where each came from.

## Area 36 — payment and route

- A packet of Eagles: gold-washed lead scrip associated with the Black Eagle Barony.
- Collection dates and a destination glyph.
- A courier schedule that matches the arrival of trade stock.

Alone, this proves commerce with someone using Barony scrip, not what was purchased.

## Area 37 — controlled trade

- Good food, wine, oil, hides, and imported stock inconsistent with the famine.
- Container marks matching dates or quantities in the captive tally.
- Prices showing that the bugbears vastly overpay when collectors accept Eagles.

This proves a controlled market and a richer buyer, not formal Barony authorization.

## Area 39 — the business record

- Counts, dates, feeding allotments, and categories of “stock.”
- Names or marks matching current captives and perhaps Deyan Sokol.
- An Iron Ring fitting and route glyph matching Shrine material.

This proves the lair is an intake station rather than a household with incidental prisoners.

## Witnesses

- Skarn explains the oath and repeated outside procedures.
- Returned prisoners identify collectors and Shrine questioning.
- Brundt's Eagles establish that Vargoth paid hired muscle from the same supply.
- Khaeros and Aldric provide separate route testimony.

## What points where

- **To the cult:** fittings, guest cord, prisoner questioning, framing materials.
- **To the Black Eagle Barony:** Eagles, destination glyph, collection route, controlled trade goods.
- **Still missing:** proof that the Baron personally ordered a particular shipment. That requires the wider assembled case.
