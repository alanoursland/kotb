# Scenario Setup — Vargoth's Alert State

## What is true

- Cave H is a slave-intake station, not a hungry community that happens to keep prisoners.
- Seventeen captives occupy two pens, including missing kobolds, hobgoblins, Skarn, and dangerous “Sir Garran.”
- Vargoth knowingly serves the Iron Ring and trades captives into the Black Eagle route.
- The bugbears have good provisions because Barony representatives exchange overpriced goods for Eagles.
- The Shrine and Vargoth exchange intelligence, captives, warnings, and framing material.
- Brundt will not help either side without a new transaction and will not join a slave rescue from principle.

## Alert states

| State | Change inside Cave H |
|---|---|
| **Relaxed** | The party has no cave agreements. The scripted welcome is patient and records remain in place. |
| **Watching** | One faction has met the party. Vargoth tailors questions and begins a frame. |
| **Preparing** | Several factions or a convoy are involved. Records are bundled, pen guards reinforced, and capture is attempted sooner. |
| **Exposed** | Shrine evidence, rescued witnesses, or Skarn has named Vargoth. He abandons prolonged hospitality, doubles area 39, and prepares the secret escape. |

Vargoth never removes the prisoners from play merely because the party chose a different order.
