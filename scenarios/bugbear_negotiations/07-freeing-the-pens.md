# Freeing the Pens

The keys hang in [area 39](../../locations/caves/39-bugbear-pen-guard.md). Opening doors is easy once the guards fall; managing seventeen frightened and dangerous people is the scene.

## Area 40

- Three kobolds, one goblin, four orcs, and two humans share one chain.
- Returned kobolds and later hobgoblins are the strongest diplomatic evidence.
- Arm captives selectively and identify a safe outward route.

## Area 41

- Three hobgoblins and two gnolls want immediate escape.
- Skarn fights Vargoth's cell and can interpret procedures and records.
- “Sir Garran” has a 50% chance each combat round to attack an ally while berserk and later attempts theft or murder.

## A safe release sequence

1. Control the corridor and keys.
2. Speak through each door before opening it.
3. Free Skarn or another coherent witness first.
4. Move noncombatants toward a guarded exit.
5. Arm those who can identify allies and follow commands.
6. Contain Garran rather than assuming “human” means safe.

## What the prisoners know

- welcome questions and separation were routine;
- guards recorded species, condition, possible ransom, and feeding costs;
- selected captives were questioned by Shrine agents;
- outside collectors took earlier prisoners and brought trade goods;
- only Skarn understands the Iron Ring oath as a structure, and even he lacks the whole Barony case.
