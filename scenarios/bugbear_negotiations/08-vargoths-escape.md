# Vargoth's Escape

The concealed door in area 36 opens into [area 43](../../locations/caves/43-fire-beetle-cave.md), not directly into Khaeros's cavern.

## When he runs

- the gong fails and the party controls the entrance;
- area 39 falls;
- Shrine evidence is shown;
- his companion dies or morale breaks;
- he can carry records or an Eagle packet with him.

## Finding the route

Freshly moved stone, a draft, red beetle glow, Vargoth's retreat tracks, or captured testimony reveals the door. Do not require a blind secret-door roll when the party watches him use it.

## Pursuit choices

- Immediate pursuit risks fire beetles, stirges, and separation.
- Securing pens first may allow Vargoth to escape but saves witnesses.
- Khaeros treats armed bugbears using the back route as enemies; if already befriended, he can block or report the passage without becoming the party's subordinate.
- A gaseous or invisible pursuer can follow, but the labyrinth should remain dangerous.

## If Vargoth escapes

The cell can still be dismantled. Vargoth becomes a fugitive who may warn outside handlers, but he does not magically rebuild Cave H. Preserve the prisoners and records as the meaningful victory.
