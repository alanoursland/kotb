# Order Variants

## Bugbears first

Vargoth is relaxed and the welcome lasts longest. The party lacks corroboration but faces fewer prepared frames.

## After Yiznak

Vargoth knows the kobolds matter. A missing kobold's tool may already be used at the Broken Culvert, and questions focus on the food route.

## After Karrgan

Vargoth presents himself as the friendlier alternative and offers false information about the missing patrol. The hobgoblin framing packet may already be in play.

## After both negotiations

Records are packed, the lower guard is ready, and Vargoth attempts capture earlier. The signs and meal remain because the welcome is his best weapon.

## After the Shrine

The party may know Vargoth's name, exact prisoner counts, and sabotage plan. He abandons prolonged bargaining when shown proof, doubles the pen guard, and prepares the secret exit. He does not remove the captives.

## After the food convoy

Vargoth has counted households and mapped the supply route. If included, he attempts an abduction after distribution; if excluded, he uses exclusion as bait for a private meeting.
