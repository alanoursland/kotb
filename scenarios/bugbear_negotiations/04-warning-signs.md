# Warning Signs — Let the Players See It Break

Do not require one secret perception check. Put several contradictions in ordinary description and answer direct questions honestly.

## Social contradictions

- Ransom is discussed before trade.
- Guards ask who knows the party is present.
- Vargoth speaks of workers but never allows one to answer freely.
- Hospitality questions map directly onto capture value and escape routes.
- The welcome remains warm while guards quietly take positions behind visitors.

## Material contradictions

- Cave H holds better food, wine, oil, and hides than starving neighbors.
- Doors and chains lock from the guard side.
- Meal allotments head downward to guarded corridors.
- Imported goods bear collection dates matching captive tallies.
- Eagles feel too heavy, show lead beneath worn gold wash, or match Brundt's false coins.

## Sensory clues

- muffled chain movement below;
- a captive coughing when conversation pauses;
- watered wine for guards beside good wine under lock;
- catnip on bugbear possessions and trade wrapping;
- a multilingual welcome phrase copied exactly in the Shrine guest room.

## Character strengths

- Danica reads intake questions, ledgers, and staged ownership.
- Brynja notices plated lead and trade fittings.
- Stelian distinguishes household paths from guarded prisoner traffic.
- Tavin sees that Vargoth's provisions contradict his hunger claim.
- A military character notices the gong, closing routes, and reserve positions.

After three clear contradictions, Vargoth should have to act or let the party withdraw. Do not preserve the deception by overruling good play.
