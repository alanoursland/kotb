# The Multilingual Welcome

Signs in Kobold, Orcish, Goblin, and other tongues promise safety, a hot meal, a bed, and protection. The text is grammatically simple and repeated across languages like a commercial notice.

## What players can notice outside

- The signs target fugitives and lone travelers more than trading caravans.
- Paths converge on the cave, but comparatively few tracks leave it.
- Drag marks and heavily laden outbound traffic appear under ordinary boot traffic.
- The food smell is conspicuously rich in a starving ravine.
- Watchers are positioned to observe retreat routes, not merely defend the entrance.

No single detail proves a trap. Together they reward caution without requiring genre suspicion.

## Responses to approaches

| Party action | Cave H response |
|---|---|
| Call openly | Area 35 invites them to the brazier. |
| Bring food | Guards praise generosity but ask who else knows the party came. |
| Bring Brundt | Brundt confirms Vargoth pays. He does not vouch for safety. |
| Ask for Vargoth | Guards offer food while a runner announces them. |
| Ask about missing people | Guards become solicitous and ask for descriptions and ransom value. |
| Mention the cult | The alert state rises; the welcome continues only long enough to assess the evidence. |
