# Vargoth's False Bargain

Vargoth may receive the party in [area 36](../../locations/caves/36-vargoths-chamber.md) or join them near the guard room if he does not want strangers near his records.

## What he claims

- His people are traders and guards unfairly feared by the other caves.
- The people below are debt laborers, criminals, or protected refugees.
- Eagles are ordinary gold received for caravan work.
- He can guarantee convoy security better than Karrgan.
- He wants food, recognition, and a place in the new peace.

## What he offers

- information about other chiefs, mixing truth with useful lies;
- one inexpensive captive sold at an inflated price as a “goodwill release”;
- bugbear guards placed near wagons and vulnerable travelers;
- a private agreement excluding Yiznak or Karrgan;
- information about the Shrine only if the party reveals what it already knows.

## What he will not promise clearly

- an end to taking travelers;
- inspection of the pens;
- release of all captives without payment;
- independent testimony from a prisoner;
- a prisoner rule that forbids replacement captures;
- records showing where workers came from or go.

Every attempt to write those terms produces delay, reinterpretation, or an attack plan.

## The purpose of talking

Let players learn Vargoth's method and choose their line. Conversation is not wasted because peace is impossible; it reveals why this case differs from hunger-driven conduct.
