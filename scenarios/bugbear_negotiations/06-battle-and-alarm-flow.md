# Battle and Alarm Flow

Run Cave H as a decisive cell takedown, not twenty-three separate room searches.

## Wave 1 — the welcome guard

The three area 35 guards attack and one reaches for the gong. Stopping the gong localizes the fight for a few rounds.

## Wave 2 — command and upper passage

On alarm, Vargoth and his companion act from area 36 while adult defenders secure area 38 and the upper corridor. Vargoth throws the hand axe +1 before closing or retreating. Adults protecting the three young do not need to charge into a hopeless fight.

## Wave 3 — the business end

Area 39's five guards hold the pen keys and the lower passage. They use throwing spears and defend the doors because the captives are the cell's wealth and witnesses.

## Player objectives beyond hit points

- prevent the gong or control reinforcement timing;
- keep Vargoth away from the secret door;
- seize the keys before prisoners become hostages;
- prevent records or Eagles from being burned or carried away;
- avoid driving panicked captives into “Sir Garran” or the medusa elsewhere;
- distinguish surrendering residents and young from active guards.

## Morale and surrender

Check morale when Vargoth falls or flees, area 39 is breached, or prisoners join the fight. A surrendered bugbear is a prisoner and witness, not a peace partner. The cell's business still ends.
