# Hospitality and Intake

Three guards in [area 35](../../locations/caves/35-bugbear-welcome-guard.md) roast meat, eat from the skewers, and offer food. The gong behind them is visible.

## Intake questions

Ask naturally, one at a time:

- Where did you travel from?
- Which chief have you met?
- Does Riverfork know you entered this cave?
- Who in the party carries the Duke's authority?
- Who would pay if one of you needed ransom?
- Where will the food wagons wait?
- Which of you keeps first watch?

The guards are learning value, outside knowledge, allies, and the easiest separation.

## Separation attempts

- invite the translator closer to the fire;
- offer a quieter room for the priest or chief envoy;
- ask armored companions to leave weapons by the entry;
- send one character to inspect “trade goods” while others eat;
- offer different sleeping places by size.

Refusal does not cause immediate attack. It makes the bugbears revise the plan.

## The skewer trick

If the party relaxes completely, the guards use the offered skewers as surprise weapons. In the campaign version, delay this until the players have had several observable contradictions or have chosen to surrender a decisive advantage.
