# Aftermath and Outcomes

## Cell dismantled

- Vargoth is dead, captured, or a fugitive.
- Both pens are open and survivors escorted safely.
- Records, Eagles, and trade goods are preserved as evidence.
- Missing kobolds and hobgoblins can return home.
- Adult collaborators face custody, dispersal, or later judgment.
- Three young and any surrendering dependents require protection and placement.

This is the complete local victory even if the Barony route continues elsewhere.

## Vargoth captured

He bargains for personal freedom, lies about superiors, and offers names mixed with enemies. This is surrender, not peace. Corroborate everything against records and witnesses.

## Vargoth escaped

The cell remains broken if captives and records are secured. He can warn the next node, but cannot erase living witnesses.

## Withdrawal with suspicion

The party leaves before seeing enough to justify force. Vargoth tails or frames them, and captives remain. Suspicion can become action through Shrine papers, missing-person evidence, Skarn, or a second covert visit.

## Party captured

Run the prison break through [Freeing the Pens](07-freeing-the-pens.md). Their own experience becomes testimony about the welcome system, but equipment and evidence must be recovered from area 37.

## No peaceful outcome

An agreement that leaves Vargoth taking, holding, or selling people is failure disguised as diplomacy. Food cannot outbid the outside slave market, and purchasing prisoners funds replacements. The meaningful alternatives are exposure, rescue, surrender, flight, or combat.
