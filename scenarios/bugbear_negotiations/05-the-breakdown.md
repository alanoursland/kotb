# The Breakdown — Where False Peace Ends

Choose the trigger created by player action. Vargoth does not confess; the operation becomes visible through what he protects.

## The party insists on the pens

Vargoth offers one supervised viewing, substitutes a lie about debt, or names an impossible purchase price. If the party demands free testimony or all keys, area 39 closes the lower route and Vargoth orders capture.

## The party shows its evidence

Shrine correspondence, a framing packet, Skarn's name, exact prisoner counts, or a Black Eagle route glyph tells Vargoth the operation is exposed. He tries to seize the evidence and orders the gong struck while moving toward area 36.

## The party refuses separation

The guards try one final courteous repositioning. When the party keeps weapons, formation, and exit control, Vargoth chooses an ambush before it becomes less favorable.

## The party catches a lie

If players produce a contradiction Vargoth cannot plausibly answer, he smiles and says:

> “Then you are worth more awake.”

The nearest guards attack to subdue where practical.

## The party withdraws

Vargoth allows an early withdrawal if he believes he can follow or frame them. Once they have seen the pens, records, or secret traffic, he attacks rather than permit departure.

## If the staff is activated

The living attackers stop and listen for one turn. Vargoth answers without honesty, stalls, and repositions only as ordinary conversation permits. The party can use the pause to demand keys, organize retreat, reveal evidence aloud, or invite surrender. The staff can prolong dialogue but cannot make Vargoth accept terms or affect later undead allies.

## If the party is captured

Their equipment goes to area 37 and they awaken chained in the pens. Skarn explains the intake process, identifies the key room, and helps plan a coordinated break. “Sir Garran” makes simply opening every chain dangerous. Continue with [Freeing the Pens](07-freeing-the-pens.md).
