# Outcomes and Follow-Through

## Full agreement

- All six prisoners are exchanged alive.
- Captive-taking for food ends, with no reward for replacements.
- Karrgan receives bounded food-paid patrol or escort work.
- Violations have named reporting and review procedures.
- Goblin food is separately accounted for, whatever political status the party chose.
- Karrgan recognizes that other chiefs make their own terms.

## Provisional agreement

- A ceasefire lasts through one delivery or joint patrol.
- Prisoners remain alive and uneaten during the test period.
- One or more prisoners may be released as proof.
- Full terms await food, return of the missing patrol, or resolution of goblin accounting.

## Delayed agreement

Use after persuasive sabotage, failed delivery, or an unresolved hostile entry. The door closes, but messages and a review date remain. A returned hobgoblin, fulfilled food delivery, neutral witness, or Shrine evidence reopens talks.

## Failed negotiation

Karrgan refuses only when the party demands submission, cannot offer survival, or attacks under safe conduct. He prepares for war and may try to dominate the goblin ward. This is a political failure, not proof that hobgoblins cannot negotiate.

## What Karrgan contributes

- disciplined escorts and bounded patrols;
- a public prisoner policy other factions can observe;
- military reports about undead movement;
- a powerful counterweight—or threat—inside the emerging federation.
