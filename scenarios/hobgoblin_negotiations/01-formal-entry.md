# Approach — Formal Entry

The skull-lined door is barred, but the Common inscription inviting visitors to dinner is genuine in Karrgan's severe sense: hospitality exists under rules.

## The challenge at the door

The party should state:

1. who sent them;
2. how many request entry;
3. what weapons they carry;
4. what business they bring;
5. who answers for the agreement.

The guards repeat the statement, open the bar, and receive visitors in [area 26](../../locations/caves/26-hobgoblin-entry-guard.md). Three crossbows remain ready. Weapons may be peace-bound, counted, or left with chosen companions; complete disarmament is negotiable rather than automatic.

## Responses

| Party opening | Guard response |
|---|---|
| “The Duke sent us to negotiate.” | “Name the offer.” Authority gets the message relayed. |
| “We brought food.” | Sacks are inspected and sealed until Karrgan assigns their meaning. |
| “We seek prisoners.” | The guard asks whether this is ransom, exchange, or threat. |
| “We found your missing patrol.” | Entry becomes immediate and heavily escorted. |
| “Open in the Duke's name.” | “The Duke is not in our chain.” The door remains barred. |

## Under escort

Visitors pass visible discipline, families, and the smell of the feast. Karrgan chooses [area 25](../../locations/caves/25-hobgoblin-feast-hall.md) for public negotiation or [area 30](../../locations/caves/30-karrgans-quarters.md) for command details.
