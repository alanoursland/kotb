# Following the Bugbear Clues

## Suspicion

- The token belongs to a missing hobgoblin, but no corpse accompanies it.
- Broad boot tracks approach from the upper ravine.
- Catnip and packing cord match Vargoth's trade bundles.
- The report knows generic military vocabulary but not Cave F's challenge and response.

This clears the party enough to continue talks. It does not yet prove where the missing patrol is held.

## Local proof

Any one completes the case:

- rescue the three hobgoblins from [area 41](../../locations/caves/41-skarns-slave-pen.md);
- seize Vargoth's pen tally;
- obtain Skarn's testimony;
- recover Vesna's correspondence or the unused hobgoblin framing packet;
- match the report's wrapping to the Shrine cache.

## Effect on Karrgan

Returning his soldiers is stronger than killing Vargoth. Karrgan now sees the bugbears as a threat to his command rather than simply another faction attacked by the Duke's envoys. He does not object to dismantling Cave H and may provide route watches, but he does not hand the party his army.
