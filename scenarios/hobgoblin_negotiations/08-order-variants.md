# Order Variants

## Hobgoblins first

This is the hardest version. Karrgan wants proof the Duke can deliver and may make the six-prisoner exchange or one joint patrol the test.

## After Yiznak

Karrgan respects an accomplished agreement but asks whether he has been subordinated to kobold engineers. Give him a separate military role without allowing him to absorb Cave A.

## After Cave H

Returning the three hobgoblins completes Proof immediately. Pen records kill the false report and demonstrate that Vargoth threatens every community.

## After the Shrine

Vesna's papers name Vargoth, identify the missing prisoners, and describe the planned frame. Karrgan grants time for rescue before final terms. The famine engine is stopped, but emergency food remains necessary.

## After the convoy

Food can release the six prisoners immediately. Settle whether goblin food is direct before Karrgan uses public order to establish permanent control.

## After a failed kobold negotiation

Karrgan offers to solve the “kobold problem” for authority or supplies. Accepting is fast and disastrous for federation balance. A better bargain limits him to his own patrol sector.
