# Karrgan's Inquiry

Karrgan treats the accusation as a command investigation. The party is watched, not bound, unless it attempts to flee or attack.

## His questions

1. Where was every party member when the report claims the ambush occurred?
2. Who supplied the material in the bundle?
3. Why does the writer know military words but not Karrgan's exact reporting formula?
4. Why is there no body?
5. What will the party do if the missing patrol is alive?

## Clearing the party without naming Vargoth

Any two can preserve the agreement:

- submit arrows, wax, and equipment for comparison;
- show escort logs or witnesses placing the party elsewhere;
- point out the wrong command formula and assembled packet;
- fulfill the prisoner-food exchange despite the accusation;
- propose a joint search led by Karrgan's scout and the party's tracker;
- allow Yiznak or Maruk to testify without asking Karrgan to accept their authority.

Karrgan may conclude the report is hostile manipulation without knowing who sent it. Terms resume under added verification.
