# Recovery — After Hostilities

Forcing the barred door activates a layered military defense. Karrgan does not confuse an armed penetration with an embassy.

## Ways to stop escalation

- withdraw beyond the door and request collection of wounded;
- use the staff to create one turn of living dialogue;
- release captured guards and return their weapons;
- state a limited ceasefire with a time and place;
- bring Brundt, Maruk, returned hobgoblins, or another witness later.

## Karrgan's minimum price

Restitution for deaths or equipment, an explanation of purpose, and a formal second approach. If the party reached the prison-kitchen by force, releasing prisoners is rescue rather than negotiated exchange; Karrgan will not treat subsequent food as proof until the military breach is addressed.

If the party killed young, attacked surrendering residents, or looted after defenders withdrew, full agreement is unlikely during the current visit. A ceasefire and later talks remain possible after destroying the famine engine or returning missing soldiers.
