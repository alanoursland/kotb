# Approach — Commander to Commander

Karrgan does not need flattery. He needs a proposal that survives contact with subordinates, rivals, and hunger.

## His opening

> “Name what you offer, what you require, who answers when your promise fails, and what happens when one of mine breaks it.”

Strong answers define food, ceasefire, patrol jurisdiction, prisoner handling, reporting, and a review date. Leontes can speak this frame naturally. Any character succeeds by being precise and treating Karrgan as an accountable leader.

## What he offers

- disciplined road or convoy patrols for food wages;
- enforcement against raids by his own bloc;
- custody and exchange of future captives under an agreed rule;
- a guarded meeting place;
- military intelligence about the ravine.

## What he tries to add

- authority over goblin food;
- precedence over Yiznak and the other chiefs;
- unrestricted right to discipline “his” patrol zone;
- weapons or armor in place of food.

The party should give him a valuable role without giving him sovereignty. A bounded patrol sector, scheduled reporting, and food wages are safer than arms or vague command.

## Proving the plan

Any one establishes material Proof:

- exchange the six prisoners for the first delivery;
- return the three missing hobgoblins;
- conduct a joint patrol with stated limits;
- show a successful kobold or gnoll agreement without asking Karrgan to ratify it.
