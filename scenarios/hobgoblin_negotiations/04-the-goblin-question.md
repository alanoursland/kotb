# The Goblin Question

The goblin ward is allied to Karrgan but watches him, while his soldiers steal its best food through the secret route between areas 21 and 28.

## Let the party discover the problem

- Goblin families receive worse food than hobgoblins.
- Guards in area 23 watch the goblin stairs as if guarding a border.
- Selected goblin goods sit in [area 28](../../locations/caves/28-hobgoblin-storeroom.md).
- The secret stairs prove systematic theft rather than ordinary tribute.

## Three settlements

### Karrgan remains overlord

He receives goblin food and accepts responsibility for their conduct. Fast and stable, but it strengthens the faction most able to dominate the ravine.

### Direct goblin allotment

Goblin porters receive and mark their own share while remaining military allies. Karrgan receives status through patrol command rather than control of their stomachs. This is the recommended balance.

### Goblin independence

The party exposes the theft and negotiates separately. This requires protection, witnesses, and a plan for Karrgan's response; otherwise it is a declaration without security.

## Karrgan's response

He will concede accounting more readily than symbolism. “Separate sacks under one command” is easier than “the goblins are not yours.” Let players decide how much constitutional work they want inside an emergency peace.
