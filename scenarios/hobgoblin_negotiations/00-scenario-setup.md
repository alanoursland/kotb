# Scenario Setup — Karrgan's Position

## What is true

- Karrgan commands 63 hobgoblins and exercises coercive influence over the neighboring goblin ward.
- Six prisoners wait in [area 24](../../locations/caves/24-hobgoblin-prison-kitchen.md) to become food and spectacle.
- Eating captives is famine-driven but has become command policy, ceremony, and status.
- Four hobgoblins secretly steal the best goods from [goblin storage](../../locations/caves/21-goblin-storage.md).
- Three hobgoblins believed lost in the wilderness are alive in [Vargoth's pen](../../locations/caves/41-skarns-slave-pen.md).
- Karrgan can become the federation's officer corps or its conqueror. He wants the first role and is capable of drifting into the second.

## What earns attention

- requesting safe conduct instead of testing the barred door;
- naming an accountable speaker and a concrete offer;
- using military language: provisioning, patrol limits, chain of command, enforcement;
- bringing actual food or returned hobgoblins;
- accepting that Karrgan must announce policy before his people.

## What provokes resistance

- moral insults without an answer to tomorrow's hunger;
- treating Karrgan as chief of every cave;
- demanding total disarmament;
- freeing prisoners by stealth during talks;
- exposing the goblin theft publicly without protecting the goblins afterward.
