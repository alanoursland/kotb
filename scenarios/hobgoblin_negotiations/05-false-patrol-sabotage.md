# Optional Complication — The False Patrol Report

Use this if Vargoth remains active and Karrgan's agreement is becoming important. Success with Karrgan does not require identifying Vargoth.

## The planted report

A bloodied bundle appears near Cave F's entrance. It contains:

- the patrol token of one of the three hobgoblins held alive in area 41;
- ducal arrowheads, wax, and cloth from the Shrine's framing packet;
- a message in competent Military Goblin claiming the Duke's envoys ambushed the missing patrol;
- a suggestion that Yiznak informed on them;
- bugbear packing cord and a trace of catnip overlooked during assembly.

## What Vargoth wants

- Karrgan detains or attacks the party;
- the party blames Yiznak;
- both negotiations collapse;
- nobody searches Cave H for the living prisoners.

## Karrgan's response

He closes the outer door, suspends terms, and summons the party under guard. He does not attack before hearing an answer; a commander who accepts every planted report is not the leader established elsewhere.
